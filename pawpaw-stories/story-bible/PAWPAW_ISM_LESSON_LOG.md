# PawPaw-ism & Lesson Log

Status: candidate language only. Do not lock final wording until Season One map is complete.

A PawPaw-ism should emerge after the experience and work literally for a small child, usefully for a teenager, and more deeply for the adult the boys become.

## Candidate lesson territory and lines
- Return/security: "People who love you can go away without leaving you."
- Throwing/aim: "Where you aim matters more than how hard you throw."
- Chest charge: "If you're gonna knock somebody down, help 'em back up."
- Rodeo/balance: "You don't learn to balance by holding on forever."
- Flip/trust: "You don't always have to know where you'll land when you know who's catching you."
- Crawling/relationship: "Sometimes I lead. Sometimes you lead. Just don't stop looking for each other."
- Boots/independence: "If the boots are too big, take my hands. You still have to take the steps."
- Dance/joy: "You don't always need music to dance."
- Dance/individuality: "You don't have to move like me to dance with me."
- Meal/fellowship: "It ain't what's on the table that makes it a good meal. It's who's sitting beside you."
- Mess/responsibility: "It's okay to make a mess. Just learn how to clean one up."
- Communication/attention: "Listen long enough and people will tell you things they don't have words for yet."
- Gentle/strength: "Being gentle doesn't mean you're not strong. It means you know when not to use all your strength."
- Gentle/love: "Sometimes loving somebody means slowing down enough to be gentle."
- Brotherhood: "Always remember where your brother is."
- Ritual/legacy territory: the small things repeated across generations can become family memory even when nobody remembers their beginning.

## Current philosophical backbone candidates
Return; aim; strength; trust; balance; independence; joy; responsibility; brotherhood; legacy/ritual.

Do not force all of these into Season One. Preserve extras for later seasons.
