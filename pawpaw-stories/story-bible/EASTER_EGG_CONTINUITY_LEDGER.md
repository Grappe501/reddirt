# Easter Egg & Continuity Ledger

## GiGi invisible-background motif — LOCKED
GiGi is primarily a background character until her featured episode.

Episode 1 may visibly establish her lunch-break routine. After that, her arrival should normally be audio-only:
"Hey y'all, I'm here for my lunch break."
"I've brought lunch."

She then becomes silent/background until an audio-only departure such as:
"Okay, I gotta get back to work."

### Visual rule
Before the reveal, use at most ONE GiGi visual appearance per episode. It should be a blink-and-you-miss-it background motif:
- deep background / as far from primary action as composition allows;
- roughly a couple of seconds, long enough to be discoverable on pause/rewatch;
- placed during the busiest or most attention-demanding foreground beat;
- camera, focus, music, dialogue and editing NEVER acknowledge her;
- she is plainly present, not artificially hidden.

Her background behavior rotates naturally: holding Waylon, feeding him face-forward across her upright lap in the recliner, burping him, cooing/talking to him, picking something up, or briefly holding one of the Itsy Bitsy Spider hand positions.

The audience should finish several episodes believing GiGi was not visually present at all.

### Spider slow burn
Across roughly 6–7 episodes, occasionally include one Spider hand position in the single GiGi background glimpse. Do NOT show the full ritual or explain it.

In GiGi's featured episode, break the established camera grammar: stay with her. She performs Itsy Bitsy Spider with Weston, revealing what those background hand shapes meant.

Later lesson/Memory material reveals the lineage: Kelly performed it with Grace, then Weston, then Waylon.

If earlier shots are replayed during the reveal, use the ORIGINAL frames—no retcon. The pleasure is discovering she was there all along.
