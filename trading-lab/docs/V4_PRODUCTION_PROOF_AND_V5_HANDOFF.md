## Constitutional invariants
- All agents are equal; roles are functions, not ranks.
- Knowledge follows the agent; no agent is deleted merely for poor performance.
- Repurpose before reproduce; evolution is bounded and operator-reviewed.
- Dissent, provenance, uncertainty and point-in-time integrity are preserved.
- Safety/legal/human benefit precede truth/evidence integrity, then research quality, learning, efficiency and cost.
- Paper/simulated execution only; no automatic real-money execution.
- Complexity is a Wealth Builder capability, not a burden placed on the user.

## V5 experience constitution
The interface must be sophisticated without being overwhelming. Every important surface follows **GLANCE → EXPLAIN → LEARN → ADVANCED → RESEARCH → TRY**. First frames answer what is happening, what matters, what needs attention, what Wealth Builder is investigating and where to go deeper. Do not greet users with chart walls or agent grids.

### Wealth Builder Intelligence Tape
A persistent footer intelligence tape is a named V5 product component for authenticated product surfaces. It may show current market benchmarks, market regime, Wealth Builder research posture, buy-focus/sell-or-avoid-focus research conclusions, strongest opportunities, material risk changes, new disagreements, research events and active investigations.

Every research conclusion shown on the tape must be timestamped, traceable to evidence, freshness-aware and expandable into an explanation. Scores are evidence/research scores, **not probabilities of profit** unless separately calibrated and proven. Recommended postures must expose bull, bear, risk, disagreement and supporting evidence on demand. The tape is a navigation surface into deeper research, not an autonomous trading instruction. The human decides whether and how to act.

Suggested interaction path: Tape item → explanation drawer → evidence dimensions → deeper research → simulation/scenario comparison. Material view changes should be surfaced explicitly (for example WATCH → FAVORABLE) rather than requiring users to discover them in dashboards.
