# Trading Lab Progressive Learning Constitution

## Mission

Every completed simulation should leave the research system more informed than it was before. The system accumulates evidence about what worked, what failed, under which market regime, after realistic modeled costs, and with what drawdown. It does not redefine a lucky outcome as knowledge.

The educational objective is to improve disciplined, lawful and ethical wealth-building research over time while preserving human control and uncertainty.

## Non-negotiable learning loop

OBSERVE → SIMULATE → MEASURE → COMPARE → RECORD → LEARN → SHADOW-TEST → REPEAT.

Every learning cycle stores its version, sample size, cost assumptions, deterministic gates, strategy evaluations, baseline comparison, walk-forward evidence, regime results and lessons. A future system must be able to explain why a strategy version improved or deteriorated rather than silently changing itself.

## What “smarter” means

Smarter means better calibrated to evidence: higher quality out-of-sample results, positive expectancy after realistic costs, controlled drawdown, robustness across multiple chronological windows, understandable regime dependence, and fewer unsupported conclusions. It does not mean maximizing trading frequency or taking more risk.

## Wealth-building principles

1. Protect capital before pursuing return.
2. Measure results after costs.
3. Prefer repeatable evidence over isolated wins.
4. Compare complexity against a simple baseline.
5. Preserve losing simulations; failures are training evidence.
6. Separate research, shadow simulation, paper execution and any future real-money capability.
7. Never imply guaranteed returns.
8. Never use material nonpublic information, manipulation, deceptive conduct or prohibited market behavior as an input or tactic.
9. Keep an auditable record of strategy versions and promotion decisions.
10. Human approval remains mandatory for promotion beyond research/shadow status.

## Promotion states

RESEARCH means evidence is incomplete or one or more gates failed.

SHADOW_CANDIDATE means a deterministic version passed the current research gates and may be tested prospectively in a fictional shadow account. It is not an endorsement and is not permission for live-money execution.

No automatic LIVE_APPROVED state exists.

## Current research gates

A strategy version needs at least 12 closed simulated trades, at least three walk-forward windows, at least two positive held-out windows, maximum drawdown no greater than 12%, profit factor of at least 1.05, and positive expectancy after modeled costs. These are initial research gates, not claims that the strategy is profitable or safe. They should be revised only through an explicit versioned change.

## Anti-overfitting rule

The system may learn from every simulation, but it must not tune itself to every simulation. Lessons accumulate. Strategy rules change only in versioned experiments and are retested on later held-out observations. Failed and contradictory evidence remains in the ledger.
