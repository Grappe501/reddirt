# V2-05 — Beginner-to-PhD Content Engine

Status: IMPLEMENTED v1
Date: 2026-09-18

The content engine resolves one Universal Knowledge Object into progressively deeper educational treatments without maintaining disconnected articles.

Depths: GLANCE, EXPLAIN, LEARN, ADVANCED, RESEARCH. TRY remains an interactive layer owned by V2-06.

The runtime detects available reviewed/authored depth, resolves requested depth, and falls back downward when content is absent. It never fabricates a missing advanced or research treatment.

Five representative concepts now carry structured Learn, Advanced and Research extensions: VWAP, P/E Ratio, ATR, Short Selling and Sharpe Ratio. These extensions remain draft educational content until the source-review pipeline promotes them.

Research depth must surface assumptions, limitations, empirical questions and methodological debates. It is not simply longer beginner prose.

This phase establishes content mechanics, not final academic corpus completion. Source review and expansion continue as the knowledge library grows.
