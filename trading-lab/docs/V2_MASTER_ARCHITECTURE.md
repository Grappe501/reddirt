## Product doctrine

Wealth Builder V2 is simultaneously a professional paper-trading intelligence terminal, a financial university, a research laboratory, and a personalized trading coach.

The defining educational rule is progressive disclosure: nothing requires prior financial knowledge, while every important concept can drill down to advanced research depth.

Universal learning path:
WHAT IS THIS? -> WHY DOES IT MATTER? -> SHOW ME -> LET ME TRY -> SHOW THE MATH -> SHOW THE EVIDENCE -> SHOW THE SOURCE.

No unexplained jargon. No unexplained numbers. No black-box AI conclusions.

## Existing V1 foundation to preserve

V2 must reuse and extend the existing Trading Lab foundation, including live market-data runtime, market breadth, Market Memory, historical analogues, strategy library, cost-aware/leakage-resistant backtesting, walk-forward evidence, progressive learning ledger, strategy lineage, calibration/feature evidence, persistent research storage, production release gates, mobile/accessibility work, and data-quality/degraded-state handling.

The existing V2 Premium specification at docs/V2_PREMIUM_INTELLIGENCE_TERMINAL.md is incorporated by reference and remains canonical unless superseded by an explicitly versioned V2 decision.

## Four integrated product surfaces

1. WEALTH BUILDER TERMINAL — market intelligence, paper trading, portfolio/risk and professional workflow.
2. WEALTH BUILDER UNIVERSITY — contextual education from zero knowledge through research depth.
3. WEALTH BUILDER LAB — hypotheses, experiments, backtests, replay and strategy science.
4. WEALTH BUILDER COACH — personalized tutoring, journaling, behavioral/performance intelligence and skill progression.

All four share Market Data -> Market Memory -> Knowledge Graph -> Research Engine -> Learning Ledger -> AI Intelligence.

## Global acceptance rules

Every phase must preserve V1 behavior unless a migration is explicitly approved. Educational explanations must distinguish definitions from interpretations and sourced facts from generated teaching language. Market intelligence must expose uncertainty, stale/degraded data and disagreement. Scores are not probabilities unless separately calibrated and labeled. V2 remains educational/paper-trading first; no automatic real-money execution. Every important displayed term/metric should be eligible for the universal explain system. Every symbol should eventually resolve to the Security Master and Symbol Intelligence architecture.

## Build cadence

A phase may contain multiple implementation slices, but the operator advances phases only after its acceptance gate passes. Each slice should be independently testable and committed. V2-02 through V2-06 establish the education substrate before later feature surfaces are allowed to create large amounts of new jargon-heavy UI.
