# V2-12 — AI Trading Copilot + Professor

Status: IMPLEMENTED v1 orchestration foundation
Date: 2026-09-18

V2-12 creates two modes over the same evidence architecture.

COPILOT explains what is happening in the current security/market context. It can receive Security Master identity, Premium decomposition, Market Brain, Market Memory, strategy evidence, fundamentals and screen context.

PROFESSOR teaches what a concept means. It receives the Knowledge Graph neighborhood, requested depth, exercises, screen context and optional live context.

The modes are intentionally distinct. Canonical education must not mutate because today's market changed; current market claims must not be smuggled into timeless concept definitions.

The response contract requires mode, answer, evidence state, source references, uncertainties and next actions. Missing evidence is named. Generated language cannot upgrade weak evidence.

A response guardrail rejects guarantee/risk-free language and blocks unsupported percentage-of-profit/probability language when Premium is in context.

This phase builds the orchestration and trust boundary, not a hidden autonomous trading agent. A future model/API route may consume the prompt packets, but it must preserve these contracts and source-state rules.

No real-money automatic execution is introduced.
