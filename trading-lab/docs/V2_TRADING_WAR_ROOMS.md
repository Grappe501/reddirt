# V2-17 — Morning / Live / Closing War Rooms

Status: IMPLEMENTED v1 orchestration foundation
Date: 2026-09-18

The War Room layer turns V2 engines into a session workflow.

Morning asks: What is the environment before the session? Its contract assembles market state, overnight context, catalysts, opportunities, watchlist, risk posture and Professor.

Live asks: What is changing now? It assembles Market Brain, opportunities, Premium velocity, watchlist, positions, risk, alerts and Copilot.

Closing asks: What happened, what did I do, and what did we learn? It assembles session summary, automatic journal, Digital Twin, Shadow Trader, strategy evidence, lessons and Professor.

Every room is timestamped. Each panel carries a source-state marker. Missing/stale inputs degrade room health instead of being silently treated as current.

War Rooms are orchestration surfaces, not new competing intelligence engines. They consume the canonical engines already built.

The runtime preserves the V2 trust rules: Premium is not probability, Opportunity is investigation priority rather than a recommendation, and no automatic real-money execution is introduced.

Future visual implementation should render these contracts responsively for desktop, tablet and mobile and allow every consequential term/metric to invoke Universal Explain.
