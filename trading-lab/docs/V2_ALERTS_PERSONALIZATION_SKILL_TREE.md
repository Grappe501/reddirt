# V2-18 — Alerts, Personalization + Skill Tree

Status: IMPLEMENTED v1 foundation
Date: 2026-09-18

V2-18 connects market attention with learning progression without turning personalization into behavioral speculation.

Initial in-app alerts support price conditions, Premium changes, surfaced opportunities, risk breaches, learning opportunities and degraded data. Every alert explains why it surfaced and is explicitly a condition notification rather than a trade recommendation.

Personalization may use observed setup history to reorder investigation opportunities, but it does not claim the user “likes,” “fears,” or “prefers” something unless such a preference is explicitly supplied. The reason for personalization remains visible.

The skill system spans Markets, Technical Analysis, Fundamentals & Accounting, Risk Management, Strategy Science and Quantitative Finance. Competencies progress through not_started, introduced, practicing and demonstrated.

The first deterministic demonstration rule requires at least three exercises with >=80% accuracy and at least two simulation applications. This is a product rule, not an academic accreditation claim.

Just-in-time learning can surface when a concept appears in the current context and the associated skill is not yet demonstrated. It offers explanation/practice without blocking trading simulation.

Initial delivery is in-app only. Future channels require explicit user preference and notification controls.
