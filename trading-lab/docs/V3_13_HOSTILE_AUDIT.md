# V3-13 Hostile Audit

Status: EXECUTABLE AUDIT HARNESS ADDED

V3 is attacked as an evidence system, not merely feature-tested. The audit targets the highest-consequence failure modes introduced by durable research, source adapters, replay/shadow experiments, Agent Alpha, tournaments, cost intelligence, AI execution, University integration and the Research Command Center.

## Attack matrix

1. AI fabricated provenance must be rejected.
2. AI provider failure must degrade to UNAVAILABLE.
3. Tournament observations from mismatched frozen conditions must not enter comparison.
4. Tiny samples must remain LOW_SAMPLE and cannot auto-promote.
5. Negative research costs must be rejected.
6. Cost efficiency must never create strategy authority.
7. Evidence score education must explicitly distinguish score from probability of profit.
8. Research field naming differences such as camelCase must not silently sever University explanations.
9. Command Center must preserve the real-money execution prohibition even under maximally favorable research display.
10. Strategy Tournament remains paper-only and cannot execute trades.
