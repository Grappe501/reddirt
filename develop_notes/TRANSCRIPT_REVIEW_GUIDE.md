# Transcript review guide

- Never publish raw automatic captions without review
- Preserve Kelly’s meaning and voice
- Correct spelling, punctuation, proper names, and obvious ASR errors only
- Do not rewrite speeches into campaign copy
- Do not remove meaningful qualifying language
- Use `[inaudible]` / `[unclear]` when needed
- Document substantive corrections in revision reason
- Require APPROVED → explicit PUBLISHED
- AI drafts (`AI_GENERATED`) always start at REVIEW_REQUIRED
