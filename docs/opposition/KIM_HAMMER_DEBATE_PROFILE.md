# KIM HAMMER DEBATE PROFILE (KH-2)

## Purpose
- Organize likely Hammer arguments, probable evidence anchors, and Kelly response frames in a source-backed format.

## High-probability argument lanes
- Election integrity/security necessity.
- Experience and institutional readiness.
- Uniformity/accountability framing for statewide procedures.

## Kelly response architecture
- Agree on valid principle first (integrity, accountability, security).
- Contrast method, implementation effects, and county burden evidence.
- Bridge to trust, transparency, participation, and county support.

## Structured response template
- Direct answer.
- Bill/source anchor.
- Values contrast.
- Voter/county impact.
- Solution path.
- Bridge line.

## Risk controls
- No motive claims as fact.
- No unsourced legal assertions.
- No personal or religious attacks.
- Mark uncertain items as `RESEARCH_QUESTION` or `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
