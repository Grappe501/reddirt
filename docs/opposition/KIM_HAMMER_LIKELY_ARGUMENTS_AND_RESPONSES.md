# KIM HAMMER LIKELY ARGUMENTS AND RESPONSES (KH-2)

## Likely arguments
- "Election integrity must come first."
- "Experience means readiness."
- "Uniform statewide controls create accountability."
- "Petition restrictions prevent fraud."

## Response method
- Agree with valid principle.
- Distinguish implementation method.
- Tie back to trust/transparency/participation/county support.
- Use source-grounded bill anchors.

## Rebuttal safety
- Do not use personal attacks.
- Do not use religious attacks.
- Do not assert motive without direct source evidence.

## Drill prompts
- How do we secure elections without unnecessary barriers?
- How do we support counties while preserving strong standards?
- How do we keep the SOS office de-politicized and service-driven?
