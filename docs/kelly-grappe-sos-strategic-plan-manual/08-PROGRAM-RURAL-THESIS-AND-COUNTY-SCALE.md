**Targets:** **Tier 3** long-tail minimums; **travel % of budget** — **[`LANE §3.3`, §4.2](./LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md)**.

---

## Strategic narrative

**Urban centers should not be the only voices** shaping Arkansas. Rural counties hold **swing pockets**, **registration upside**, and **credibility** when treated with respect.

---

## Operational implications (budget-aware)

1. **Travel** is a **strategic line** — **28–38%** in bootstrap per **LANE**; **document** counties-per-trip **maps** to reduce deadhead.  
2. **County party loop** — **75** counties **≥ 1** meeting; **Tier 1** **≥ 3**.  
3. **Extension Homemakers / conservative rooms** — relational **slow burn**; **GOTV** asks only after relationship (**T-21** or **informational** earlier if lawful).  
4. **Unusual rooms** — track in **tasks**; aim **2+** unusual-room events **per Tier 1** county pre-primary.
