# Compliance, governance, and risk

**Budget / vendor risk:** Large **media** commitments require **counsel** review against **cash** and **LANE §4.3** lockbox — **no** oral-only deals **T-21 → E**.

---

## Governance stack

1. **Campaign counsel** — finance, electioneering, disclaimers, coordination, **GOTV** volunteer **distance** law.  
2. **Compliance lead** — filing calendar; **ad** snapshots; **text** compliance.  
3. **Digital / comms** — STOP/HELP; **hours** accuracy **T-7 → E** is a **legal**-adjacent **risk**.

---

## Data and privacy (voter and supporter)

- **Minimize** exports; **access control** on admin.  
- **Retention + opt-out** propagation — **freeze** experimental segmentation **T-7** (avoid breaking suppression).

---

## Paid media and fundraising

- **Disclaimers** — committee name; **treasurer** address where required.  
- **Match programs** — **finance** documents **triggers**; **no** misleading **multipliers**.

## Content risk (GOTV-intensified)

- **Defamation** — **zero**.  
- **Intimidation** — volunteers **expelled**; legal notified.
