# Strategic framework and theory of change

**Targets:** County tiers, margin allocator, registration ambition, and **GOTV master clock** — **[`LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md`](./LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md)**.

---

## North star narrative

**The Secretary of State’s office belongs to the people** — not parties, not insiders. Kelly’s campaign offers **steady, transparent administration**, **access to the ballot**, and **confidence that every county counts**. The opponent to apathy and cynicism is **competence plus presence**: showing up, listening, and running systems that work.

---

## Theory of change (logic model)

```text
Inputs
  ├── Trusted partners (non-partisan registration orgs, faith and civic leaders)
  ├── First-party relationships (decades of organizing contacts)
  ├── Voter file + lawful modeling
  ├── Collateral and merch (tangible visibility)
  ├── Paid + earned media (local papers, radio, APA digital)
  └── Campaign OS (RedDirt) + county portal (county workbench)

Activities
  ├── Register (partner-led training; campaign tracks)
  ├── Educate (SOS duties, transparency, election access — honest framing)
  ├── Relate (small gatherings, Power of Five, invites into unusual rooms)
  ├── Activate (event presence, councils, fairs, tours)
  └── Convert attention into vote plan + turnout

Outputs
  ├── New registrations (by county / week) — pace bands **LANE §2.3**
  ├── Cohort contacts (gap voters, youth, persuasion universes)
  ├── Hosts and gatherings (# events, # counties) — **Tier 1** minimums **LANE §3.1**
  ├── Media placements (LTE, columns, radio, digital impressions)
  └── Volunteer depth — **molecule targets** **LANE §5**

Outcomes
  ├── Narrow midterm/presidential turnout gap in target counties
  ├── Improved candidate ID / support among persuasion targets (where measured)
  ├── Winning coalition — **margin allocator** **LANE §1.2**
  └── Election-night competitiveness and credible mandate to govern transparently

Impact
  └── A Secretary of State administration known for access, clarity, and service statewide
```

## GOTV integration (strategic)

All pathways must **converge** into **[`LANE §6`](./LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md)**:

- **Narrative:** Final **three weeks** emphasize **SOS office stake** + **where/when to vote** (creative approved by compliance).  
- **Geography:** **Tier 1** counties carry **62%** of planning margin burden — relational and media **must not** thin out there first.  
- **Youth / faith / rural** programs each owe a **named deliverable** into the **§6.2 program matrix** (rides, pulpit info-only moments, fair-adjacent visibility).

## Guiding principles

1. **People over politics** — civic service framing; avoid petty personalization; cite **public record** only.  
2. **All 75 counties** — resource concentration follows **LANE §3** tiers; narrative welcomes everyone.  
3. **Rural authenticity** — farmers, small towns, Methodist roots, and respect for local institutions are **strategic assets**.  
4. **Permission and consent** — especially SMS/email; **opt-out honored instantly**; surge scripts **counsel-approved**.  
5. **Non-partisan partnership boundaries** — registration and integrity conversations stay **civic** where required.
