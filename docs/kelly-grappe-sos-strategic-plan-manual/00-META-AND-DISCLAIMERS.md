## What this manual is not

- **Not legal advice.** Campaign finance, fundraising compensation, voter contact (TCPA, CAN-SPAM, state law), coordination with allies, and disclaimer requirements require **qualified counsel**.
- **Not a data dictionary for voter PII.** Operational detail on file fields, exports, and storage belongs in governed **internal** SOPs with access control.
- **Not a substitute for official election administration.** Voter registration status and ballot rules are authoritative from **Arkansas election officials** and official lookup tools; the campaign site and OS **point to** those sources where applicable.
- **Not an opponent research publication.** Opposition intelligence in RedDirt is engineered for **public-record, human-reviewed** workflows per project docs; this strategic manual does not restate opponent claims.

## Numbers lane and redaction

**[`LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md`](./LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md)** holds **vote margin hypotheses, registration goals, county tiers, budget envelopes, GOTV lockbox sizes, and integrated GOTV program matrix**. When exporting manuals to vendors, volunteers, or coalition partners:

- **Remove** or **redact** dollar envelopes and GOTV lockbox figures unless finance approves.
- **Summarize** registration goals as **statewide ambition** without per-county spreadsheets when working with non-partisan partners.
- **Never** attach raw voter targets or persuasion universe sizes to public-facing PDFs.

## Public-facing language (alignment with county workbench)

When publishing or training volunteers, avoid engineering jargon on **public** surfaces: e.g. database, API, ingest, schema, RAG, workbench (use **campaign resources**, **local plan**, **operator tools** internally). See `countyWorkbench/docs/COUNTY_WORKBENCH_MASTER_PLAN.md` §6.

## Approved public vocabulary (from Campaign System Manual)

Where helpful for consistency with training materials:

- **Campaign Companion** — human-centered assistance (not “the AI product” in public).
- **Guided Campaign System** — structured onboarding and next steps.
- **Field Intelligence** — aggregate, permissioned insight — **not** public voter dossiers.

## Versioning rules

- **Major** (2.0): Strategy reset or election phase change (primary → general pivot).
- **Minor** (1.x): New program pillar, **numbers lane** restructuring, major county tier change, or budget phase shift.
- **Patch** (1.1.x): Corrections, KPI tweaks, clarifications, **single-table** LANE updates.
