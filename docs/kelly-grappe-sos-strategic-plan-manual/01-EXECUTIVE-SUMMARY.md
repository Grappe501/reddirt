## Strategic bet

**Scale without a traditional large headquarters staff** by combining:

1. **Distributed relational organizing** — small gatherings, regional social teams, Power of Five–style peer recruitment.
2. **Data-informed prioritization** — voter file and modeled scores to find **registration velocity**, **presidential-to-midterm drop-off**, and **persuasion universes** (lawfully and ethically defined).
3. **High-trust messengers** — churches and religious communities when invited, Extension-style and conservative-leaning rooms approached with respect, multi-faith and multi-lingual partnership, and **always-on** visibility (travel, events, social proof).

## Technology enabler (honest scope)

**RedDirt** is the **campaign operating system**: public site surfaces, forms, admin Workbench, Prisma/Postgres data, communications rails, email command center (governed), voter file warehouse, read-only voter modeling layers, election results ingest for **planning context**, tasks, events, and growing relational-organizing foundations.

**County workbench** is a **separate** Netlify-bound county portal: **Pope-first** full profile; **75-county** shells; operator tools for readiness, intelligence taxonomy, path-to-victory planning, and **mailto/email draft** centers (**no send** from that product by design today).

**Targets ↔ systems:** Registration goals should be written to **`CountyCampaignStats.registrationGoal`** in RedDirt and kept honest on **county workbench** public pages — see **LANE §2.2** and Part 2 audit.

**The manual’s** Part 2 documents strengths, gaps, and what programmatic promises the tools **can** support this cycle.
