# Program: election integrity listening tour

**Budget:** Roll into **events / travel** lines — **[`LANE §4.2`](./LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md)**; target **≥ 8–12** tour stops / general cycle in **Tier 1 + Tier 2** mix (planning).  
**GOTV:** Tour **ends** **T-21** for **new** audiences — pivots to **vote logistics** only; existing relationships **feed** captains — **[`LANE §6.2`](./LANE-BUDGET-VICTORY-MATH-AND-TARGETS.md)**.

---

## Purpose

**Non-partisan, non-threatening** conversations about **transparency**, **access**, and **confidence** — appropriate to a Secretary of State candidate.

**Not:** unsourced fraud claims, opponent attacks, fear.  
**Yes:** libraries, civic halls, restaurants — **community-initiated** when possible.

---

## Format options

| Format | Audience | Run time | Suggested counties |
|--------|----------|----------|---------------------|
| **Community salon** | 15–40 | 75–90 min | **Tier 2–3** community anchors |
| **Panel + Q&A** | 40–120 | 90 min | **Tier 1** quarterly |
| **coffee / breakfast** | local leaders | 60 min | **Monthly** per region |

**Target (planning):** **≥ 1** tour or salon per **campaign region** per **quarter** through **T-28**.

---

## Moderation standards

1. **Opening norms** — civility, citations, no harassment.  
2. **Fact anchors** — **official** sources only.  
3. **Close** — **three** CTAs: register (**partner**), **host** gathering, **poll worker** interest **informational**.

---

## Logistics checklist

- Venue agreement; accessibility; **ASL**; **press** policy.  
- Sign-in + **opt-in**; **separate** volunteer pipeline.  
- **Budget:** line item **$300–$800** per stop (planning) — venue, coffee, printing — reconcile with finance.
