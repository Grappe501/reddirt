## The eight-part story

### 1. Three-candidate race

Kelly Grappe (D) · Kim Hammer (R) · Michael Pakko (L)

Arkansas elects statewide constitutional offices by **plurality**. There is no runoff. The largest coalition wins.

### 2. Victory does not require 50%+1

| Model | Target |
| ----- | -----: |
| Traditional 50%+1 working target | 498,963 |
| Plurality win range | 390,000–420,000 |
| Campaign working goal | 400,000+ |
| Stretch goal | 430,000+ |

### 3. Democrats have substantial midterm drop-off

Statewide raw drop-off (2024 Presidential D − 2022 SOS D): **102,070 votes**.

Recovering half = **51,051 votes** — before persuasion, before Republican conversion.

See [Chapter 4 — Democratic Drop-Off](./chapter-04-democratic-drop-off/README.md).

### 4. Republicans emerged from a fractured primary

Norris vs Hammer split direct-democracy Republicans from paper-ballot Republicans. Primary turnout collapsed ~70% from first round to runoff. Conversion opportunity exists among disenchanted Republicans.

See [Chapter 6 — Republican Conversion Model](./chapter-06-republican-conversion-model.md).

### 5. Libertarians create an alternative lane

Pakko baseline ~4.3% (2024 Treasurer). Some voters will choose Libertarian over Republican — plurality math favors a unified Democratic coalition that does not need to win every Republican.

### 6. Registration growth creates additional opportunity

Statewide target: **50,000** new registrations (Lane 3).

See [Chapter 5 — The 50,000 New Voter Plan](./chapter-05-fifty-thousand-new-voter-plan/README.md).

### 7. Relationships matter more than television

Power of 5 organizing model: every supporter identifies five people. Eyeball-to-eyeball campaign. Republican conversion target: peel 12% through relationships, not ads.

### 8. Every county has a path to contribute

No county is written off. Even limited Hope Index counties contribute through retention, registration, or relationship conversion.

See [75 County Playbook](../part-iii-arkansas-battlefield/chapter-09-seventy-five-county-playbook/README.md).
