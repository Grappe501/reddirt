> **Executive Book 2.0** · Campaign Operating System manual · Internal leadership briefing  
> **Replaces:** Executive Book 1.x "Power of 5" chapter — same doctrine, now the **central engine** of the Campaign OS

**Live system:** [People Power tab](/election-plan?tab=peoplePower) in the Election Plan portal.

---

## Why PPEN exists

Executive Book 1.x treated organizing as one chapter among twelve. In **Campaign OS 2.0**, PPEN is the **connective tissue** between every other system:

```text
People → Relationships → Communities → Counties → Campaign
```

Without PPEN, County Workbench shows empty pipelines. Coalition Command has no trusted messengers. Fundraising has no hosts. Communications has no insider feed subscribers. GOTV has no vote plans. **PPEN is how the movement scales.**

The campaign is not an advertising funnel (`Ad → Vote`). It is a **relationship funnel**:

```text
Conversation → Relationship → Trust → Volunteer → Host → Recruit Five → Vote
```

PPEN is the operating system that records, routes, and multiplies that funnel statewide.

---

## Four layers — Person, Participation, Relationship, Impact

PPEN is built on four first-class objects. Every feature in the Election Plan portal eventually hangs from these:

| Layer | Question | What it owns |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Person** | Who are they? | Identity, consent, username, access level, primary county |
| **Participation** | How are they involved *here*? | Role in a county, city, coalition, event committee, comms program |
| **Relationship** | Who do they know and influence? | My Five, Help 10 Participate, local trusted networks |
| **Impact** | What changed because of them? | Votes, volunteers recruited, leaders developed, dollars raised |

**Critical doctrine:** A Person is one human. A **Participation Record** is involvement in one context. Steve Grappe might be Faulkner County Volunteer Lead *and* Sherwood Event Committee Member *and* Veterans Coalition Contributor — three participations, one person. This is required for permissions, reporting, comms scoping, and leadership placement.

## Person Layer

The Person Layer answers: *Who is in the movement, and what can they access?*

| Field (target) | Purpose |
|----------------|---------|
| Name, address, phone, email | Contact and assignment |
| Username | Unique activation handle |
| Status | prospect · active · suspended |
| County | Primary geographic home |
| City | Optional community workbench link |
| Coalitions | Many-to-many coalition interests |
| Skills, interests | Placement and training routing |

**Access levels L1–L6** scope what workbench sections, CCH insider feed, and admin surfaces a person sees:

| Level | Label | Typical surfaces |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| L1 | Participant | My Journey, own My Five / Help 10 |
| L2 | Leader | Community, event, volunteer, coalition workbenches |
| L3 | County | County Leadership Workbench |
| L4 | Regional | Multi-county rollups |
| L5 | State | CCH hub, SMOS hub, statewide programs |
| L6 | Executive | War room, escalations, cross-workbench reports |

**Build gate:** Person + Participation schema (PPEN A.0b) ships **before** fake dashboard counts. Zeros are honest until records exist.

## Participation Layer — activation and placement

The Participation Layer answers: *Where does this person show up in the OS?*

Context types: `county` · `community` · `coalition` · `event_committee` · `comms` · `program`

Example:

```text
Steve Grappe (Person)
├── Faulkner County Volunteer Lead          (participation: county)
├── Sherwood Event Committee Member         (participation: event_committee)
├── Direct Democracy Coalition Participant  (participation: program)
├── Veterans Outreach Contributor           (participation: coalition)
└── Substack Insider Member                 (participation: comms)
```

### Activation pipeline (A.0c — the real front door)

The OS begins at the **website volunteer form** — not County Workbench, not admin spreadsheets:

```text
Volunteer Signup → Confirmation Email → One-Time Activation Link
→ Username + Password + Terms → Person Record → Journey Record
→ Participation Record(s) → County Assignment → L1 Grant → My Journey home
```

One Person. One or more Participation records. No duplicate contact rows.

**Integration hooks:** CCH (public feed at L1, insider feed after training), county volunteer pipeline counts, community workbench people lists, coalition interested-candidate counts.

## Relationship Layer — My Five and Help 10 Participate

The Relationship Layer answers: *Who do they know, and who will they help participate?*

### My Five (mandatory journey track)

Every supporter recruits **five** trusted contacts. Those five recruit five. Power of 5 is **Tier 4** of the surrogate layer:

```text
Kelly → Regional Surrogates → County Captains → Hosts → Power of 5 Network
```

| Level | Objective |
|-------|-----------|
| Individual | Complete your Five · log conversations |
| Team | ~5 people · mutual accountability |
| County | Captain ladder · strike team roles |
| State | **60,000 deep relational network** |

Power of 5 is **doctrine + journey track**, not a generic org-chart slot. It lives in My Journey, not as a mislabeled community leadership role.

### Help 10 Participate (mandatory civic mission)

Distinct from My Five. **Help 10 People Participate** — not "Register 10 Voters."

| Activity | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Registration verification | Confirm eligibility without partisan pressure |
| Vote plans | Trusted messenger follow-through |
| Polling information | Nonpartisan election-day support |
| Election reminders | Turnout activation from known relationships |

Help 10 is the bridge between PPEN and **Chapter 10 — Voter Engagement**. Relationship depth built here deploys on Election Day.

### The relationship ladder

Every contact moves through:

```text
Meet → Listen → Follow Up → Invite → Engage → Volunteer → Lead → Recruit Five
```

Human Contact Index (planning anchor: 250,000 conversations) measures ladder **volume**. Local ownership measures ladder **quality**.

## Impact Layer — "Because of You"

The Impact Layer answers: *What changed because this person showed up?*

Every participant profile eventually shows **record-backed** impact — never dashboard placeholders:

```text
Because of You

People Joined              (direct recruits)
Network Impact             (gen-2 / gen-3 reach)
People Helped Participate  (Help 10 completions)
Vote Plans Completed       (verified vote-plan records)
```

These are **movement retention metrics**. They derive from Relationship records and verified edges — 0 until A.1/A.1b engines populate data.

## My Journey — participant home

After activation, every participant lands on **My Journey** — three parallel tracks:

```text
Every participant
├── Leadership Journey     (optional specialization)
├── My Five                (mandatory — network growth)
└── Help 10 Participate    (mandatory civic mission)
```

Plus: Training · Certifications · Volunteer Hours · Events · Leadership Opportunities · County · Current Role · Next Step

My Journey is the **default home screen** of the Campaign OS for L1 participants. Leaders graduate to role-specific Leadership Workbenches (Chapter 6).

## Eyeball-to-eyeball doctrine (organizing philosophy)

PPEN implements the **Arkansas Living Room Strategy** — a relationship campaign, not an advertising campaign:

```text
Relationships create trust.
Trust creates participation.
Participation creates turnout.
Turnout creates victory.
```

### Event pyramid

| Tier | Examples | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Large events** | Sherwood launch · forums · festivals | **Visibility** |
| **Medium events** | County meetings · union halls · NAACP · Rotary | **Credibility** |
| **Small events** | Breakfasts · house parties · living rooms | **Relationship building** |

Schedule all three every week. Visibility without relationship depth is wasted motion. Relationship depth without visibility is invisible momentum.

### Every stop creates more stops

Before leaving any event, three asks:

1. **Who else should we meet?**
2. **Who would host the next gathering?**
3. **Who are your five?**

### The Big Table Democrat

Kelly's defining message connects directly to PPEN. The campaign welcomes rural Democrats, conservative Democrats, union Democrats, faith Democrats, young Democrats, independents, and moderate Republicans willing to listen.

**We do not ask people to give up who they are. We ask Arkansans to come back to the table.**

Organizing implication: every conversation starts with **listening**, not persuasion scripts. Local messengers lead in their own networks.

## Perfect Week Doctrine (PPEN execution rhythm)

Every week the campaign:

1. Attends events  
2. Recruits volunteers (Power of 5)  
3. Builds endorsements  
4. Tells stories  
5. Captures contacts (Person + Participation)  
6. Schedules future visits  
7. Generates local media  
8. Builds coalition relationships  

Three jobs: **visible momentum** · **validators** · **volunteer machine**.
