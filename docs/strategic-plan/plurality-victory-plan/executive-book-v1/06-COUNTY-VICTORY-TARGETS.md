# County Victory Targets

## Why this chapter exists

Campaign leadership thinks in VCI, lane recovery, and opportunity scores. County chairs think in votes.

Every county leader should be able to answer four questions in under a minute:

1. How many more votes do we need?
2. What percentage increase are we shooting for?
3. What is our weekly pace between now and Election Day?
4. How many Power of 5 leaders does that require?

This chapter translates the statewide win model into those numbers for all 75 counties.

## How to read the table

- **Baseline** — weighted Democratic SOS-equivalent vote from recent elections (2022 SOS, 2022/2024 Treasurer, 2024 Presidential).
- **Target** — county share of the statewide working target with cushion.
- **Growth** — net additional Democratic votes required above baseline.
- **% Increase** — growth divided by baseline — the number local leaders immediately understand.
- **Weekly** — growth divided by weeks remaining in the field plan.
- **Po5** — Power of 5 leaders needed (each leader × 5 voters × 10 conversations ≈ 50 net votes).

## Traffic lights

| Color | Range | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Green | 0–10% | Likely achievable with steady organizing |
| Yellow | 10–20% | Requires intentional county program |
| Red | 20%+ | Exceptional effort — surges, coalitions, surrogates |
| ★ Strategic | Any % | Priority county regardless of difficulty |

## What county chairs do with this

Open your county playbook. The victory target panel at the top is your organizing number.

Share it with donors, local elected officials, and volunteer leaders. It is easier to organize around **"we need a 17% increase — 1,120 votes — 22 Power of 5 leaders"** than around statewide projections.

Every county playbook inherits these numbers automatically.
