# Campaign Doctrine — The Arkansas Way to Win

> **Chapter 0 — Read this first** · Philosophy before mechanics · Every volunteer, county chair, surrogate, donor host, campus captain, LTE writer, and Power of 5 leader

Most campaigns believe a perfectly written plan wins elections.

**We do not.**

A perfectly executed plan will defeat a perfectly written plan every time.

This campaign is built on a simple belief:

- **People** matter more than consultants.
- **Relationships** matter more than television.
- **Trust** matters more than money.
- **Community** matters more than politics.

We are not building a campaign.

**We are building a movement.**

A movement that leaves stronger communities behind wherever it goes.

A movement that develops leaders.

A movement that teaches people how to organize.

A movement that creates relationships that exist long after Election Day.

---

## Build quietly, stand up everywhere

We are intentionally building quietly.

Sun Tzu teaches that strength should not always appear as strength.

| Others focus on… | We focus on… |
| ---------------- | ------------ |
| Appearances | Foundations |
| Headlines | Relationships |
| Spending money | Building trust |
| Impressions | Conversations |

**The goal is simple:**

Spend four months building a statewide network of leaders, volunteers, hosts, storytellers, coalition partners, community champions, and trusted messengers.

Then stand up a statewide campaign operation that appears to emerge everywhere at once.

Not because it happened suddenly.

**Because it was built patiently.**

County by county. Town by town. Church by church. House by house. Conversation by conversation.

That is how grassroots campaigns become winning campaigns.

## How power grows

Every volunteer is expected to **recruit leaders**.

Every leader is expected to **develop additional leaders**.

Every relationship is expected to **create new relationships**.

**Power grows through trust.**

**Trust grows through presence.**

**Presence grows through service.**

That is how we win Arkansas.

## Fundraising doctrine — Open doors

Most statewide campaigns build around call time.

**Our model is different.**

Kelly's most valuable asset is **time**. A candidate spending six hours a day calling dead-end donor lists is not meeting voters.

### Community fundraising

- House parties · Backyard events · Coffee gatherings
- Community dinners · County fundraisers · Campus fundraisers
- Small-donor digital fundraising · Story-driven fundraising · Coalition fundraising

**The ask becomes:**

> *"Who should Kelly meet?"*

not

> *"Who can Kelly call?"*

That creates **introductions** instead of cold calls.

### The fundraising team's mission: Open doors

Not raise money directly.

- **Open doors**
- **Build events**
- **Create introductions**
- **Expand networks**

**Money follows relationships.**

Mechanics and targets live in [Campaign Budget & Fundraising Targets](/election-plan/executive-book/budget).

## Quitman 100 — example mission

Quitman is the type of community project we build.

Not registration. Not fundraising. Not a giant turnout target.

### Goal

**100 meaningful conversations before Election Day.**

### Definition

100 people personally engaged through:

- Coffee meetings · Breakfasts · Church conversations
- Rotary · Civic clubs · House parties
- Small business visits · Community events

### Then

Recruit **10 Power of 5 leaders**. Each recruits **5**.

That creates a **50-person relationship network**.

This is achievable. People can see it. People can own it. People can track it.

**The message:**

> *Quitman can directly influence a statewide election — not through money, through relationships.*
