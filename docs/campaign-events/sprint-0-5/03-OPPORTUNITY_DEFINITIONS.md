**Sprint 0.5 · Status:** Draft — pending leadership lock

## Principle (lock this)

> A county can be **Critical + Strong + Low opportunity** (saturated base — limited Kelly ROI).  
> Another can be **Important + High opportunity + Moderate readiness** (high Kelly ROI).  
> The engine must distinguish **electoral weight** from **marginal gain**.
