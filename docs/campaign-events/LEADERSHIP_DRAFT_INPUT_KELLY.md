## Pre-session homework (all participants)

Arrive with **one sentence** answering:

> What is our winning theory?

Not a paragraph. Not a strategy deck. One sentence — written before hearing others.

**Facilitator:** Collect on index cards or shared doc. Compare before debating county tiers. If five leaders produce five different theories, alignment work comes first. If answers converge, strategic cohesion is confirmed.

## Values foundation (for winning theory discussion)

**People over politics.** Community over state politics. Training people to organize in their community matters more than the campaign alone. The campaign should leave **structure, organization, and leadership** behind.

**Reconciliation.** Bring Arkansans back together. Find values to build on across difference.

**Balls and strikes.** The Secretary of State job is **100% non-partisan** — execute the law, the Constitution, and the legislative framework without politicizing the office.

**Direct democracy.** Support the ballot initiative process as intentional citizen accountability.

**Republic and democracy.** Arkansas is both. The SOS job is to improve civic engagement, voter registration, and voter participation — and to establish guidelines for ballot initiatives while partnering with citizens through the process.

**Community self-organization.** Guide communities to recruit, build teams, and campaign independently. Individual rights; government exists to serve the people.
