# 08 — Systems / gaps / Destiny became Grace

## Verbatim (from source)

Years of fighting… Years of uncertainty…  
is this going to work?  
And I learned something in that process—  
systems don’t always work the way they should.  
People fall through cracks. Good people. Kids. Families.  
And if no one steps in—those gaps stay open.  
And I wasn’t willing to let that happen.  
And somewhere along the way—Destiny became Grace.  
And she changed me… resilience… identity… strength.  
And now she’s a mother—and she’s filling gaps for the next generation.

## Expansion levers

- **Systems**: one careful beat—**not** naming villains; naming *gaps* (already your frame).
- **Grace**: lessons you already list—unpack *one* with a concrete **feeling** (identity) not a new story.
- **Next generation**: handoff image—*mother filling gaps*—echo mom section without copying it.
