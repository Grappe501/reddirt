# 10 — Dad / husband / statewide initiative

## Verbatim (from source)

My dad taught me—no ceilings. “Conceive it. Believe it. Achieve it.”  
And he taught me—your life is about what you do for others.  
My husband showed me what that looks like.  
Statewide ballot initiative. No money. No experience.  
People said it was impossible… But he stepped anyway.  
And we didn’t get all the way there. But we got close.  
Closer than anyone thought we could.  
And that’s when I realized—  
you don’t step because you’re guaranteed success.  
You step because it matters.

## Expansion levers

- **Dad**: one beat on *ceiling* as **limit someone else tries to place on you**—generic.
- **Husband**: honor **proximity** without inventing dollar amounts or vote totals—keep “close” as you already framed it.
- Bridge back to **tonight**: the lesson transfers to any impossible-looking thing the audience carries.
