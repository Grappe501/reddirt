# 07 — Destiny: Christmas / I stepped

## Verbatim (from source)

There was a moment in my life where everything changed.  
A Christmas party. My parents’ house. Full. Loud. Busy.  
And right in the middle of it—I met her. She was three.  
*(pause)* Her name was Destiny. *(pause)* And I knew.  
And here’s the truth—you don’t always get a plan…  
But sometimes—you just know.  
And when you know—you have a choice. Walk away… or step.  
So I stepped. Monday morning—I got on the phone.  
And then I got to work. Paperwork. Calls. Follow-ups…  
Because I had learned something already—when something matters—you move.  
But it didn’t happen overnight. It took years…

## Expansion levers

- **Pacing**: Christmas contrast (noise / warmth / pivot) without inventing who else was there.
- **Process**: generic “stack of steps” language—forms, waiting, revisiting—*no* fabricated agencies.
- Emotional truth you already own: **uncertainty** as a companion, not a verdict.
