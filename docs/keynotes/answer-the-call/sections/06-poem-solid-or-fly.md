# 06 — Poem: solid ground / taught to fly

## Verbatim (from source)

Years later, she gave me a poem.  
A woman standing at the edge of darkness. About to step.  
“There will be something solid… or you will be taught how to fly.”  
I used to think that was about bravery.  
*(pause)*  
Now I think it’s about responsibility.
