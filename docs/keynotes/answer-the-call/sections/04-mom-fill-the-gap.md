# 04 — Mom: fill the gap

## Verbatim (from source)

That belief didn’t start with me. It started with my mom.  
*(pause)*  
My mom is here tonight.  
And if you know her—you know this is true—  
she is the first person to fill the gap.  
You need supper? She’s got it.  
You need to pull off an event? She’ll handle it.  
Something falls through? She steps in before anyone even asks.  
*(pause)*  
She doesn’t wait. She doesn’t hesitate. She just moves.
