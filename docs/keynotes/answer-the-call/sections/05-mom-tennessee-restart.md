# 05 — Mom: Tennessee / walk away / start over

## Verbatim (from source)

But that didn’t come from nowhere.  
That came from a lifetime of doing what was required—no matter the cost.  
My mom stood by my dad as he built his career.  
And she worked. Hard.  
The kind of hard where you’re doing whatever it takes to keep things moving forward.  
The kind of hard where nobody sees it. Nobody applauds it.  
But everything depends on it.  
And then she made a decision.  
She built something of her own.  
She learned how to sell.  
And she didn’t just try—she became one of the top two agents in the entire state of Tennessee.  
*(pause)*  
That doesn’t happen by accident. That’s discipline…  
And then life shifted. We moved.  
And she had to walk away from everything she built.  
*(pause)*  
And here’s what I learned from that—  
you can build something great… and still be asked to start over.  
*(pause)*  
And when that moment comes—you don’t sit down.  
*(pause)*  
You step in again.
