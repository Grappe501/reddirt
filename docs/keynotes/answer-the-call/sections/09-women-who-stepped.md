# 09 — Women who stepped (historical quad)

## Verbatim (from source)

Women like Clara Barton—who walked into war zones without training—because people needed help.  
Jo Ann Robinson—who didn’t wait for permission—and moved a movement forward overnight.  
Septima Poinsette Clark—who taught people to navigate systems designed to keep them out.  
Katherine Johnson—who stayed in rooms that didn’t welcome her—because the work needed her.
