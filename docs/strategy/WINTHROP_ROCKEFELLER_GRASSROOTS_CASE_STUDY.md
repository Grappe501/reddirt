# Winthrop Rockefeller Grassroots Case Study

Phase 5A uses Rockefeller as an inspiration case study for coalition-building and grassroots transition-era campaigning, not as a direct modern tactical template.

## Directly sourced anchors

- Rockefeller's 1966 election was a watershed in Arkansas political history and the first Republican gubernatorial win since Reconstruction-era benchmarks.
- Encyclopedia of Arkansas describes major political change and the end of Faubus-era political organization dynamics.
- Volunteer artifact evidence exists (1966 campaign volunteer badge).
