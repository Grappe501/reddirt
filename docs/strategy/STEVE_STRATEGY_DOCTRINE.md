# Steve Strategy Doctrine

Phase 5A establishes a doctrine intake layer so the Campaign Brain learns how Steve frames Arkansas campaign work before county strategy generation.

## Governing doctrine themes

- Unity over division.
- People organizing their own communities.
- Trust and relationships as core campaign infrastructure.
- Eye-to-eye local campaigning.
- County-by-county, city-by-city, precinct-by-precinct execution.
- Secretary of State as a balls-and-strikes office focused on process legitimacy.

## Operational implications

- Event visibility and relational trust are first-order inputs.
- Volunteer ladders and phone trees are planning primitives.
- Doctrine remains read-only until Steve review.
- No final strategy output in Phase 5A.

## Guardrails

- No individual targeting.
- No contact list generation.
- No outreach automation.
- Missing doctrine segments remain `MISSING` or `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
