# GOTV Backward Planning Model

This model frames planning backward from Election Day and remains operational-only in Phase 5A.

## Backward windows

- `T-90` to `T-60`: captain structure, poll watcher training plan, visibility map.
- `T-59` to `T-30`: volunteer shift commitments, phone tree readiness, relational pushes.
- `T-29` to `T-14`: early vote coverage planning, legal and material readiness, candidate travel lock.
- `T-13` to `T-1`: dry-runs, backup coverage, escalation prep.
- `T-0`: Election Day execution and reporting.

## Mandatory controls

- Legal/compliance review flags are required in each window.
- No legal advice generation.
- No individual voter targeting or outreach automation.
