# Relational organizing foundation (RedDirt Reach)

**Lane:** `RedDirt/` only

## Vision

**RedDirt Reach** helps volunteers organize through **people they already know**: trust, context, follow-ups, and respectful asks — with **staff review** before anything sensitive scales.

## Current state (foundation)

- Database support for relational tables is summarized in `data/relational-organizing-capability-map.json` (generated by the foundation report script).
- The **preview page** at `/admin/workbench/people/relational-organizing` explains future volunteer and staff flows; it does **not** save data yet.

## Safety and privacy

- **No bulk contact import** is activated from this slice.
- **No public mass texting** from Reach preview.
- Relationship notes can be sensitive — **train volunteers and staff** before go-live.
