1. **Left alone:** Meet Kelly conversation rhythm; hero still; no long homepage testimonials.  
2. **Biggest tradeoff:** Published campaign-confirmed names without public announcement URLs or dates — gains trust through clarity of *who*, costs the polish of sourced timestamps until Steve supplies them.
