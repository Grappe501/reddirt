## Product frame

Voters for a statewide constitutional office are **decision-focused**. They ask:

1. Who is she?
2. Can she do this job?
3. Does she understand this office?
4. Will she represent people like me?
5. Can I trust her?

Everything on the public site must support those questions. Biography, media, and CTAs are evidence—not destinations.

**Locked promise:**

> Kelly is prepared to serve Arkansas.

**Not the promise:**

> Kelly has an inspiring story.

---

## Doctrine locks

### 1. Kelly is the guide, not the product

The campaign is about the office and the people it serves. Kelly’s story is evidence of preparation, not the product being sold.

### 2. Every page gets one job

| Page | Primary job |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Build trust and interest |
| `/about` | Explain qualifications |
| `/priorities` | Explain governing approach |
| `/about/journey` | Demonstrate listening and engagement |
| `/kelly-speaks` | Let voters hear Kelly directly |
| `/campaign-photos` | Show authentic campaign activity |
| `/endorsements` | Third-party validation |
| `/get-involved` | Convert interest into participation |

If a page drifts into another page’s job, trim it.

### 3. Media is proof, not decoration

Photography, video, trail stills, and (when confirmed) endorsements substantiate claims. Prefer showing Mena, confirmed trail stops, Hot Springs Village video, etc. over repeating “she listens.”

### 4. Replace adjectives with evidence

Avoid unsupported: dedicated, passionate, hardworking, committed, servant leader. Ask: can we show this instead?

### 5. Homepage tone stays calm

Not a fundraising appeal, flyer, white paper, or memoir. A confident introduction. Visitor exit thought:

> I understand who Kelly is, what she wants to do, and why I should pay attention.

## Remaining launch priority order

1. **Visual polish** — spacing, intentional crops, CTA/button consistency, typography, mobile
2. **Technical QA** — production build, accessibility, link crawl, forms
3. **Media quality** — captions, alt text, selection, video presentation
4. **Content accuracy** — endorsements, transcripts, office-specific claims

**Do not** add major sections or rewrite large copy blocks unless a factual issue is found.
