## 1. Executive vision

Email Command Center V2 is **not** an incremental improvement to a campaign inbox.

It is the transition **from:**

- campaign software  
- workflow tooling  
- operator dashboards  
- email infrastructure  

**into:**

### A full campaign intelligence operating system

V2 transforms the system from a **governed communications platform** into a **continuously learning, operationally aware, AI-assisted** campaign command infrastructure.

The purpose of V2 is **not** simply to add features.

The purpose of V2 is to create:

- a self-improving campaign intelligence layer  
- a scalable operational brain  
- a campaign memory system  
- a multi-surface command infrastructure  
- a guided self-building architecture  
- a continuously extensible platform  

The system should eventually become capable of:

- reasoning about campaign operations  
- coordinating communication workflows  
- understanding campaign philosophy  
- organizing knowledge  
- managing scheduling and logistics  
- assisting with field operations  
- improving strategic decision support  
- generating operational recommendations  
- continuously extending itself through **governed** self-build protocols  

V2 is where the project evolves **from** “campaign dashboard” **into** “campaign operating intelligence infrastructure.”

## 2. V2 core mission

### Mission statement

Build the most intelligent, **governed**, extensible, AI-assisted campaign operating system possible for lean statewide campaigns.

The system must:

- centralize campaign intelligence  
- reduce operational chaos  
- preserve institutional memory  
- increase communication quality  
- improve staff coordination  
- strengthen governance and safety  
- expand campaign capacity without requiring large staffing growth  
- create repeatable operational systems  
- enable future self-building capabilities  

The system must **never** become:

- reckless automation  
- uncontrolled AI execution  
- hallucinated political messaging  
- ungoverned mass communication  
- opaque black-box operations  

**Human approval remains central.**

## 3. V2 strategic philosophy — guiding principles

1. **Queue-first operations** — Everything becomes structured operational workflow.  
2. **AI-assisted, human-governed** — AI recommends; humans approve.  
3. **Source-grounded intelligence** — Claims must be distinguishable from inference.  
4. **Campaign memory matters** — The system should remember institutional knowledge.  
5. **Every communication creates intelligence** — Emails, tasks, events, responses, and workflows should enrich the system.  
6. **Governance is infrastructure** — Safety and review are not optional layers.  
7. **Self-build must be controlled** — Self-building behavior must operate inside explicit protocols.  
8. **Operations must become scalable** — The system should amplify staff capacity.  
9. **Visibility creates discipline** — Operators always understand what exists, what is blocked, what is pending, what is risky, what is ready.  
10. **The system must be extensible** — Every subsystem should support future expansion.

## 5. V2 major system layers

### Layer A — Communications intelligence layer

**Purpose:** Transform communication into operational intelligence.

**Existing foundation (repo-aligned, high level):** Gmail monitor, queue triage, Message Studio, SendGrid foundation, AI drafting, editorial review, send governance, analytics shells.

**V2 expansion goals (future slices):**

- Advanced drafting, adaptive messaging, narrative continuity, tone optimization, strategic framing  
- Persuasion analysis, message clustering, reply prediction, communications scoring  
- **Campaign Voice Engine V2** — indexed doctrine, speech/writing memory, issue-specific voice, audience-aware framing, role-aware drafting, historical recall  
- **Strategic messaging** — issue-response planning, rapid-response recommendation, conflict detection, narrative consistency, opposition-sensitivity review (**no** unsourced claims)  
- **Deliverability intelligence** — send timing, fatigue detection, risk scoring, subject optimization, engagement learning  

**Progress tracking (V2 sub-bars — design only):** communications intelligence · campaign voice · editorial intelligence · deliverability intelligence · strategic messaging.

---

### Layer B — Campaign memory and knowledge layer

**Purpose:** Long-term institutional memory.

**V2 goals:**

- **Campaign knowledge graph** — people, organizations, issues, counties, events, campaigns, legislators, volunteers, donors, relationships, narratives (governed, consent-aware).  
- **RAG / retrieval** — speeches, blogs, policy docs, briefs, debate prep, donor language, volunteer scripts, media statements, plans (**ingestion and licensing are explicit packets**).  
- **Memory intelligence** — retrieve prior messaging, avoid contradictions, cite known positions, reuse framing, identify gaps (**citation ≠ legal approval**).  
- **Knowledge quality system** — indexed / unindexed / stale / approved / source quality / confidence.  

**Progress tracking:** knowledge ingestion · retrieval quality · campaign memory · knowledge graph · source grounding.

---

### Layer C — Operational intelligence layer

**Purpose:** Structured operational intelligence beyond comms alone.

**V2 goals:** task intelligence (recommendations, prioritization, escalation, dependencies, workload), daily executive intelligence (briefings), operational forecasting, workflow optimization (duplicates, stalled flows, stale messaging).

**Progress tracking:** task intelligence · operational forecasting · executive briefing · workflow optimization.

---

### Layer D — Calendar and scheduling intelligence layer

**Purpose:** Campaign scheduling intelligence.

**V2 goals:** tentative scheduling engine (holds, prep, travel, follow-ups), AI schedule intelligence (routing, clustering, travel minimization, overbooking warnings), calendar governance (tentative vs approved), scheduling communications (replies, reminders, prep packets).

**Progress tracking:** tentative scheduling · calendar AI · travel intelligence · scheduling workflow.

**Repo note:** Calendar HQ and related infrastructure **already exist** in RedDirt; V2 means **governed intelligence** on top, not greenfield calendar.

---

### Layer E — Audience and relationship intelligence layer

**Purpose:** Deeper understanding of campaign relationships.

**V2 goals:** relationship graph, audience intelligence (segmentation, affinity, fatigue), relationship recommendations, geographic intelligence (heatmaps, regional opportunities, organizer gaps).

**Progress tracking:** relationship graph · audience intelligence · geographic intelligence · engagement intelligence.

---

### Layer F — Automation intelligence layer

**Purpose:** **Governed** operational automation.

**Philosophy:** Reduce repetition and improve coordination; **never** bypass approvals, send uncontrolled communications, or mutate sensitive data automatically without explicit packets.

**V2 goals:** automation policy engine (triggers, approval gates, escalation, confidence thresholds), safe action library (reminders, review prompts, reconciliation, escalation, prep, draft preparation), observability (why triggered, what blocked, overrides).

**Progress tracking:** automation policy · observability · safe action library · escalation governance.

---

### Layer G — Analytics and strategic intelligence layer

**Purpose:** Executive-level campaign intelligence.

**V2 goals:** executive dashboard, predictive intelligence (honest uncertainty), strategic insights (what is working, counties, failing workflows).

**Progress tracking:** executive analytics · predictive intelligence · strategic insights · operational metrics.

---

### Layer H — Self-build intelligence layer

**Purpose:** **Governed** self-building architecture — **not** uncontrolled autonomous coding.

**Vision:** Guided autonomous expansion — understand architecture, goals, governance, boundaries, dependencies; generate **safe** implementation plans; execute **bounded** slices; self-report progress; self-generate handoffs; self-track readiness — **all inside human-approved protocols**.

## Appendix A — Current technical context (non-normative)

The following observations describe **today’s** RedDirt build posture; they are **not** promises of V2 completion.

- **Build health:** Production `next build` succeeding, large route surface (including organizing, county, media, relational, events, ECC, automation, calendar, social, owned media) indicates a **broad campaign OS**, not a single marketing site.  
- **Message Studio:** Large client bundle reflects dense editorial + drafting + review + packet tooling — treat as **subsystem**, not a simple composer.  
- **Email Command Center:** Dedicated routes (analytics, audiences, automation, daily, Gmail, imports, map, message studio, profiles, readiness, send execution, SendGrid) match **enterprise-style** subsystem boundaries.  
- **Calendar HQ:** Existing calendar workbench, webhooks, sync, and planning/suggest APIs mean **scheduling intelligence in V2 is an evolution**, not a greenfield.  
- **AI extension points:** e.g. `src/lib/email-workflow/intelligence/extension-points.ts` — pluggable intelligence seams align with Layer A/H direction.  
- **Governance:** `email:no-send-scan` and explicit **`EMAIL_WORKFLOW_CAN_SEND_FROM_ITEM`** posture are **structural** protections; V2 must preserve them.  
- **Lint warnings:** Unused variables and `<img>` hints are **quality/polish**, not structural “system broken” signals.  
- **Leverage point:** The largest near-term value is **intelligence depth** (memory, retrieval, reasoning, orchestration) on top of existing infrastructure — **bounded slices**, not random scope expansion.

## Appendix B — Biggest intentional “moat” (design intent)

**Campaign memory + governed knowledge graph + retrieval** — once the system can **honestly** access and cite institutional material (with consent, licensing, and RLS/governance), downstream drafting, scheduling, and executive briefings compound in value. This remains **packetized work**; no automatic ingestion of sensitive or third-party content without explicit design.
