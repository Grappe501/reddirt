## Governing philosophy

All faith outreach is grounded in the [Big Table Democrat Doctrine](../relational-organizing/BIG-TABLE-DEMOCRAT-DOCTRINE.md):

- Dignity for all people · freedom of conscience
- Room for Christian Democrats and faith communities without demanding agreement on every issue
- Relationships before rhetoric · local presence before national party fights

**Do not lead with national Democratic brand identity.** Lead with schools, elections, community, and respect.

## Field principles

1. Listen first — pastors and faith leaders set the pace
2. No lecture — Power of 5 and house conversations, not rallies in sanctuaries
3. Log outcomes — `faithLeadersEngaged` in [event-outcomes.json](../../../data/campaign-brain/event-outcomes.json)
4. Connect to Lane 4 through **trust**, not debate
