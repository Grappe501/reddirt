# Standard Ask Package

When Kelly meets a current official, former official, county chair, pastor, labor leader, or community influencer:

1. What should we know about your area?
2. Who are the 5–10 people we should meet?
3. Who are the volunteer leaders here?
4. What events should we attend?
5. What organizations should we engage?
6. Would you be willing to make introductions?
7. Would you consider supporting the campaign financially?
8. How would you like to stay involved?

**Priority:** Face-to-face whenever possible.
