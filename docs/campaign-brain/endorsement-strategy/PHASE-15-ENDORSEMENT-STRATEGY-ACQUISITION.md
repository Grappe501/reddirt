# Phase 15 — Endorsement Strategy & Acquisition System

Updated: 2026-06-15

## Mission

Identify, prioritize, secure, track, announce, and **activate** endorsements — not collect names.

## Five evaluation values

- **Credibility**
- **Volunteer activation**
- **Donor activation**
- **Network access**
- **Voter persuasion**
