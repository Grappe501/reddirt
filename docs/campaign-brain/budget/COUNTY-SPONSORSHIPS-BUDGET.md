# County Event Sponsorships Budget

> Planning budget framework only — not final accounting, not guaranteed fundraising, not donor-facing claims. · **Modeled assumptions** — relationship-building spend across 75 counties.

County fairs, festivals, Democratic dinners, Juneteenth, civic events, candidate forums — $100–$500 each adds up across 75 counties

Small **$100–$500** sponsorships accumulate quickly. These are often among the highest relationship-building expenditures in rural Arkansas.
