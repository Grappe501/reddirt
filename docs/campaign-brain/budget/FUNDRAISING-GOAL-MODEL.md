## Monthly burn target (working scenario)

Leadership should plan to raise roughly **$39,001/month** through Election Day — including Kelly's salary, field strategy, and the digital force-multiplier program.

## Digital doctrine

Digital is a **force multiplier for field operations** — not a replacement for them. Do not budget like a typical statewide campaign. See [DIGITAL-ADVERTISING-BUDGET.md](./DIGITAL-ADVERTISING-BUDGET.md).

## Sherwood contribution

Model Sherwood as **net** fundraising after costs — see [SHERWOOD-60-BUDGET.md](./SHERWOOD-60-BUDGET.md). Mid scenario net: ~$11,500 projected (not guaranteed).
