# Governance Checkpoint

> Architecture complete. The question is organizational readiness.

---

## Is the organization prepared to feed the Brain?

### The Brain needs

- Verified dates · verified contacts · attendance estimates
- Outcome reporting · visit logs
- Registration counts · volunteer counts
- Clerk interactions · faith interactions

### The campaign produces

- Travel · events · conversations · registrations · relationships

**The operational challenge:** ensure the second consistently updates the first.

---

## What was built (frozen)

| Layer | Status |
| ----- | ------ |
| Strategy (lanes · VCI · clusters · plurality) | Complete |
| Intelligence (playbooks · registration · clerk/faith) | Complete |
| Optimization (scoring · coverage · recommendations) | Complete |

Additional strategic modules will produce **diminishing returns**.

## Accountability layer (minimal)

[Brain Health Dashboard](./brain-health-dashboard.md) — five metrics:

| Metric | Goal |
| ------ | ---: |
| Verified events | 300+ |
| Counties covered | 75 |
| Guardrail violations | 0 |
| Outcome reports submitted | 100% |
| Days since last Brain update | ≤7 |

If these improve, the Brain improves. If they stagnate, recommendation quality declines.

## Operation Calendar Truth — exit criteria

When met, the campaign can confidently lock Weeks 1–20:

- 300+ verified events
- All 75 county fairs verified
- Tier A county events verified
- Guardrail violations below 10
- Every county assigned a contact owner ([`county-contact-owners.json`](../../data/campaign-brain/county-contact-owners.json))
- Weekly update cadence functioning

Track live: [brain-health-dashboard.md](./brain-health-dashboard.md)

---

## The distinction that matters

Most campaigns measure **activity**.

This system measures **progress against opportunity** — captured vs remaining.

That keeps strategy, operations, and field work connected through the final stretch.

---

## Success definition

Not inventing new models.

**Discipline:** verified information flowing into the system every week.

Once that rhythm exists, the Brain can do the job it was built to do.
