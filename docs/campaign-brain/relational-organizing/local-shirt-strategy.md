# Local Shirt Strategy

> Community pride on front. Campaign logo on back. Local printer wins.

---

## Why local editions

Not because of shirts — because of **relationships**.

A central campaign shirt is inventory. A **Conway Edition** is a community stakeholder event.

---

## Model

- **Front:** Community pride — local landmark · slogan · county identity
- **Back:** Kelly Grappe campaign logo · website · disclaimer
- **Made by:** Local vendor (screen printer · embroidery shop)
- **Sold / distributed:** Through local champions · events · fundraisers

---

## What local editions produce

- Local printer wins (economic participant)
- Community identifies with the shirt (local ownership)
- Local media covers it (earned media)
- Local business becomes stakeholder (Relationship Capital)

Far more valuable than 5,000 generic shirts ordered online.
