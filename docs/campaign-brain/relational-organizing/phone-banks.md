# Phone Banks

> The Brain distinguishes call **purpose** — not just call volume.

---

## Four types

### Relationship calls

- Reconnect former Democrats (Lane 2)
- Invite to events
- Recruit volunteers

**Goal:** 5,000 · Track: `phoneBanks.relationshipCalls`

---

### Visit invitation calls

- Before Kelly arrives in county
- Confirm local champions · drive attendance

**Goal:** 3,000 · Track: `phoneBanks.visitInvitationCalls`

---

### GOTV calls

- Final 30 days
- Confirmed supporters · low-propensity Democrats

**Goal:** 15,000 · Track: `phoneBanks.gotvCalls`

---

### Follow-up calls

- After events
- Convert attendees into volunteers · sign captains · postcard writers

**Goal:** 4,000 · Track: `phoneBanks.followUpCalls`

---

## Rollup

All types sum to `relationshipCapital.phoneCallsCompleted`.
