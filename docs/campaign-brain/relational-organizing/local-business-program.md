# Local Business Relationship Program

> The campaign becomes an **economic participant** in the community.

---

## For every county visit

### Stay local

Airbnb · local inns · county-seat hotels when practical.

### Eat local

Independent restaurants. Avoid chains when a local option exists.

### Shop local

Office supplies · print shops · event materials from local stores.

### Highlight local

- Substack · social media · campaign email
- Letters to the editor · local newspaper submissions
- Thank-you posts tagging local businesses

### Thank local

Public recognition at events · signed thank-you from Kelly when appropriate.

---

## Relationship Capital

Each highlighted local business increments `localBusinessesHighlighted` in [`relationship-assets.json`](../../data/campaign-brain/relationship-assets.json).

Target: **200+ local businesses highlighted** statewide.

---

## Field checklist (per visit)

- [ ] Stayed local (lodging)
- [ ] Ate local (restaurant named in visit log notes)
- [ ] Shopped local (vendor for materials)
- [ ] Highlighted local (social / media / LTE)
- [ ] Thanked local (public recognition)

Log in county visit notes or relationship-assets byCounty.
