# The Big Table Doctrine

## Governing Philosophy · A Working-Class Democrat for All of Arkansas

> **Classification:** Campaign governing philosophy — read before faith outreach, Lane 4 conversion, surrogate deployment, and county-chair recruitment.

Kelly Grappe represents a different kind of Democrat than many Arkansans have seen in years.

This campaign is rooted in **working-class politics**, **community rebuilding**, **fiscal responsibility**, **public education**, **local dignity**, and **respect for personal freedom**.

---

## We believe

**Arkansas is strongest when everyday people can build good lives in strong communities.**

**Public education is not charity** — it is the foundation of self-sufficiency, workforce development, and long-term fiscal responsibility. If Arkansas wants fewer people dependent on government assistance, then Arkansas must invest seriously in the schools, skills, and opportunities that help families stand on their own.

**Dignity for all people.** Arkansans have the right to believe, worship, live, and love according to their conscience, so long as those beliefs do not take away the rights and freedoms of others.

**Our politics has become too small, too angry, and too controlled by national party fights.** Arkansas needs leaders focused on everyday life: good schools, honest elections, small businesses, safe communities, local jobs, and a government that works.

**Secretary of State and all elected offices at the county level and below should be nonpartisan.** Election administration, county government, clerks, sheriffs, judges, school boards, and local offices should serve the people first — not political parties.

**Only American citizens should vote in American elections.** We also believe corporations and employers must be held accountable when they exploit undocumented workers and help fuel the immigration crisis for profit.

**The Democratic Party must build a bigger table.**

---

## Room at the table for

- Working-class Democrats
- Rural Democrats
- Christian Democrats
- Pro-life Democrats
- Conservative Democrats
- Union Democrats
- Civil rights Democrats
- Small business Democrats
- Independents who want honest government and a better Arkansas

We do not have to agree on every single issue to work together for better schools, better jobs, fair elections, stronger communities, and dignity for every Arkansan.

---

## What we are asking

This campaign is **not** asking people to give up who they are.

It is asking Arkansans to **come back to the table**.
