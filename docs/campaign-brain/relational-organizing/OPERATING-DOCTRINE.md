# Relational Organizing Engine

## Kelly Grappe Campaign Operating Doctrine

> **Fifth execution engine** — underneath the Four Lanes. All four lanes depend on human relationships.

**Governing philosophy:** [Big Table Democrat Doctrine](./BIG-TABLE-DEMOCRAT-DOCTRINE.md)

---

## This is not an advertising campaign

It is a **relationship campaign**.

We believe:

- Voters trust people they know more than people they see on television
- Local relationships create local credibility
- Repeated presence matters
- Campaigns are won by **conversations**, not impressions

Every activity should strengthen relationships within Arkansas communities.

---

## What we prioritize

1. Yard signs · Shirts · Buttons · Flags
2. Local volunteers · House parties
3. Civic clubs · Churches · Libraries · County clerks
4. Local media · Local businesses
5. County fairs · Festivals · Sports events
6. Extension Homemakers · Chambers · Rotary · Lions · Community organizations

We seek to **become part of local communities** — not simply visit them.

---

## Every county visit leaves behind

- New relationships · New volunteers
- New signs · New shirts · New postcards
- New phone bank participants · New postcard writers · New local champions

**The objective is not attendance. The objective is local ownership.**

---

## How this connects to the Four Lanes

| Lane | What moves it |
| ---- | ------------- |
| Lane 1 — Democratic Retention | Repeated local presence · trusted messengers |
| Lane 2 — Democratic Reactivation | Relationship calls · house parties · peer postcards |
| Lane 3 — Registration | Youth outreach · civic events · local champions |
| Lane 4 — GOP / Independent Conversion | Credibility built through community participation |

**Relationship Capital is the mechanism through which the Four Lanes actually move.**
