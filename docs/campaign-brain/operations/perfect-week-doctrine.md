# What Does a Perfect Week Look Like?

> One-page operational doctrine · Campaign Operations Mode

The calendar serves this philosophy — not the other way around.

---

## Every week we:

1. **Attend events** — county fairs, diners, school events, civic clubs, Democratic meetings, small businesses
2. **Recruit volunteers** — Power of 5 · county captains · founding team growth
3. **Build endorsements** — asks logged · meetings scheduled · validators identified
4. **Tell stories** — Reel · album · community story · local spotlight · upcoming stop (every stop)
5. **Capture contacts** — sign-in → Po5 → Mobilize → human contact index
6. **Schedule future visits** — relationship density over county-box checking
7. **Generate local media** — advisories · clerk intros · civic club coverage
8. **Build relationships** — NAACP · labor · AEA · clerks · faith · business validators

---

## Three jobs (Kelly's lens)

### Job 1 — Build visible momentum

People should see the campaign everywhere: fairs, diners, schools, Juneteenth, volunteer meetings, county Dem meetings, civic clubs, small businesses.

### Job 2 — Build validators

NAACP branches · AEA leaders · labor leaders · county clerks · former and current elected officials · faith leaders. These people create permission structures for voters.

### Job 3 — Build the volunteer machine

June 28 founding volunteer launch is one of the most important dates remaining. Without the network, systems remain theoretical.
