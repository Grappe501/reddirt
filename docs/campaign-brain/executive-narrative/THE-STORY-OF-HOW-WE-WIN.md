## I. Arkansas Is Different in 2026

This is not a generic Democratic statewide campaign trying to convince a majority of Arkansans to become Democrats.

Three facts change everything:

1. **Plurality wins.** Arkansas elects constitutional officers by plurality. There is no runoff. The largest coalition wins.

2. **Three candidates on the ballot.** Kelly Grappe (D) · Kim Hammer (R) · Michael Pakko (L). Victory does not require 50%+1.

3. **The electorate is already larger than we thought.** Over **102,000 Arkansas Democrats** voted in the 2024 presidential election but did not appear in the 2022 midterm baseline. They are not strangers. They are **our people who stopped showing up in midterms.**

Our first job is not persuasion at scale. Our first job is **bringing our own people back.**

## III. Why the GOP Primary Matters

Kim Hammer emerged from a **fractured Republican primary.** Norris Republicans and Harrison Republicans are not the same coalition. Primary turnout collapsed roughly **70%** from the first round to the runoff.

That fracture is our Lane 4 opportunity.

We do not win by attacking Republicans. We win by **relationships** — moderate Republicans, direct-democracy Republicans, faith-based conservatives, and business leaders who want a Secretary of State who shows up for counties.

Michael Pakko holds a **Libertarian lane** (~4% baseline from 2024 Treasurer). Some voters who might otherwise hold their nose for Hammer will have an alternative. Plurality math rewards a **unified Democratic coalition** that executes on four lanes simultaneously.

## IV. Why Democratic Drop-Off Matters

**102,070 votes.**

That is the number of Democrats who voted in 2024 but not in the 2022 midterm comparable.

Recovering half = **51,051 votes** — before a single Republican conversion, before a single new registration.

Pulaski County alone: **22,481 missing Democrats.** Recover 50% = **11,241 votes.**

This is the message for county chairs who think *we can't win here:*

> We already have the voters. We just have to bring them back.

Every county playbook now shows drop-off, recovery at 25%/50%/75%, and a Hope Index. Lane 2 is **measurable**, not theoretical.

High school seniors. Community colleges. Universities. Trade schools. Naturalized citizens. Military families. Young professionals. Community registration events.

Registration is not separate from victory. It is **Lane 3 of the same coalition.**

## VI. Why Power of 5 Matters

Television will not win this race. **Relationships will.**

Power of 5: every supporter identifies five people. Every conversation creates five more. Every room creates ten more rooms.

This is an eyeball-to-eyeball campaign.

- Lane 2: bring back the Democrats who already voted
- Lane 3: register the young and the new
- Lane 4: peel 12% of Republicans through trust, not attack ads

County playbooks now assign **Power of 5 goals** and volunteer targets by tier. Every county chair knows their mission.

## VII. Why County Fairs Matter

Arkansas still meets at fairs, festivals, and county seats.

**75 county fairs** are indexed in the Campaign Brain. Each receives an event score based on VCI weight, lane opportunity, clerk relevance, and media value.

Not every fair requires Kelly personally. Some require a **surrogate**, a **volunteer lead**, or a **county chair** with a registration table.

The system answers: **YES / MAYBE / NO** — and who should go.

## VIII. Why Clerk Relationships Matter

This race is for **Secretary of State.**

County clerks run elections in Arkansas. They need a SOS who funds training, publishes clear rules, and answers the phone — not another unfunded mandate from Little Rock.

Kelly's advantage: this is not a generic statewide office. **Clerk relationships are a scheduling priority**, not an afterthought.

The Clerk Relationship Layer tracks status, meetings, and follow-ups in all 75 counties. Benton and developing counties get **High priority** until relationships are established.

ACCA, county clerk conferences, and clerk-room conversations are disproportionately valuable events.

## IX. Why Faith Outreach Matters

Arkansas is one of the most church-attending states in America.

Faith outreach is not messaging. It is **routing.**

Christian congregational outreach. Interfaith opportunities — Muslim, Jewish, and faith-based service organizations. Pastor networks. Minister alliances. Community worship events.

The Faith Engagement Index ranks all 75 counties for faith-based organizing priority. Faith integrates into cluster routing alongside fairs and registration drives.

Kelly's story — stewardship, service, transparency — travels well in faith communities when it is **relationship-first**, not partisan lecturing.
