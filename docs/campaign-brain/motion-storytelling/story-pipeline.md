# Story Pipeline — Content Pyramid

Every stop generates multiple pieces. **Motion = momentum** — the public should never see silence.

## Pyramid

| Level | Asset | Timing |
| ----- | ----- | ------ |
| 1 | 15-second vertical video | Same day |
| 2 | Photo carousel | Same day |
| 3 | Local story | Next day |
| 4 | Substack article | Within week |
| 5 | Email recap | Weekly |

## Weekly cadence

- **Monday** — Live stop — vertical video same day
- **Tuesday** — Story from Monday — local angle
- **Wednesday** — Another angle from Monday stop
- **Thursday** — Local business spotlight
- **Friday** — Weekend event teaser
- **Saturday** — Live event — carousel + clip
- **Sunday** — Weekly recap email + Substack if ready

## Story categories (not "Kelly attended event")

- **Local Business Spotlight** — goal 200 by Election Day
- **Arkansas Story (farmer, teacher, veteran…)** — goal 300 by Election Day
- **Community Spotlight** — goal 200 by Election Day
- **Arkansas Problem** — goal 100 by Election Day
- **Arkansas Hope** — goal 200 by Election Day
