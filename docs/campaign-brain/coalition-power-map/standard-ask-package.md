# Standard Relationship Ask Package

Every Kelly meeting — coalition, labor, faith, elected official, or community leader:

1. What should Kelly know about your area?
2. Who are the 5–10 people she must meet?
3. Who are the volunteer leaders in your area?
4. What events should Kelly attend?
5. Can you introduce us to local networks?
6. Can you help us connect with email/text/social networks where appropriate?
7. Can you help us identify financial supporters?
8. Can you personally support the campaign financially?
9. Can we schedule a follow-up meeting?
10. Can we publicly or privately list you as a supporter if appropriate?
