# MACROSCOPIC LIFE

## Book One — Bibliography and Endnote Register v1.0

**Status:** PUBLICATION APPARATUS — CONTROLLED WORKING REGISTER

This register is the shared scholarly apparatus for the print and digital editions. It does not replace the claim-source control files. It translates those controls into publication-facing endnote families and a normalized bibliography queue.

## Citation doctrine

1. Cite thesis-bearing factual claims, quantitative claims, specialist mechanisms, disputed claims, and bridge claims.
2. Do not cite the intelligent-microbe thought experiment as though it were an empirical result.
3. Do not use citations to disguise inference as established science.
4. Prefer authoritative reviews for broad framing and primary literature for narrow empirical claims.
5. Where a broad claim could otherwise depend on one laboratory or research program, add independent literature or narrow the prose.
6. Project synthesis is labeled as project synthesis.
7. The Eleven Tests are not attributed to one external source and are never presented as a consensus definition of life.
8. Endnotes should clarify evidence state when the reader could reasonably confuse observation, interpretation, analogy, or speculation.

Mandatory disclosure:
> **The Eleven Tests are a proposed synthesis for model comparison in this project. They are not a consensus definition of life or biological individuality. Different tests draw on different research traditions, several tests may depend on overlapping mechanisms, and positive results must not be added into an organism score. Their purpose is to make a stronger higher-order-individual hypothesis compete against serious lower-level alternatives and risk failure.**

Higher-level causation caution: higher-level variables may have explanatory or intervention value under specified models without settling ontology or proving biological individuality.

# Bibliography normalization queue

Before print release, every retained source must receive:
- complete author list or style-compliant truncation;
- publication year;
- exact article/chapter/book title;
- journal/book/publisher;
- volume/issue where applicable;
- page/article range where applicable;
- DOI where available;
- PMID/PMCID where useful for verification;
- edition for books where relevant;
- stable official URL only when required by the selected publication style.

Do not fabricate missing bibliographic fields. Unverified metadata remains in the queue until checked against the publication itself, PubMed/PMC, Crossref, publisher, or other authoritative bibliographic record.
