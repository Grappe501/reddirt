# Macroscopic Life — PUB-9C-R4

## Consciousness Citation, Ancestry, and Claim-Burden Review

**Status: RESEARCH ANCESTRY MAPPED — CONSCIOUSNESS REMAINS DOWNSTREAM OF INDIVIDUALITY**

This review prevents the Macroscopic Life consciousness module from presenting established consciousness theories as project inventions or treating any currently disputed theory as settled science.

## Core doctrine

> **SIGNAL ≠ INFORMATION ≠ INTEGRATION ≠ INTELLIGENCE ≠ AGENCY ≠ CONSCIOUSNESS.**

> **HIGHER-ORDER INDIVIDUALITY ≠ HIGHER-ORDER CONSCIOUSNESS.**

Passing the Macroscopic Life individuality tests does not establish phenomenal consciousness. Conversely, a theory of consciousness does not by itself establish biological individuality.

## Ancestry map

### Global Neuronal Workspace / Global Workspace approaches

Ancestry: workspace theories propose that conscious access is associated with information becoming globally available/broadcast across otherwise specialized processing systems.

Macroscopic Life use: **calibration only**. Global availability is a candidate dimension to investigate if a higher-order individual were independently supported. It is not permission to rename global telecommunications a conscious workspace.

### Integrated Information Theory

Ancestry: IIT links consciousness to intrinsic causal/informational structure and integration.

Macroscopic Life use: **one competing consciousness framework, not canon**. We may investigate integration, differentiation, and causal structure without adopting IIT's identity claims or treating a particular information measure as a consciousness meter.

### Recurrent Processing / re-entry approaches

Ancestry: recurrent or re-entrant processing theories emphasize feedback/recurrent interactions rather than purely feed-forward processing.

Macroscopic Life use: recurrent causal organization is a candidate research dimension. Recurrence alone is not consciousness.

### Higher-Order theories

Ancestry: higher-order approaches connect conscious awareness to representations or states concerning other mental representations/states.

Macroscopic Life use: motivates asking whether a candidate macro-system contains any physically identifiable higher-order/self-representational process. A database containing records about civilization is not automatically a higher-order conscious representation.

### Predictive-processing approaches

Ancestry: predictive and generative-model traditions analyze perception/cognition in terms of prediction, inference, prediction error, or related hierarchical processing.

Macroscopic Life use: relevant to Prediction Advantage and possible consciousness research, but prediction is not consciousness and retrospective fit is not prospective prediction.

### Attention Schema Theory / self-model approaches

Ancestry: Attention Schema Theory proposes that awareness is related to an internal simplified model of attention used in control.

Macroscopic Life use: motivates a candidate **self-model/control-model** dimension. A civilization-scale self-description would need to participate causally in control, not merely exist as text, maps, statistics, or cultural narratives.

## Contemporary state of the field

Consciousness science remains theoretically plural. Reviews compare global-workspace, integrated-information, recurrent/re-entry, higher-order, predictive-processing and other approaches rather than reporting one universally accepted mechanism.

A major 2025 adversarial collaboration directly tested divergent predictions from Global Neuronal Workspace Theory and Integrated Information Theory. The importance for Macroscopic Life is methodological: consciousness theories should be forced to make differential predictions and risk losing, rather than accumulating compatible observations after the fact.

That principle is fully aligned with Test 11 — Model Competition.

## Macroscopic Life's project-specific synthesis

The following are **not claimed as consensus consciousness criteria**. They are candidate research dimensions to be examined only after a candidate higher-order individual has independent evidential support:

1. global availability across specialized subsystems;
2. recurrent/re-entrant integration;
3. differentiated but coordinated states;
4. multimodal or cross-domain unity;
5. self-model/control-model dynamics;
6. selective attention-like allocation;
7. counterfactual sensitivity;
8. report/readout or behaviorally accessible state discrimination;
9. reversible loss-of-integration analogue;
10. explicit competition among consciousness theories.

No additive score across these dimensions is permitted to become a `consciousness meter`.

## Scale-dependence question

Macroscopic Life may legitimately ask:

> If phenomenal consciousness depends on particular forms of causal organization, integration, recurrence, global availability, self-modeling, or some mechanism not yet settled, must the relevant boundary always coincide with the organism boundary humans intuitively choose?

This is a **research question**, not an established result.

The possibility that a component can participate in a conscious system without representing the whole is familiar from ordinary nervous systems. The upward analogy establishes logical possibility of epistemic asymmetry only. It is not evidence that civilization or any other macroscopic candidate is conscious.

## Claim-burden locks for Book One

Allowed:

- consciousness science contains multiple competing theories;
- consciousness is not interchangeable with intelligence, agency, information, or integration;
- different theories emphasize different candidate mechanisms or functional architectures;
- competing consciousness theories can and should be tested using divergent predictions;
- higher-order consciousness is a legitimate downstream question if higher-order individuality first becomes empirically plausible.

Not allowed without substantially stronger evidence:

- civilization is conscious;
- global communication constitutes a global workspace in the neuroscientific sense;
- integration alone establishes experience;
- recurrence alone establishes experience;
- prediction alone establishes experience;
- a civilization-scale self-description establishes a conscious self-model;
- inability of humans to perceive a larger consciousness is evidence that one exists;
- any single current consciousness theory has been scientifically settled.

> **DO NOT ASK WHETHER THE WHOLE IS CONSCIOUS UNTIL YOU HAVE FIRST EARNED THE RIGHT TO CALL IT A WHOLE.**

Then, if higher-order individuality becomes empirically defensible:

> **DO NOT INFER EXPERIENCE FROM ORGANISMHOOD. MAKE COMPETING CONSCIOUSNESS MODELS PREDICT DIFFERENT OBSERVATIONS.**

This preserves the project's central discipline:

> **MEASURE. PERTURB. COMPARE MODELS. ALLOW FAILURE.**
