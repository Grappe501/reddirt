# P1 — “SYSTEM CHOOSES” RISKS PRE-LOADING AGENCY

The strongest narrative line is currently:

> **A GOAL DISCOVERED ONLY AFTER THE SYSTEM CHOOSES IS A STORY UNTIL IT PREDICTS THE NEXT CHOICE.**

It is memorable, but scientifically it creates a circularity risk. Chapter 10 is supposed to establish goal ownership before Chapter 11 asks whether the whole can choose. Saying the system “chooses” in the Chapter 10 evidentiary rule can smuggle the next property into the test.

Repair:

Retain the rhetorical force while operationalizing the term immediately or replacing it in the permanent signature.

Preferred repair candidate:

> **A GOAL IDENTIFIED ONLY AFTER THE OUTCOME IS A STORY UNTIL IT PREDICTS THE NEXT TRADEOFF.**

This is slightly less dramatic but much cleaner scientifically.

The heading `Make the Forest Choose` may remain as narrative provocation if the prose explicitly states that “choose” here means forcing incompatible trajectories, not demonstrating agency. Alternatively rename to `Force the Tradeoff` in prefreeze.

P1 severity: HIGH.

# P2 — “TARGET PHYSICALLY IMPLEMENTED IN THE THERMOSTAT” NEEDS CONTROL-THEORY PRECISION

The draft says the thermostat's target is physically implemented in the controller and that it detects departure from a control rule.

This is broadly defensible, but thermostat architectures differ. Simple bimetallic thermostats, hysteretic controllers, digital thermostats, proportional controllers, and more complex HVAC control systems implement reference/control behavior differently.

Repair:

Avoid implying every thermostat literally computes an explicit subtraction or symbolic comparison.

Use wording such as:

> The control architecture is physically sensitive to a reference condition or rule; alter that reference and the controller's behavior changes.

This preserves the positive-control point without committing to one comparator architecture.

P1 severity: HIGH because the thermostat is a recurring canonical adversary.

# P3 — HOMEOSTASIS/ALLOSTASIS CLAIM SHOULD NOT OVERSTATE CONSENSUS

The draft says later work on allostasis emphasized that successful regulation need not mean returning every variable to one fixed value. This is broadly grounded, but allostasis is not a single uncontested framework and terminology around setpoints, predictive regulation, and physiological stability varies.

Repair:

Add a small pluralism brake:

> In influential allostasis frameworks...

or equivalent.

Do not imply that all modern physiology has replaced fixed-setpoint homeostasis with one agreed allostatic model.

P1 severity: MODERATE.

# P4 — “SOMETHING MORE IMPORTANT” IS TOO TELEOLOGICAL BEFORE THE TEST

The draft says:

> One variable can be allowed to move so that something more important remains viable.

“More important” is precisely what the chapter is trying to infer.

Repair:

Use:

> One variable can move while another constraint remains within a viability-relevant range.

Then the tradeoff experiment asks whether a hierarchy among such constraints is prospectively recoverable.

P1 severity: HIGH conceptual cleanliness.

# P5 — TRADEOFF HIERARCHY MUST INCLUDE MECHANISTIC NULLS MORE EXPLICITLY

The draft correctly lists fixed architecture, scarcity, fragility, evolutionary history, and local feedback as rivals. But the chapter's most original-looking move—predicting sacrifice—will attract the strongest hostile response.

A network can have fixed priority structure without a separately owned goal. A hard-wired protection hierarchy can prospectively predict sacrifice.

Repair:

State explicitly:

> Predicting sacrifice is evidence for organized priority structure; it becomes evidence for higher-level goal ownership only when the higher-level target model adds prediction or intervention power beyond a comparably rich mechanistic priority model.

This prevents the tradeoff test from becoming a hidden sufficiency criterion.

P1 severity: HIGH.

# P6 — REGENERATION SECTION NEEDS A LEVIN-PROGRAM FIREWALL IN BODY PROSE

Repair:

Then preserve:

> **TARGET MORPHOLOGY IS AN EXPERIMENTAL HYPOTHESIS BEFORE IT IS AN ONTOLOGY.**

P1 severity: HIGH source/consensus integrity.

# P7 — “NO SINGLE CELL CONTAINS THE FINISHED ANATOMY” NEEDS CAREFUL MEANING

Use:

This also aligns with frozen Figure 8 doctrine.

P1 severity: MODERATE-HIGH.
