## Chapter 2 — The World Your Senses Built

### Endnote 2 — BIB-011 + BIB-012
Attach to the first sentence or sentence-cluster establishing that animals can access ultraviolet and/or polarized-light information unavailable to unaided human vision.

Supported burden: UV-sensitive photoreception and polarization-sensitive visual systems are documented biological sensory modalities.

Do not use this note to claim that any animal experiences UV or polarization phenomenologically as a human-style added color.

### Endnote 3 — BIB-013
Attach to the first sentence explaining pit-viper infrared sensing through facial pit organs.

Supported burden: pitvipers detect thermal radiation using specialized facial pit organs that form spatially informative thermal input.

Do not call this ordinary visible-light vision without qualification.

### Endnote 4 — BIB-014
Attach to the first sentence explaining bat echolocation as sensory access through emitted sound and returning echoes.

Supported burden: bats use echolocation to extract spatially useful information unavailable through human unaided audition alone.

### Endnote 5 — BIB-015
Attach to the first sentence introducing elasmobranch electroreception / ampullae of Lorenzini.

Supported burden: elasmobranchs possess specialized electroreceptors capable of detecting weak electric fields.

### Endnote 6 — BIB-016
Attach to the first sentence stating that birds can use geomagnetic information for orientation/navigation.

Supported burden: behavioral use of geomagnetic information is well supported.

Caution: receptor/transduction mechanism is not treated as fully settled by this citation.

## Chapter 5 — When Parts Become a Whole

### Endnote 14 — BIB-017

## Chapter 7 — The Body Electric

### Endnote 20 — BIB-019
Attach to the first passage discussing endogenous wound electric fields/current and directional cell migration during wound healing.

Supported burden: epithelial injury generates regulated endogenous electrical signals; physiologically relevant fields can guide migration of wound-healing cells.

Mandatory firewall: this is ordinary ion transport, membrane/tissue electrophysiology, and electrotaxis. It is not evidence for an aura, hidden life force, consciousness, or higher-order organismhood.

Endnote 19 remains OPEN for a broader independent membrane-physiology/bioelectricity authority beyond the developmental-bioelectricity literature.

## Chapter 9 — The Missing Limb

### Endnote 24 — BIB-020
Attach to the first passage explaining positional information/cues in salamander limb regeneration.

Supported burden: successful limb regeneration depends on positional information and tissue integration mechanisms.

### Endnote 25 — BIB-021
Attach to the first detailed passage describing blastema formation and progenitor-cell re-patterning.

Supported burden: salamander limb regeneration proceeds through blastema formation involving progenitor-like cells, proliferation, and repatterning.

### Endnote 26 — BIB-022
Attach to the broader claim that salamanders are a major vertebrate model system for regeneration and can restore complex structures including limbs.

Supported burden: salamanders exhibit extensive regenerative capacity and provide experimentally tractable models for mechanisms of tissue/organ restoration.

Mandatory brake for all three notes: restored anatomy does not by itself demonstrate a consciously represented target, a centralized controller, or goal awareness.

## Chapter 11 — Living Ahead of Now

### Endnote 30 — BIB-023
Attach to the first passage using food anticipation to show that organisms can prepare in advance of a recurring event through distributed oscillatory and memory-related mechanisms.

Supported burden: food-anticipatory behavior can involve distributed oscillators and memory-related mechanisms in body and brain.

### Endnote 31 — BIB-024
Attach to the passage distinguishing entrainment from simple immediate reaction.

Supported burden: scheduled food availability can entrain anticipatory activity through a food-entrained oscillator system separable from the canonical light-entrained SCN system.

### Endnote 32 — BIB-025 + PROJECT CRITERION
Attach to the passage where the manuscript moves from preparation to the stronger concept of biological anticipation/prediction.

Supported external burden: biological literature contains examples and proposed frameworks for anticipatory processes in organisms without complex cognition.

Project-only burden: **If a proposed prediction cannot be prospectively specified in a way that permits it to be wrong before the outcome, the project will not count it as strong evidence of prediction.**

That prospective-error rule is the Macroscopic Life project’s methodological criterion. It must not be attributed as a consensus definition from Deans 2021.
