## Freeze rationale

The prefreeze manuscript survives the final attack because it does not ask the reader to believe that forests reproduce. It builds an adversarial method for distinguishing higher-level reproduction from recurrence, propagation, regeneration, reconstruction, component lineage, ecological inheritance, and observer-imposed similarity.

Its central scientific move is level ownership: reproduction occurring inside a proposed whole is not automatically reproduction of that whole. At the same time, nested reproduction remains possible, so the chapter avoids the opposite error of assuming that component implementation rules out a higher-level reproductive description.

The chapter also survives the external-dependence problem. It no longer requires self-sufficiency, and it treats parental causal contribution as degree-sensitive. The copier/scaffolding example is explicitly framework-dependent rather than a dictionary argument.

The forest experiment remains a hostile hypothetical. Parent P and offspring O must be independently individuated, lineage evidence must be prospective rather than shopped after the result, and the higher-level account must compete against fair reconstruction/component rivals.

### Parent-Contribution Test

A Macroscopic Life project synthesis. It asks whether the hypothesized contribution of parent P changes offspring O formation in prospectively predicted ways after fair environmental, component, and historical rivals are preserved. It does not require P to be the sole, necessary, or sufficient cause.

### Founding package

A Macroscopic Life project scaffold for a prospectively hypothesized transmission structure causally generated through P. It cannot be defined after the result as whatever variables happen to predict O.

### PA_reproduction

`PA_reproduction = Performance(H) - Performance(B)`

H = higher-level parent–offspring model.
B = best fair component/environmental rival.

Positive PA demonstrates predictive value for the specified task, not reproduction by definition.
