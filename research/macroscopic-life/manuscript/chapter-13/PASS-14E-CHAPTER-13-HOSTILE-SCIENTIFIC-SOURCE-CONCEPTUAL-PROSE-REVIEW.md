## The 26 required repairs

### 1. Replace the seed's absolute opening formulation
Current prose says the seed does not contain the future tree as a miniature object. Keep the intuition, but avoid sounding like a claim against developmental preformation in an unnecessarily absolute/popularized way. Tighten to: the seed is not a miniature adult tree and does not contain most of the matter that will compose the adult.

### 2. “Tree reproduced” needs organism-level precision
The opening is scientifically ordinary, but specify that seed production is part of the tree's reproductive life cycle. This avoids implying every seed necessarily becomes an offspring individual.

### 3. Crystal paragraph needs a replication/reproduction brake
Crystal growth is a useful adversary, but do not imply that ordered templating has no relationship to replication literature. Say only that pattern extension does not by itself establish the parent–offspring reproduction claim tested here.

### 4. Flame paragraph risks conflating propagation and recurrence
The fire example is propagation, while hurricanes are recurrence/reconstruction. Make the distinction explicit so the chapter does not collapse its own categories.

### 5. Regeneration section needs framework qualification
The sentence implying lineage plus independent individuality cleanly resolves regeneration/reproduction is too strong. Pass 14B explicitly says borderline cases also depend on life history and individuation framework. Add this qualification.

### 6. “Lineage is a historical claim” survives; “history has to leave evidence” needs softening
Historical lineage can be inferred indirectly. Replace with “a strong contested lineage claim must be supported by evidence of causal history.” Do not imply every lineage leaves a unique observable marker.

### 7. Body example: “enormous amount of reproduction” is rhetorically loose
Cell division is not universally described as reproduction in every multicellular context. Replace with “enormous numbers of cell divisions and lower-level lineage events.” This prevents the chapter from using the word it is trying to discipline.

### 8. Social-insect section needs exact source metadata
Replace working note [1] with verified citations from 14B: Peeters & Ito 2001, DOI 10.1146/annurev.ento.46.1.601; Cronin et al. 2013, DOI 10.1146/annurev-ento-120811-153643. Keep species/life-history variation explicit.

### 9. Colony paragraph overstates “colony-level reproduction” as framework-neutral
Change to “can be analyzed as colony-level reproduction under relevant organismality/evolutionary frameworks.” Avoid implying consensus across all social-insect theory.

### 10. Factory/copier passage conflicts partially with 14B
This is the largest conceptual repair. 14B notes that Godfrey-Smith's broad reproduction framework can treat photocopying as scaffolded reproduction of tokens. The draft currently uses the copier too decisively to imply the page does not reproduce. Rewrite the section so the copier demonstrates framework dependence: a broad lineage account may call it scaffolded token reproduction, while this does not establish organismality or life. The book must not win by dictionary.

### 11. Verify Godfrey-Smith citation exactly
Replace working note [2] with: Peter Godfrey-Smith, “Reproduction, Symbiosis, and the Eukaryotic Cell,” PNAS 112 (2015): 10120–10125, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1421378112. Do not quote wording not verified as verbatim.

### 12. Promote graded causal contribution
The draft asks whether the proposed parent makes “the right kind” of causal contribution. Add that parental causal responsibility can be degree-sensitive and does not require P to be necessary or sufficient by itself. This is a direct 14B correction.

### 13. Hurricane sentence “atmosphere-ocean system can construct hurricanes repeatedly” is acceptable but anthropomorphic-adjacent
Use “can repeatedly generate the conditions/processes from which hurricanes form.” Avoid assigning construction agency to atmosphere-ocean dynamics.

### 14. Ecology paragraph requires exact source grounding
Working note [3] is currently a domain placeholder. Replace it with a small verified source set from the 14B ledger or a dedicated source verification pass. Do not freeze generic claims about succession, assembly, filtering, priority effects, and historical contingency without bibliographic anchors.

### 15. Ecological inheritance note must be exact
Replace note [4] with Odling-Smee et al., “Niche Construction Theory: A Practical Guide for Ecologists,” Quarterly Review of Biology 88 (2013): 4–28, DOI 10.1086/669266. Keep wording neutral regarding Extended Evolutionary Synthesis debates.

### 16. “Biological parents do not transmit only bodies or genes” is too broad
Some parents do not transmit “bodies” in a useful sense. Rewrite around inheritance channels and parent-conditioned environments rather than bodies-versus-genes rhetoric.

### 17. Burned forest: “Only some are reproduction” is slightly too categorical
Some listed alternatives can overlap with reproduction under particular frameworks. Change to “Those possibilities do not all make the same reproduction claim, and several require no forest-level reproduction at all.”

### 18. Second forest: parent-conditioned environmental effects crossing the original boundary needs caution
Keep as hypothetical candidate pathway, not an assumed real forest mechanism. Signal explicitly that the example is composite.

### 19. Lineage markers need anti-shopping firewall
The draft lists many possible markers. Add: markers must be specified before outcome and cannot be selected after the fact from whichever channel happens to connect P and O.

### 20. `LINEAGE MUST BE INFERRED...` duplicates `SIMILARITY DOES NOT ESTABLISH LINEAGE.`
Signature scarcity violation. Keep one typographically promoted. Recommendation: retain the shorter `SIMILARITY DOES NOT ESTABLISH LINEAGE.` as the signature; convert the longer line to ordinary prose.

### 21. Remove the Parent name remains potentially misleading
14B suggested Parent-Dependence Test as less literal. The draft explains the nuance well, but final prose should either rename it **Parent-Contribution Test** or explicitly label “Remove the Parent” as shorthand. Recommendation: **Parent-Contribution Test** is scientifically cleaner and avoids implying necessity.

### 22. Founding Package risks becoming a catch-all
The current brake is good but needs one additional exclusion: the package cannot be defined merely as “whatever variables predict O.” It must correspond to a hypothesized transmission structure causally generated by P.

### 23. PA model labels need consistent notation
The draft calls the fourth model “a fourth” and then uses a generic equation. Define H and B consistently with earlier chapters: H = higher-level parent–offspring model; B = best fair rival. Preserve four-part Prediction Advantage doctrine.

### 24. Model C sentence overbundles explanatory/predictive/intervention power
Not every valid lineage study will permit intervention. Revise to say the higher-level account must survive fair rival comparison using the strongest available evidence routes; intervention is especially strong where feasible, but historical/phylogenetic/provenance triangulation can substitute where manipulation is impossible.

### 25. Earth section: “Life has already left Earth aboard spacecraft” needs wording precision
Terrestrial organisms/material have been transported beyond Earth's surface, but avoid using this as a broad astrobiological claim. Rewrite narrowly and source it if retained. The scientific point does not depend on the flourish.

### 26. Working endnotes cannot survive prefreeze
Every placeholder/reconciliation note must be replaced by full verified bibliographic entries. No “will be locked,” “exact metadata will be reconciled,” or generic literature-domain notes may remain in 14F.
