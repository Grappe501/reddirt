## Frozen project-specific tool

### Selection Ownership Test

A Macroscopic Life synthesis:

> After fair component, composition, environmental, local-competition, migration, demographic, grouping, linked-trait, and post-hoc rivals are considered, does a prospectively specified heritable property of the proposed whole causally contribute, under specified conditions, to differential reproductive success of higher-level lineages?

### PA_selection

`PA_selection = Performance(H) - Performance(B)`

H = higher-level selection model.
B = strongest fair lower-level/contextual rival.

Positive PA demonstrates task-specific predictive advantage, not causal selection by definition.

## Frozen success-currency rule

> A quantity can be biologically important without being the reproductive currency of the level under test.

Biomass, persistence, stability, productivity, component offspring, and component propagules may all matter scientifically without automatically measuring reproductive success of the proposed higher-level individual.

## Frozen selection-of / selection-for firewall

A lineage carrying D can increase because a correlated property E is causally favored.

Therefore:

`selection of D-bearing lineages ≠ selection for D`

This firewall applies before adaptation is inferred.

## Frozen adaptation firewall

Current selection on a property does not by itself establish that the property originated, spread historically, or was maintained because of its currently observed effect.

`selection ≠ adaptation ≠ optimization ≠ progress ≠ purpose`

Higher-level selection, even if demonstrated, does not establish agency, intelligence, consciousness, or life under every definition.

## Frozen evolutionary-transition brake

Selection can change the distribution and fate of variation but is not the sole source of novelty. Evolutionary transitions may involve cooperation, conflict mediation, specialization, dependence, bottlenecks, policing, and other mechanisms. No one route or fitness-decoupling metric is universal.

## Frozen Earth brake

> One observed Earth is not a demonstrated population of planetary parent-offspring lineages.

Selection within Earth cannot silently become selection among Earths.

## Frozen closing bridge

> **If selection can act on the whole, can it build the whole?**
