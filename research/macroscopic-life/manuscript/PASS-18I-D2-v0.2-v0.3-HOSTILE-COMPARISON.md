## What v0.3 improved

### 1. Chapter 11 no longer rebuilds the forest from zero

The revised choice passage now returns to the same hypothetical forest and explicitly carries forward candidate constraint G from Chapter 10. This is exactly the cumulative architecture the book needs. The new version asks only the new burden: whether organization of the proposed whole contributes causally to which viable response trajectory occurs.

This is a genuine narrative improvement, not merely a word-count reduction.

### 2. Chapter 12 now treats the forest as an accumulated candidate

The individuality chapter now says, in effect, risk the same forest again and make boundary H compete with serious alternatives. That preserves continuity with earlier tests while moving directly to the individuality problem.

### 3. Prediction Advantage callbacks are becoming callbacks

The Chapter 11 and Chapter 12 edits stop reprinting the full four-part PA lesson and instead point back to the established discipline. This is scientifically legitimate because Chapter 5 already owns the full rule.

### 4. The no-new-physics disclaimer is shorter where it should be

The Chapter 11 higher-level causation passage now states the physical implementation once and immediately asks whether the higher-level variable remains scientifically productive across lower-level implementations. The argument advances instead of reopening the ontology lesson.

### 5. The late-book lineage sequence remains intact

Nothing in v0.3 weakens the staircase:

`individuality → reproduction → inheritance → selection → transition`

Chapter 16 still explicitly locks reproduction, heredity, selection, and transition together without claiming that success on one property proves the next.

## Remaining hostile findings

### P1 — Late-book method repetition is still visible

Severity: HIGH reader issue / LOW scientific issue.

By Chapters 13–16 the reader already knows the Macroscopic Life testing grammar. Yet the manuscript still repeatedly reintroduces combinations of prospective definition, fair rivals, held-out evaluation, causal testing, model outcomes, and the reminder that positive PA does not prove ownership.

These statements are individually correct. The problem is cumulative rhythm. The book occasionally sounds as though it distrusts the reader's memory.

Required repair: retain the property-specific equation, the new rival unique to the chapter, the ownership test, and the failure condition. Compress generic method recitals to the smallest sufficient callback.

### P1 — Model A/B/C sections remain too structurally repetitive late

Severity: HIGH reader issue / LOW scientific issue.

Chapters 15 and 16 still pause for explicit Model A, Model B, and Model C prose after the reader has encountered this architecture repeatedly. The property-specific outcomes matter and must remain, but the presentation can become more narrative.

Required repair: preserve all three outcomes and U/X availability, but compress repeated explanatory boilerplate. Model B's scientific value should remain unmistakable without repeating the full signature mechanically.

### P1 — Chapter 9 still partly resets the forest

Severity: MEDIUM-HIGH.

The anticipation chapter says `Now we can return to the forest` and then re-establishes a forest entering a drought period before building the new problem. This is readable in isolation, but whole-book reading should make the forest feel like the same experimental candidate carrying accumulated history.

Required repair: revise only the seam. Do not remove the drought experiment. Enter with the existing forest and state what new temporal burden is being imposed.

### P1 — Chapter 10 still contains a second methodological warm-up after Chapter 9

Severity: MEDIUM.

The marble and thermostat are scientifically useful and should remain. The later forest return, however, can more explicitly inherit the experimental history instead of sounding like another fresh hypothetical setup. The chapter should feel like the forest has now survived enough weaker questions to be forced into a tradeoff test.

Required repair: seam compression, not conceptual deletion.

### P2 — Negative-definition cadence remains dense

Severity: MEDIUM.

The manuscript's precision often comes from sentences of the form `X is not Y`, and many are excellent signatures. But clusters remain in which several adjacent paragraphs perform the same defensive function. In the late chapters, the live rival can often replace a catalog of every stronger property not yet earned.

Required repair: protect signatures and first definitions; compress repeated catalogs after the distinction is established.

### P2 — Earth sections are scientifically strong but can advance faster

Severity: MEDIUM.

`Earth Is Not a Population` in Chapter 15 and `No Staircase to Earth` in Chapter 16 are both valuable because they attack different property claims. They should remain. The hostile issue is not their existence; it is repeated generic language around what Earth does not inherit from systems inside it.

Required repair: Chapter 15 should own the missing planetary lineage/population problem. Chapter 16 should own the anti-staircase / transition problem. Remove any sentences that merely repeat the other's burden.

### P2 — Chapter 16's Transition Ownership / model machinery is denser than the ending needs

Severity: MEDIUM.

The final chapter has earned a mature reader. Its historical-versus-present-causal distinction is essential, as are S0→S1 and `PA_transition`. But after those are established, the following Model A/B/C section can be tightened so the chapter accelerates into `No Staircase to Earth` and the final aperture.

Required repair: preserve the historical reconstruction distinction, S0→S1, the transition-specific Model C definition, and reversibility. Remove repeated method explanations already supplied by the preceding paragraphs.
