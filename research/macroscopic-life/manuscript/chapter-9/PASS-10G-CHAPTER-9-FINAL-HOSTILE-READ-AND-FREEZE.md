## 2. Scientific anticipation terminology — PASS

The manuscript no longer protects Macroscopic Life by defining established anticipation out of existence. Circadian preparation and predictive regulation are allowed to count as legitimate anticipation in their scientific contexts.

The stronger Macroscopic Life question is explicitly higher-level ownership and flexible future-sensitive causal control.

## 3. Circadian precision — PASS

Circadian clocks are described as entrainable biological oscillators whose rhythms may persist under suitable constant conditions. The prose does not infer model-based forecasting from phase persistence.

The distinction is correctly stated:

> A clock can anticipate a recurring event without establishing a flexible forecast of that event.

## 4. Cue-decoupling test — PASS

`Make the cue lie.` remains an effective experimental turn.
