# Overall verdict

The Pass 10A architecture survives, but one important terminology correction is required.

Scientific literatures already use **anticipation**, **anticipatory regulation**, and **predictive behavior** for mechanisms substantially weaker than the project's proposed strongest category. Circadian systems are routinely described as anticipating recurring environmental changes; allostasis is explicitly framed as predictive or anticipatory regulation; microbial gene networks have been described as predictive/anticipatory; adaptive plasticity can use cues correlated with later selective environments.

Therefore Macroscopic Life must **not** claim that cue-based or clock-based preparation “is not anticipation” as a universal scientific definition.

Instead, the project must say:

> **CLOCKS, CUES, AND EVOLVED PROGRAMS CAN LEGITIMATELY SUPPORT ANTICIPATORY BEHAVIOR IN EXISTING SCIENTIFIC USAGE. THEY DO NOT BY THEMSELVES ESTABLISH THE STRONGER FUTURE-SENSITIVE CONTROL CLAIM MACROSCOPIC LIFE NEEDS FOR HIGHER-LEVEL OWNERSHIP.**

This is the Chapter 9 equivalent of the Chapter 8 hysteresis correction.

# 1. CIRCADIAN ENTRAINMENT — STRONG POSITIVE CONTROL, NOT A FALSE MEMORY-LIKE CATEGORY

Circadian literature treats endogenous clocks as timekeeping systems that coordinate physiology and behavior with predictable environmental cycles. Reviews describe circadian clocks as enabling organisms to anticipate recurring environmental changes. Plant circadian systems coordinate growth, molecular activity, and metabolism with daily and seasonal cycles, while mammalian clocks coordinate tissue-specific physiology and feeding-related rhythms.

Relevant sources:

- Dobson, C. M. (2014). *Dynamics and Timekeeping in Biological Systems*. Annual Review of Biochemistry 83:159–164. DOI 10.1146/annurev-biochem-013014-102724.
- *Regulation of Mammalian Physiology by Interconnected Circadian and Feeding Rhythms* (2017). Review literature describes circadian clocks as endogenous timekeeping systems that adapt physiology and behavior in an anticipatory fashion.
- *The Game of Timing: Circadian Rhythms Intersect with Changing Environments* (Annual Review of Plant Biology, 2023). Plant circadian clock coordinates multiscale temporal schedules with recurring environmental cycles.
- *Keeping in Sync with an Unpredictable Environment: Insights from Circadian Biology* (Annual Review of Genetics, 2026). Cross-taxonomic review explicitly describes temporal coupling as enabling anticipation of recurring environmental changes while emphasizing vulnerability when historical cue reliability changes.

Scientific consequence:

Pass 10A's line `A CLOCK CAN ARRIVE EARLY WITHOUT PREDICTING` is rhetorically useful but scientifically too categorical if “predicting” is being used in the broad circadian sense.

Safer project formulation:

> **A CLOCK CAN PREPARE FOR WHAT COMES NEXT WITHOUT ESTABLISHING A FLEXIBLE FORECAST OF WHAT COMES NEXT.**

The project's stronger test should therefore distinguish **entrained temporal anticipation** from **forecast-sensitive updating**, not declare the former scientifically non-anticipatory.

# 2. ALLOSTASIS — ANTICIPATORY REGULATION IS ESTABLISHED TERMINOLOGY

Allostasis literature explicitly frames regulation as predictive/feedforward and contrasts it with purely reactive error correction.

Key sources:

- Sterling, P. (2012). *Allostasis: a model of predictive regulation*. Physiology & Behavior 106(1):5–15. DOI 10.1016/j.physbeh.2011.06.004.
- Schulkin, J., & Sterling, P. (2019). *Allostasis: A Brain-Centered, Predictive Mode of Physiological Regulation*. Trends in Neurosciences 42(10):740–752. DOI 10.1016/j.tins.2019.07.010.
- Schulkin, J. (2011). *Social allostasis: anticipatory regulation of the internal milieu*. Frontiers in Evolutionary Neuroscience 2:111. DOI 10.3389/fnevo.2010.00111.
- *The neural basis of homeostatic and anticipatory thirst* (2017) reviews feedforward anticipatory responses associated with food intake, core temperature, circadian signals, and satiety before extracellular fluid state is fully corrected.

Grounded lesson:

Preparation before disturbance can be real anticipatory physiology without requiring conscious forecasting or a symbolic future representation.

Permanent Chapter 9 restraint:

> **ANTICIPATORY REGULATION DOES NOT BY ITSELF ESTABLISH A REPRESENTED FUTURE.**

This should replace any architecture language that treats “anticipation” as synonymous with model-based prediction.

# 3. FEEDFORWARD CONTROL — PRESENT CUES CAN LEGITIMATELY DRIVE ANTICIPATORY ACTION

Physiology recognizes feedforward control initiated by signals that precede the regulated disturbance. Recent thermal-physiology review literature explicitly describes feedforward control as anticipatory when cues about magnitude/timing of thermal challenge activate effectors before the challenge changes the regulated variable.

This supports Pass 10A's causal-direction doctrine:

`present cue/state → current action → later consequence`

The future need not causally act backward.

Approved brake:

> **THE FUTURE CAN BE THE TARGET OF CONTROL WITHOUT BEING THE SOURCE OF THE SIGNAL.**

Scientific status: strong as project synthesis, provided it is understood as ordinary forward causation rather than a universal philosophical theory of time.

# 4. MICROBIAL PREDICTIVE BEHAVIOR — IMPORTANT POSITIVE CASE AND IMPORTANT TERMINOLOGY WARNING

Tagkopoulos, Liu & Tavazoie (2008) is central.

- Tagkopoulos, I., Liu, Y.-C., & Tavazoie, S. (2008). *Predictive behavior within microbial genetic networks*. Science 320(5881):1313–1317. DOI 10.1126/science.1154456.

The study reported correlations in *E. coli* transcriptional responses corresponding to environmental covariation during transitions associated with the mammalian gastrointestinal tract and used evolutionary simulations to explore networks that capture environmental structure. The authors described this as predictive/anticipatory behavior and reported decoupling under novel environments as evidence relevant to associative adaptation.

Macroscopic Life must use this carefully.

What it supports:
- neural tissue is not required for scientific literature to discuss predictive/anticipatory biological behavior;
- evolutionary history can embed correlations between sequential environmental states into regulatory networks;
- changing environmental correlations can test whether regulatory coupling updates or decouples.

What it does **not** automatically establish:
- conscious prediction;
- explicit internal future representation;
- model-based planning;
- higher-level organism/ecosystem anticipation;
- general microbial “intelligence.”

This source strongly supports Pass 10A's evolutionary-counterfeit/ownership problem but requires softer terminology: evolutionary encoding of environmental regularities can itself be called predictive behavior in legitimate scientific usage.

# 5. EVOLUTIONARY PREPAREDNESS — ARCHITECTURE SURVIVES, WORDING MUST DISTINGUISH TIMESCALES

Phenotypic plasticity literature strongly supports the importance of cue reliability. Adaptive plastic responses can evolve when current/developmental cues predict later selective environments. When cue reliability changes, historically adaptive responses can become maladaptive.

Useful sources:

- *Phenotypic plasticity in response to climate change: the importance of cue variation* (2019). Emphasizes that adaptive plasticity depends on cues reliably predicting the selective environment and discusses predictive plasticity versus bet-hedging.
- Hill, K. A. P., Pfennig, K. S., & Pfennig, D. W. (2024). *Assessment and the regulation of adaptive phenotypic plasticity*. Development. DOI 10.1242/dev.203101.
- *Characterization, costs, cues and future perspectives of phenotypic plasticity* (2022). Reviews reliability of environmental cues, signal/noise problems, fitness consequences, and maladaptive responses when cues become unreliable.

Pass 10A candidate:

`PREPAREDNESS CAN BELONG TO EVOLUTION BEFORE PREDICTION BELONGS TO THE ORGANISM.`

Scientific verdict: conceptually strong but too binary if interpreted as denying organism-level predictive function to evolved mechanisms. Evolution can build mechanisms whose current operation is legitimately described as predictive or anticipatory.

Safer version:

> **EVOLUTION CAN BUILD PREPARATION FOR THE FUTURE WITHOUT THE CURRENT SYSTEM HAVING TO LEARN THE FUTURE DURING ITS OWN LIFETIME.**

The chapter should distinguish:
- evolutionary acquisition of cue-response structure;
- developmental/plastic assessment;
- within-lifetime updating;
- flexible forecast-sensitive control.

These are different causal timescales, not mutually exclusive categories.

# 6. CUE RELIABILITY IS THE CENTRAL EXPERIMENTAL LEVER

The plasticity and circadian literatures strongly support Pass 10A's cue-decoupling logic.

If a cue historically predicts a later selective environment, selection can favor a response to that cue. Environmental change can break the relationship and create mismatch.

Therefore one of Chapter 9's strongest experimental moves is:

> **CHANGE THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE CUE AND WHAT COMES NEXT.**

Possible manipulations remain scientifically defensible:
- false cues;
- omitted expected events;
- shifted timing;
- changed event probability;
- changed periodicity;
- cue combinations with altered reliability;
- predictable versus stochastic regimes.

But interpretation must distinguish **online updating** from **evolutionary change across generations**. A system that fails to update during one experiment may still possess a legitimate evolved anticipatory mechanism.

# 7. PREDICTIVE INFORMATION — DECODABILITY IS NOT CAUSAL USE

Bialek, Nemenman & Tishby formalized predictive information as mutual information between past observations and future observations.

- Bialek, W., Nemenman, I., & Tishby, N. (2001). *Predictability, complexity, and learning*. Neural Computation 13(11):2409–2463. DOI 10.1162/089976601753195969.

Palmer et al. measured predictive information in sensory populations:

- Palmer, S. E., Marre, O., Berry, M. J. II, & Bialek, W. (2015). *Predictive information in a sensory population*. PNAS 112(22):6908–6913. DOI 10.1073/pnas.1506855112.

This creates a crucial Macroscopic Life distinction.

A present state can contain measurable information about a future state. A scientist can decode that information. Neither fact proves that the system causally uses that information to choose current action.

Permanent brake:

> **PREDICTIVE INFORMATION CAN EXIST FOR THE OBSERVER BEFORE PREDICTIVE CONTROL EXISTS FOR THE SYSTEM.**

This is strongly aligned with Chapter 7's `INFORMATION CAN EXIST FOR THE SCIENTIST BEFORE IT EXISTS FOR THE SYSTEM.`

Operational requirement:
- identify predictive state;
- show it precedes action;
- perturb it;
- show current action changes in the predicted direction;
- show fair non-predictive rivals cannot explain the effect equally well.

# 8. MODEL PREDICTIVE CONTROL — ENGINEERED POSITIVE CONTROL

Model predictive control (MPC) provides a clean engineered example in which predicted future trajectories explicitly participate in current action selection. Contemporary reviews describe MPC as using models/data to predict input-output trajectories and optimize constrained closed-loop control.

Useful sources:

- Hewing, Kabzan & Zeilinger (2020). *Learning-Based Model Predictive Control: Toward Safe Learning in Control*. Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems 3:269–296. DOI 10.1146/annurev-control-090419-075625.
- *An Overview of Systems-Theoretic Guarantees in Data-Driven Model Predictive Control* (Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems, 2025). DOI 10.1146/annurev-control-030323-024328.

MPC supports the architecture's distinction between:
- effective control without explicit forecast modeling;
- control that explicitly evaluates predicted future trajectories.

However, engineered MPC is an analogy/positive control for mechanism, not evidence that biological/ecological systems implement MPC.

Permanent brake:

> **A MECHANISM CAN SHOW US WHAT FUTURE-SENSITIVE CONTROL WOULD LOOK LIKE WITHOUT SHOWING THAT NATURE USES THAT MECHANISM.**

# 9. PREDICTION ERROR — KEEP THE BRAKE

Prediction-error terminology is established in neuroscience and computational learning, but it spans different constructs and implementations. Human neuroimaging meta-analysis confirms widespread use of prediction-error constructs across incentive, perception, cognition, and action domains, while reviews of expectation/predictive coding treat prior expectations and incoming evidence computationally.

Relevant sources:

- Summerfield, C., & de Lange, F. P. (2014). *Expectation in perceptual decision making: neural and computational mechanisms*. Nature Reviews Neuroscience 15:745–756.
- Corlett, P. R., Mollick, J. A., & Kober, H. (2022). *Meta-analysis of human prediction error for incentives, perception, cognition, and action*. Neuropsychopharmacology 47:1339–1349.

Pass 10A brake survives strongly:

> **AN ERROR SIGNAL IS NOT A PREDICTION ERROR UNTIL THE PREDICTION IS INDEPENDENTLY IDENTIFIED.**

A deviation, novelty response, homeostatic error, mismatch, or surprise-like signal must not be renamed prediction error merely because it occurs after an unexpected event.

Macroscopic Life should avoid importing predictive-coding/free-energy vocabulary as proof of anticipation at unfamiliar scales.

# 10. REPRESENTATION — DO NOT MAKE IT A REQUIRED EARLY CLAIM

Existing literatures freely use words such as prediction, expectation, internal representation, and model, but these terms carry field-specific commitments.

Pass 10A was correct to prefer operational language first:
- predictive information;
- future-relevant state;
- forecast-sensitive state;
- future-sensitive control;
- flexible updating.

The line:

> **CORRELATION WITH THE FUTURE IS NOT REPRESENTATION OF THE FUTURE.**

survives.

A system can have a state statistically predictive of future input without that state qualifying as a representation under every philosophical theory of representation.

Do not make representation necessary for the chapter's lower or intermediate evidentiary levels.

# 11. REVISED PROJECT LADDER AFTER LITERATURE GROUNDING

Pass 10A ladder needs terminology repair because existing science calls clocks/cues anticipatory.

Recommended Macroscopic Life evidentiary ladder:

`Reactive Regulation → Cue-Anticipatory Regulation → Entrained Anticipation → Forecast-Sensitive Control → Flexible Model-Based Anticipation`

Explicitly PROJECT SYNTHESIS.

## Reactive Regulation
Response follows the regulated disturbance.

## Cue-Anticipatory Regulation
A present cue that reliably precedes a later challenge triggers preparation before the challenge changes the regulated variable. Legitimate anticipatory regulation in existing scientific usage.

## Entrained Anticipation
An endogenous oscillator/timekeeping process produces temporally appropriate preparation and may free-run when external forcing is temporarily removed. Legitimate anticipation in circadian usage; does not by itself establish flexible forecasting.

## Forecast-Sensitive Control
Current action changes with an estimate/distribution of later conditions and survives tests that decouple simple cue magnitude or fixed phase from future outcome.

## Flexible Model-Based Anticipation
Candidate predictive state updates when temporal/environmental relationships change and causally changes current action across novel combinations or altered regimes. Strongest project category; does not imply consciousness.

Important: this ladder orders evidence for the project's stronger claim, not scientific legitimacy of terminology.

# 15. FOREST EXPERIMENT — LITERATURE-GROUNDED CORRECTION

The Pass 10A forest experiment remains viable but must not set up “circadian/seasonal entrainment” as if it were fake anticipation. Instead it asks which kind and which level of anticipation is present.

Possible outcomes:

### Outcome A — Shared cues explain preparation
Individual organisms independently respond to photoperiod, temperature, soil moisture, or other present predictors. Forest-level pattern is aggregate cue-anticipatory regulation. Real biology; higher ownership not demonstrated.

### Outcome B — Entrained components explain preparation
Circadian/seasonal clocks produce preparation before recurring conditions. Legitimate biological anticipation; higher forest-level forecast ownership not demonstrated.

### Outcome C — Evolved fixed preparedness explains preparation
Responses remain tied to historically selected cue relationships and fail to update on the tested timescale when relationships change. Real adaptive history; stronger flexible forecast claim not demonstrated.

### Outcome D — Component updating explains preparation
Individual organisms or microbial subsystems update predictive relationships; forest-level observables follow their aggregation. Component anticipation real; whole ownership not demonstrated.

### Outcome E — Higher-level future-sensitive organization adds causal work
An independently justified forest-level state tracks later conditions, changes current system-level preparation, survives cue/phase/evolution/component rivals, is perturbable, generalizes across changed regimes, and adds Prediction/Intervention Advantage.

Only Outcome E supports the strongest Macroscopic Life higher-level anticipation claim.

# 16. PREDICTION ADVANTAGE — KEEP, BUT TARGET CONTROL AS WELL AS DECODING

`PA_anticipation = Performance(Future-Sensitive Model) - Performance(Best Fair Rival)`

Therefore strong evidence needs both:

**Prediction Advantage + Intervention/Control Advantage.**
