## Numbering doctrine

1. Endnote numbers are assigned by first appearance in the physical reader master.
2. Repeated sources may receive new endnote numbers when they support a distinct chapter-local claim; the bibliography ID remains stable.
3. `OPEN` means unresolved authority metadata or unresolved final claim placement. It is not permission to invent a source.
4. `PROJECT SYNTHESIS` is not assigned to an external authority.
5. Chapter 15 should cite the record rather than restart the literature review.
6. Chapter 16 should add minimal new empirical sourcing.
7. Final insertion into the physical manuscript waits until materialization is executed and exact sentence-level anchors are mapped.
