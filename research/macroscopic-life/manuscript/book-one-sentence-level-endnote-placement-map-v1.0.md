## Placement doctrine

1. Insert a note after the narrowest sentence or sentence cluster that creates the factual burden.
2. Do not attach a citation to a rhetorical question merely because the following paragraph is empirical.
3. Do not use one broad citation to support multiple mechanistically distinct claims unless the source actually does so.
4. `PROJECT SYNTHESIS` notes identify author synthesis, not external authority.
5. `OPEN` slots remain uninserted until authority resolution is complete.
6. If the materialized master differs from the anchor phrase below, locate the semantically equivalent sentence and record the exact final wording before insertion.
