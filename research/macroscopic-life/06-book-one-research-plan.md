# Macroscopic Life — Book One Research Plan

## Answer: How Many Research Passes Are Needed?

For Book One, the recommended build is **30 research passes before final manuscript lock**.

That is the right balance between speed and credibility.

The larger 40-pass queue remains the full research program, but Book One does not need every deep technical pass before drafting. The first book needs enough evidence to support the six-act progression, enough adversarial review to avoid pseudoscientific overreach, and enough synthesis to make the final hypothesis intellectually coherent.

### Tier D — 2 Synthesis Passes
These convert research into a book rather than a pile of notes.

29. Chapter-by-chapter evidence packet synthesis
30. Full-manuscript scientific integrity and claim-classification pass

## Total

**30 Book One research passes.**

This is not 30 years of research and it is not 30 giant academic monographs. A pass is a defined research sprint that answers a focused question, captures high-quality sources, updates the evidence ledger, records counterarguments, and feeds the relevant chapters.

## What We Do Not Need Before Book One

Book One does not require complete mathematical modeling, original experiments, full simulation infrastructure, expert interviews across every field, or a comprehensive test of the Macroscopic Life hypothesis.

Those belong to the larger project.

Book One needs to accomplish a different task:

**Make the question intellectually unavoidable while remaining honest about what has and has not been demonstrated.**

## Research Pass Completion Standard

A pass is complete only when it has:

1. a research note or evidence packet;
2. primary or high-quality review sources wherever available;
3. claims mapped into `03-evidence-ledger.md`;
4. strongest supporting interpretation;
5. strongest skeptical interpretation;
6. chapter implications;
7. unresolved questions;
8. explicit boundary between fact, analogy, hypothesis, and metaphysics.

## Recommended Pace of Construction

Do not execute all 30 passes before writing prose.

Use the cycle:

**Research cluster → evidence update → chapter architecture → draft → red-team → revise.**

This preserves energy and allows the book itself to reveal what additional research is actually needed.

## Book One Research Milestone Bar

- Passes 1–4: **Perception foundation complete**
- Passes 5–8: **Signal and scale foundation complete**
- Passes 9–14: **Emergence foundation complete**
- Passes 15–18: **Information/intelligence foundation complete**
- Passes 19–24: **Physics/philosophy boundary complete**
- Passes 25–28: **Adversarial integrity complete**
- Passes 29–30: **Manuscript evidence lock complete**

At Pass 18, the majority of the book can already be in active draft.

At Pass 24, all six acts can be drafted.

At Pass 30, Book One can be locked for publication-level editing.
