## Chapter 7

## Chapter 8

Distributed local interactions can generate reproducible large-scale anatomical form without any component containing a complete explicit representation of the finished body.

Self-organization does not establish life, intelligence, agency, consciousness, or a universal morphogenetic field.

## Chapter 9

After perturbation, biological systems can in some cases restore characteristic organization through distributed, physically instantiated regulatory processes. Perturbation can reveal what is actively maintained, but restoration alone does not establish an explicit target representation, consciousness, or higher-order individuality.
