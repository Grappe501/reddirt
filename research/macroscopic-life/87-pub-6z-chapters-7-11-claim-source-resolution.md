# CHAPTER 7 — THE BODY ELECTRIC

## Claim family 7.1 — Cells maintain membrane potentials through ion gradients and selective permeability

### Claim

Living cells maintain measurable electrical potentials across membranes because ions are distributed unequally and membranes contain selective ion pathways.

### Source class

**A — textbook / authoritative cell-physiology foundation**

### Burden

LOW. This is settled physiology.

### Production rule

Use a standard cell-physiology or neurophysiology source for the basic membrane-potential mechanism. Do not rely on developmental-bioelectricity papers for elementary electrochemistry.

### Firewall

> **ELECTRICITY IN THE BODY IS ORDINARY PHYSICS ORGANIZED BY LIVING SYSTEMS.**

---

## Claim family 7.2 — Bioelectric states participate in development and regeneration outside classical excitable tissues

### Claim

Membrane potential and ion-channel/gap-junction state can influence proliferation, migration, differentiation, patterning, and regenerative outcomes in non-neural/non-muscle tissues.

### Strong review anchors

- Review: **Bioelectric signaling as a unique regulator of development and regeneration** — supports the existence of developmental/regenerative roles for membrane voltage and emphasizes that mechanistic interpretation remains an active area of research. citeturn549572search0
- Review: **Mechanisms Underlying Influence of Bioelectricity in Development** — surveys ion-channel and signaling mechanisms by which bioelectric state can alter developmental processes. citeturn549572search7
- Review: **Bioelectric Signaling in Regeneration: Mechanisms of Ionic Controls of Growth and Form** — supports the role of bioelectric signaling in regenerative patterning. citeturn549572search6

### Production rule

Final endnotes should pair one broad review with representative primary experiments for the specific example being described.

### Firewall

These papers establish bioelectric signaling as a physiological regulatory layer.

They do **not** establish:

- consciousness in tissues;
- a mystical energy field;
- organismhood at another scale;
- a universal morphogenetic field;
- a nonphysical life force.

> **BIOELECTRICITY IS ORDINARY BIOPHYSICS USED BY LIVING SYSTEMS.**

### Claim

Electrical state can propagate or coordinate across multicellular tissues through mechanisms including ion channels, gap junctions, voltage-sensitive signaling, and downstream biochemical pathways.

### Source anchors

- **Bioelectrical controls of morphogenesis: from ancient mechanisms of cell coordination to biomedical opportunities** — broad tissue-scale coordination review. citeturn549572search1
- **Reprogramming cells and tissue patterning via bioelectrical pathways** — mechanism-oriented review of ion-channel/gap-junction pathways and downstream consequences. citeturn549572search11

It does not prove higher-order cognition.

## Claim family 7.4 — Frequency and resonance

### Claim

Biological systems can contain oscillatory electrical, chemical, and mechanical processes. Frequency is a measurable parameter; resonance requires a defined oscillator, coupling mechanism, forcing condition, predicted response, and reproducible measurement.

### Source architecture

Use standard physics / biophysics references for resonance rather than papers making speculative biological resonance claims.

### Firewall

> **FREQUENCY IS A MEASUREMENT, NOT AN EXPLANATION.**

> **A SHARED WORD IS NOT A SHARED MECHANISM.**

No paper should be cited merely because it uses the word `frequency` or `resonance` in a biological context.

# CHAPTER 10 — MEMORY WITHOUT A BRAIN

The chapter's central source problem is definitional.

`Memory` occurs in many literatures with different mechanisms and meanings. The book therefore must source each example at its own mechanistic level rather than citing a broad `memory in biology` review and treating all examples as one process.

---

## Claim family 10.1 — Functional memory requires a persistent physical state that can alter later response

### Claim

For memory language to do causal work in this book, something physically instantiated must persist and later re-enter processing in a way that changes a subsequent response.

### Source architecture

This is partly an operational definition proposed by the book. It should not be falsely attributed as a universal consensus definition.

Anchor it with concrete examples from epigenetic memory, trained immunity, and stress priming.

### Firewall

> **TRACE ≠ STORAGE ≠ FUNCTIONAL MEMORY ≠ CONSCIOUS RECOLLECTION.**

> **THE PAST MATTERS LATER ONLY IF SOMETHING PHYSICAL PERSISTS AND CAN RE-ENTER CAUSAL PROCESSING.**

# ACT III CROSS-CHAPTER SOURCE RISKS

## Risk 1 — One-lab concentration in developmental bioelectricity

A substantial portion of the conceptual vocabulary around bioelectric pattern memory, target morphology, and physiological software is associated with Michael Levin and collaborators.

That work is important and relevant, but the book should not build an entire broad theory of morphogenesis from one research program.

### Rule

For Chapters 7–9:

- use Levin-lab and allied bioelectric work where the experiment specifically concerns bioelectric manipulation;
- pair with independent developmental-biology, mechanics, regeneration, positional-information, and systems-biology literature for broader claims;
- clearly separate experimental result from theoretical interpretation.

---

## Risk 2 — Anthropomorphic terminology embedded in source papers

Terms such as:

- goal state;
- target morphology;
- decision;
- memory;
- computation;
- error correction;

can appear in legitimate scientific papers while carrying stronger ordinary-language connotations.

### Rule

A source author's use of anthropomorphic or computational vocabulary does not automatically license the strongest everyday meaning in Book One.

The manuscript must translate the claim into measurable operations.

---

## Risk 3 — Cross-domain use of `memory`

Neural memory, epigenetic persistence, immune training, stress priming, regenerative pattern persistence, and institutional archives have different substrates and mechanisms.

### Rule

Do not use one citation family to validate another.

> **THE WORD `MEMORY` DOES NOT CREATE A COMMON SUBSTRATE.**

---

## Risk 4 — Cross-domain use of `anticipation`

Circadian entrainment, conditioned responses, feedforward physiology, learned prediction, explicit forecasts, and conscious foresight are distinct.

### Rule

Always state the operational class being discussed.

---

## Risk 5 — Teleology by grammar

Phrases like `the tissue knows`, `the cells know what is missing`, `the body wants`, and `the system predicts` can outrun the evidence.

### Rule

Use mechanism-first alternatives unless the anthropomorphic phrase is explicitly presented as shorthand and immediately unpacked.
