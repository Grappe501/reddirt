## P1-07 — Integration worked example

**Suggested location:** Chapter 5, after Macroscopic Organization middle category.

A network can be densely connected without being deeply integrated.

Imagine two communication systems with the same number of nodes and the same number of links. In the first, most messages are redundant and routes can be removed with little change in system-level performance. In the second, particular relationships are necessary for combining information across regions, and disrupting them sharply reduces the system's ability to coordinate.

Both systems are connected. Only the second has demonstrated stronger evidence of integration under the specified task.

> **CONNECTED IS NOT INTEGRATED. INTEGRATION HAS TO SURVIVE PERTURBATION AS A CAUSAL CLAIM.**

The same rule applies upward. Network density, synchronized timing, communication volume, or correlation may tell us where to look. They do not by themselves cross the barrier from organization to integration.

## P1-05 — Operational agency definition

**Suggested location:** Chapter 12, after intelligence definition and before consciousness boundary.

### Agency Is Another Loaded Word

For this project, **operational agency** means a system persistently regulates or selects actions relative to defined state variables, constraints, or goals in a way that can be tested against alternatives.

That is deliberately weaker than conscious intentional agency.

A thermostat can regulate a target variable without having intentions. An organism can display adaptive goal-directed behavior without our having settled every question about consciousness. A human can act intentionally and consciously.

These are not the same evidentiary claim.

> **GOAL-DIRECTED REGULATION ≠ CONSCIOUS INTENTION.**

If we ever attribute agency to a candidate macroscopic whole, we must specify the state variables, action space, persistence, feedback, and competing lower-level explanation before using the word as evidence.

## P1-04 — Information specificity

**Suggested location:** fold into Chapter 12 `Information Is Not One Thing`.

When this book uses the word *information*, ask which sense is meant.

- **Statistical information:** variables covary in a measurable way.
- **Stored state:** a physical configuration persists and can affect later processing.
- **Functionally available information:** a system can use a state difference to change performance or action.
- **Semantic information:** a representation has content for an interpreting system or practice.
- **Whole-Only Information:** the formal test case in which relevant information is available only in relationships among components under the stated comparison.

These meanings can overlap. They should not be collapsed merely because English uses one word for all of them.

> **INFORMATION IS NOT A SUBSTANCE. NAME THE RELATIONSHIP OR FUNCTION YOU MEAN.**

## P1-03 — Prediction burden

**Suggested location:** Chapter 11 callback and Chapter 14 Test 6.

A higher-level system does not earn Prediction Advantage because we can look backward and find an impressive pattern.

For every prediction claim, specify four things in advance:

1. the **predictor** — the physical subsystem, model, or measured macro-state making the forecast;
2. the **target** — the future variable to be predicted;
3. the **error measure** — how success and failure will be scored;
4. the **baseline** — the strongest lower-level, null, or simpler model the higher-level predictor must beat.

> **NO PROSPECTIVE TARGET + NO ERROR METRIC + NO BASELINE = NO PREDICTION ADVANTAGE.**

This rule prevents hindsight from masquerading as foresight.

## P1-06 + P1-08 — Test dependence doctrine and worked example

**Suggested location:** Chapter 14 immediately before the Eleven Tests; mirror on `/tests` and `/method`.

### Eleven Questions, Not Eleven Votes

The Eleven Tests ask different questions, but a single experiment can inform more than one test.

Suppose we identify a proposed civilization-scale communication structure and perturb a major integrating pathway. After the perturbation, coordinated performance declines. A macro-state model also loses predictive advantage, and an intervention defined at the macro level no longer produces the same downstream effect.

That one experiment may inform:

- **Integration Ablation** — because disrupting organization reduced whole-scale capability;
- **Prediction Advantage** — because the macro predictor lost prospective performance;
- **Higher-Level Intervention** — because the intervention-response relation changed.

But it is still substantially **one evidence unit**, generated by one perturbation on one system under one experimental context.

We do not count it as three independent confirmations simply because it touches three questions.

> **TESTS ARE QUESTIONS. EVIDENCE UNITS ARE OBSERVATIONS. DEPENDENCE MUST BE DISCLOSED.**

The stronger Model D case requires diagnostic evidence from sufficiently different perturbations, timescales, substrates, datasets, and mechanism families—not a large score produced by reusing the same observation.

## P1-09 — Nonmystical macro-causation worked example

**Suggested location:** Chapter 14 Test 9 / method site.

### Higher-Level Intervention Does Not Mean a Force From Above

Consider room temperature in a building.

`Room temperature` is a macro-variable produced by enormous numbers of molecular interactions. Yet a thermostat can measure that macro-variable and use it to control a furnace. Setting the thermostat from 68°F to 72°F can produce reproducible downstream changes in fuel use, air temperature, component activity, and heat flow.

Nothing supernatural descended from the level of `room temperature` into the molecules.

The macro-variable earned practical causal usefulness because a real intervention defined at that level participates in a mechanism connecting measurement, control, and lower-level physical change.

Macroscopic Life asks an analogous but much harder question:

> **ARE THERE CANDIDATE WHOLE-LEVEL VARIABLES WHOSE INTERVENTION RELATIONS ADD REPRODUCIBLE EXPLANATORY OR PREDICTIVE POWER BEYOND STRONG LOWER-LEVEL ALTERNATIVES?**

A successful answer would establish higher-level causal usefulness under the model. It would not prove a new force, consciousness, or God.

Civilization clearly contains maintenance, emergency response, replacement, reconstruction, error correction, and institutional recovery mechanisms.

That is not yet the same claim as autonomous repair by civilization as one individual.

> **MAINTENANCE INSIDE A SYSTEM ≠ REPAIR AUTONOMY OF THE SYSTEM AS A WHOLE.**

Current evidence reviewed here does not establish that stronger result.

## P1-10 — Elevate Model C

**Suggested location:** Chapter 14 verdict and Chapter 15 opening/close.

Model C is not the place the theory goes when Model D fails.

It is a substantive scientific possibility:

> **REAL MACROSCOPIC ORGANIZATION CAN EXIST WITHOUT A NEW HIGHER-ORDER INDIVIDUAL.**

Under Model C, civilization can contain vast distributed memory, computation, sensing, institutions, networks, transport, energy systems, adaptive responses, and collective problem solving while remaining an organized ecology of organisms and artifacts rather than one new organism.

That result would still change how we study civilization. It would tell us that macroscopic organization is scientifically real and causally useful while establishing where the evidence barrier to individuality has *not* been crossed.

> **MODEL C IS A DISCOVERY, NOT A CONSOLATION PRIZE.**
