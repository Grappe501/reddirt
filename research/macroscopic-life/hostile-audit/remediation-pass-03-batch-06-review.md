## Hostile review

### Notes 30–32 — anticipation is not foresight

The manuscript already carries the decisive brake: `PREPARATION ≠ PREDICTION ≠ FORESIGHT ≠ PROPHECY`. The circadian and food-entrainment authorities support biological timing, oscillation, entrainment, and anticipatory preparation. They do not establish conscious knowledge of future events.

Note 32 is deliberately split between external authority and **PROJECT-CRITERION**. The literature can motivate anticipation/prediction distinctions; the project's requirement for a named predictor, prospective target, error measure, and strongest fair baseline is Macroscopic Life's methodological burden. It must not be laundered into the source as though the source authored the Eleven Test criterion.

### Note 33 — collective intelligence does not establish one agent

Kameda et al. supports information aggregation and collective performance. The selected anchor is intentionally weaker than `THE WHOLE CAN SOLVE A PROBLEM NO COMPONENT SOLVES ALONE` because that slogan can be screenshot as an individuality claim if detached from its adjacent brake. The approved factual host establishes interaction-dependent coherent performance while preserving architectural plurality.

### Note 34 — distributed cognition without automatic constitutive extension

Hutchins supports the importance of socially and materially distributed cognitive work. The manuscript explicitly leaves open the stronger philosophical question of whether external resources are constitutive parts of cognition. This prevents a citation to distributed cognition from becoming a shortcut to a civilization-scale cognitive individual.

### Note 35 — information is relational, not substance

The PID authority is used only to support the legitimacy of information carried by relationships among variables/components and the need to distinguish informational contributions. It does not establish Macroscopic Life's **Whole-Only Information** test, higher-order individuality, semantic content, agency, or consciousness. The project's out-of-sample and perturbational test remains project synthesis.
