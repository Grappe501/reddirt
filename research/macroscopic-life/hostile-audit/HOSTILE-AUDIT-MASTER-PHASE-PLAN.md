# Macroscopic Life — Overall Hostile Audit Master Plan

## Status

**PRE-FREEZE RED TEAM — 8 PHASES TOTAL**

No publication freeze should occur until all eight phases are complete, all P0 findings are zero, all P1 findings are either repaired or explicitly accepted with a written rationale, and the final scoring pass re-establishes defensibility, cohesion, educational effectiveness, ancestry confidence, source redundancy, and pseudoscience resistance.

## Why this audit exists

PUB-9C strengthened Book One by adding the four-stage evidentiary ladder, an earlier null model, a stronger model-selection rule, a consciousness boundary, and a research-program framing. Those improvements also created new interfaces where the argument could accidentally overclaim, duplicate evidence, blur categories, or attach ancestry to a stronger claim than the source supports.

The hostile audit therefore assumes **Macroscopic Life is wrong** and tries to identify the strongest reasons it might fail.

## Severity

- **P0 — publication blocker:** scientifically false, materially unsupported, logically circular, unfalsifiable, or likely to invert the project's stated conclusion.
- **P1 — major weakness:** defensibility, ambiguity, causal ownership, evidence dependence, ancestry, or reader-misinterpretation problem that materially weakens the work.
- **P2 — refinement:** wording, pedagogy, redundancy, sourcing depth, or precision issue that should be improved but does not invalidate the framework.
- **PASS:** survives the hostile test as written.

## Eight phases

### Phase 1 of 8 — Causal Ownership + Level Attribution

Attack every statement that says or implies a whole **senses, remembers, predicts, repairs, regulates, acts, learns, decides, computes, communicates, reproduces, or intervenes**.

For each claim ask:

1. Which physical components perform the mechanism?
2. At what level is the measured variable defined?
3. Does the capability belong to a component, subsystem, distributed relation, or proposed whole?
4. Would removing the macro label leave the explanation unchanged?
5. What intervention could distinguish genuine macro-level causal ownership from convenient aggregation?

Deliverable: ownership taxonomy, risky-claim register, repair rules, Phase-1 score.

### Phase 2 of 8 — Test Independence + Evidence Dependence

Attack the Eleven Tests for double counting. Determine where Boundary Perturbation, Integration Ablation, Prediction Advantage, Whole-Only Information, Higher-Level Intervention, Memory Turnover, Repair Autonomy, and Model Competition may rely on the same underlying evidence.

Deliverable: dependence matrix, conditional interpretation rules, minimum independent evidence families.

### Phase 3 of 8 — Category Leakage + Semantic Discipline

Search every use of:

**pattern / organization / integration / individual / organism / life / signal / information / memory / intelligence / agency / consciousness / prediction / anticipation / resonance / frequency / field / boundary / repair / reproduction.**

Attack any sentence where one category silently becomes another.

Deliverable: category dictionary, leakage register, canonical replacement rules.

### Phase 4 of 8 — Ancestry + Originality Hostile Prior-Art Review

Try to destroy the project's originality claims by locating the strongest prior art for:

- Pattern → Organization → Integration → Higher-Order Individuality;
- Macroscopic Organization as a middle category;
- Boundary Perturbation;
- Memory Turnover;
- Prediction Advantage;
- Whole-Only Information;
- Higher-Level Intervention;
- the Eleven-Test framework as a whole;
- Macroscopic Life as a falsifiable research program.

Deliverable: ancestry map, originality confidence bands, claims we may and may not make.

### Phase 5 of 8 — Domain-Expert Red Team

Read the project as eight hostile specialists:

1. evolutionary biologist;
2. philosopher of biology;
3. systems/network scientist;
4. information theorist/mathematician;
5. neuroscientist/consciousness researcher;
6. physicist/thermodynamics researcher;
7. archaeologist/cultural-evolution researcher;
8. skeptical scientific editor.

Deliverable: strongest objection from each field and project response/remediation.

### Phase 6 of 8 — Speculation, Resonance, Consciousness + Pseudoscience Firewall

Attack the areas most vulnerable to mystical or supernatural reinterpretation:

- resonance/frequency;
- biological fields/bioelectricity;
- consciousness above the human scale;
- epistemic asymmetry;
- higher-level intervention and God;
- convergence / pyramids / proposed civilizational generative recurrence;
- cosmic-scale analogies.

Deliverable: screenshot-safe doctrine, explicit non-claims, testable formulations, removal of any unfalsifiable wording.

### Phase 7 of 8 — Educational + Reader-Misinterpretation Audit

Simulate first-time general readers, a highly intelligent 10th-grade reader, scientifically trained readers, hostile social-media readers, and readers seeking confirmation of mystical beliefs.

Attack:

- cognitive jumps;
- concepts that are too simple or repetitive;
- concepts that are too compressed;
- emotional implication stronger than evidentiary conclusion;
- screenshot vulnerability;
- places where the brakes arrive too late.

Deliverable: educational score, reread score, misconception map, minimal surgical fixes.

### Phase 8 of 8 — Final Synthesis + Release Requalification

Recalculate the project's publication scores after all repairs:

- scientific defensibility;
- internal cohesion;
- educational effectiveness;
- ancestral confidence;
- research/source redundancy;
- pseudoscience resistance;
- originality/synthesis defensibility;
- publication readiness.

Required final state:

- P0 = **0**;
- unresolved P1 = **0**, unless explicitly accepted and documented;
- all revised scientific sentences sourced or correctly labeled synthesis/method/speculation;
- Model C remains the evidence-supported civilization verdict unless evidence genuinely changes;
- Eleven Tests remain falsifiable and Test 11 Model Competition remains mandatory;
- consciousness stays downstream of individuality;
- resonance remains a measurable coordination hypothesis, never a universal explanation;
- no figure redesign triggered by asset recovery problems;
- revised scholarly apparatus rerun after any prose change.

## Scoring targets

These are goals, not predetermined verdicts:

- Scientific defensibility: **≥96%**
- Internal cohesion: **≥97%**
- Educational effectiveness: **≥96%**
- Ancestral confidence: **≥95%**
- Backup research/source redundancy: **≥95%**
- Pseudoscience resistance: **≥98%**
- Original synthesis defensibility: **≥92%**
- Intellectual publication readiness: **≥96%**

The audit may lower these targets if the evidence justifies doing so. Scores must follow findings, not protect the project.

## Freeze rule

**PUB-9C-R16 is held until Hostile Audit Phase 8 closes.**

The audit is allowed to narrow claims, relabel claims as speculation, add sourcing, or remove weak material. It is not allowed to strengthen a preferred conclusion merely to improve a score.
