# Macroscopic Life — Hostile Audit

## Phase 4 of 8 — Ancestry + Originality Prior-Art Attack

**Status: COMPLETE — ORIGINALITY CLAIMS NARROWED, STRENGTHENED, AND MADE MORE DEFENSIBLE**

## Mission

Assume every apparently original Macroscopic Life contribution has already been proposed somewhere. Search aggressively for the strongest prior art, not weak similarities. Reduce or abandon originality claims whenever prior work already occupies the territory.

This phase distinguishes:

- **ESTABLISHED ANCESTRY** — the idea is already well represented in existing scholarship;
- **KNOWN COMPONENT / NEW USE** — the ingredient is established, but Macroscopic Life uses it in a distinct architecture;
- **DISTINCTIVE SYNTHESIS** — familiar elements are assembled into a materially new conceptual framework;
- **CANDIDATE ORIGINAL METHOD** — no close prior framework was identified in this audit, but priority is not claimed without systematic literature review;
- **NOT ORIGINAL** — broad idea clearly has significant precedent.

No priority claim in this file should be read as proof of first publication.

# 1. EVOLUTIONARY INDIVIDUALITY

Prior art is strong.

Major-transitions research explicitly studies how formerly independent entities become parts of a new higher-level individual. Existing work identifies interdependence, conflict reduction, division of labor, communication, reproduction, heredity, and shifts in the level of selection as central features.

Recent classification work also distinguishes physiological and evolutionary dimensions of biological organization and explicitly treats biological units as occupying multiple forms rather than one binary organism/non-organism category.

### Verdict

- `Individuality can evolve` — **NOT ORIGINAL**
- `Cooperation is not enough` — **NOT ORIGINAL**
- `Conflict suppression matters` — **NOT ORIGINAL**
- Using this ancestry as the calibration layer for civilization-scale tests — **KNOWN COMPONENT / NEW USE**

### Revised originality score

**10 / 100** for the biological proposition itself.

**50 / 100** for its role inside the Macroscopic Life testing architecture.
