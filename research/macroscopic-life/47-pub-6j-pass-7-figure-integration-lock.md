# Global figure rules — locked

## 1. Three-part integration

Every figure receives:

1. a lead-in sentence or short paragraph;
2. the figure;
3. a post-figure interpretation.

The reader should never encounter a full-page figure with no explanation of why it appears there.

## 2. No prose duplication

If a figure clearly presents a comparison, matrix, hierarchy, or timescale panorama, prose should not immediately enumerate every item again.

## 3. Caption function

Captions must answer:

- What is shown?
- What narrow scientific point does it support?
- What tempting overinterpretation does it not support, when needed?

## 4. Screenshot safety

Every figure must remain scientifically defensible when detached from the surrounding page.

Therefore critical brakes belong in the figure/caption itself when a cropped image could otherwise imply:

- hidden energies;
- literal subjective sensory experience;
- universal evolutionary ladders;
- organismhood from cooperation;
- aura/biofield claims;
- fuzzy boundary = no boundary.

## 5. Alt text

Alt text describes information structure, not visual mood.

Do not write `beautiful blue scientific illustration...`.

Describe the scientific relationships a reader would lose by not seeing the figure.
