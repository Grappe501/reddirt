# Macroscopic Life — Evidence Ledger

This ledger prevents the project from blending established science, analogy, speculation, and metaphysics into a single evidentiary category.

## Evidence Classes

### E1 — Established empirical finding
Replicated or broadly accepted scientific observation supported by strong evidence.

### E2 — Active scientific question
A legitimate research area with unresolved interpretation, mechanism, or scope.

### E3 — Cross-disciplinary analogy
A structural or functional comparison useful for generating questions but not sufficient to establish identity or causation.

### E4 — Speculative hypothesis
A proposed explanation or possibility that goes beyond available evidence but can be stated coherently and, ideally, connected to potential tests.

### E5 — Philosophical thought experiment
A conceptual device for exposing assumptions or exploring consequences.

### E6 — Cultural/metaphysical claim
A belief, tradition, spiritual interpretation, or non-empirical claim. It may be studied historically or phenomenologically without being represented as established physical fact.

## Seed Claims

### ML-001
**Claim:** Different species inhabit substantially different sensory worlds because their sensory systems detect different physical information.

Evidence class: **E1**

Intro-book use: foundational.

### ML-002
**Claim:** Unaided human senses reveal only a subset of physically measurable phenomena.

Evidence class: **E1**

Intro-book use: foundational.

### ML-003
**Claim:** Human-scale intuition becomes unreliable in important domains such as quantum mechanics.

Evidence class: **E1/E2**, depending on the specific interpretation attached.

Intro-book use: epistemic humility only.

### ML-004
**Claim:** Living organisms use electrical potentials and electrical signaling as integral parts of biological function.

Evidence class: **E1**

Intro-book use: foundational bridge to bioelectricity.

### ML-005
**Claim:** Resonance and synchronization can strongly shape coupled physical and biological systems.

Evidence class: **E1**, when tied to specific demonstrated mechanisms.

Required caveat: do not generalize from demonstrated resonance to unspecified "energies" or consciousness claims.

### ML-006
**Claim:** Human beings possess a single scientifically established overall "frequency" that determines health, consciousness, or spiritual state.

Evidence class: **Not established.** Treat popular versions as E6 or unsupported unless a precise measurable variable is specified.

### ML-007
**Claim:** Auras as commonly described in spiritual traditions correspond to a single scientifically verified field around the body.

Evidence class: **Not established; E6** unless a specific measurable claim is independently demonstrated.

Research note: separately catalog real thermal, electromagnetic, biochemical, acoustic, and electrostatic emissions from organisms.

### ML-008
**Claim:** Complex systems can exhibit emergent system-level behavior not attributable to any one component acting alone.

Evidence class: **E1/E2**, depending on the system and definition.

Intro-book use: foundational.

### ML-009
**Claim:** Human civilization can be usefully compared with an organism in terms of flows, specialization, signaling, regulation, and resource processing.

Evidence class: **E3**

Required caveat: usefulness of analogy does not establish that civilization is biologically alive.

### ML-010
**Claim:** Humanity is literally a cellular component of a larger living organism.

Evidence class: **E4**

Status: open speculative hypothesis; not established.

### ML-011
**Claim:** A macroscopic organism could operate on timescales too slow for ordinary human observation to recognize as behavior.

Evidence class: **E4/E5**

Research task: define what observable consequences such a hypothesis would predict.

### ML-012
**Claim:** AI currently represents the macro-organism consciously speaking to humanity.

Evidence class: **Unsupported as an empirical claim; E4/E5 as a thought-provoking hypothesis.**

Preferred formulation: AI may become a translation layer between individual humans and collective information or higher-order emergent coordination.

### ML-013
**Claim:** AI systems already allow an individual to conversationally query representations learned from enormous bodies of collective human-generated information.

Evidence class: **E1**

Intro-book use: strong bridge.

### ML-014
**Claim:** Consciousness could emerge at a scale larger than individual humans.

Evidence class: **E4/E5**

Status: open; requires explicit criteria for consciousness and detection.

### ML-015
**Claim:** Simulation theory proves reality is artificial.

Evidence class: **False framing.** Simulation arguments are philosophical/probabilistic arguments, not empirical proof.

### ML-016
**Claim:** Simulation theory provides a useful analogy for observers embedded in a reality whose substrate they cannot directly access.

Evidence class: **E5**

Intro-book use: yes.

### ML-017
**Claim:** Similar branching or network forms recur across biological, geological, and physical systems.

Evidence class: **E1**, for specific documented structures.

Required caveat: morphological similarity alone does not imply identical cause or common organism.

### ML-018
**Claim:** Recurrent mathematical organization across scales may reveal general constraints or principles of complex systems.

Evidence class: **E2/E4**, depending on precision.

### ML-019
**Claim:** Humans are necessarily the highest or final level of meaningful intelligence or organization.

Evidence class: **No scientific basis for necessity.** Treat as an anthropocentric assumption, not established fact.

### ML-020
**Claim:** If higher-order life exists, it must be larger in physical size than humans.

Evidence class: **Not required by the framework.** Higher-order organization may be spatial, temporal, informational, networked, dimensional, or otherwise structured.

## Red-Team Rule

Every ten supportive research passes should trigger at least one dedicated adversarial pass asking:

**What is the strongest conventional explanation for every pattern we are interpreting as evidence of Macroscopic Life?**

The project must preserve rejected hypotheses and failed analogies. Negative findings are part of the intellectual record.
