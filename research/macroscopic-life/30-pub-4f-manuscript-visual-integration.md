# MACROSCOPIC LIFE — PUB-4F MANUSCRIPT / VISUAL INTEGRATION

## Status

**PUB-4F COMPLETE — VISUAL RHYTHM AND MANUSCRIPT INSERTION ARCHITECTURE LOCKED**

PUB-4F integrates the Class A, B, and C visual systems into the 16-chapter v0.5 voice-locked manuscript.

The objective is not to place an image beside every concept.

It is to decide where a visual reduces cognitive load, where a page turn can carry an argument, where captions must do scientific work, where prose can be shortened because the figure now teaches the mechanism, and where the book should remain deliberately text-only.

Governing rule:

> **A visual enters the manuscript only when it changes what the reader can understand, remember, or question.**

---

# 1. BOOK-LEVEL VISUAL RHYTHM

The complete book should alternate among four states:

1. **TEXT IMMERSION** — sustained narrative/intellectual momentum.
2. **VISUAL EXPLANATION** — diagram reduces mechanism complexity.
3. **VISUAL DISRUPTION** — image changes scale or destabilizes assumption.
4. **WHITE-SPACE PAUSE** — reader is given room after a conceptual turn.

The desired rhythm is not `text-image-text-image.`

It is:

**story → mechanism → pause → pullback → question → text → test**

---

# 2. PAGE-TURN DOCTRINE

Page turns should be used at major epistemic reveals.

Preferred uses:

- reader sees local world before larger scale is revealed;
- seductive analogy appears before scientific brake;
- mechanism appears after a conceptual mystery is posed;
- failed test appears after a strong candidate property;
- Chapter 16 returns visually to Chapter 1 after the reader has traveled the full argument.

Do not use page-turn suspense to imply a speculative conclusion is about to be revealed as fact.

---

# 3. TEXT-ONLY ZONES

Several kinds of prose should remain unillustrated:

- the strongest scientific brakes when a picture would soften their force;
- philosophical questions that benefit from reader imagination;
- moments of explicit uncertainty;
- Chapter 15 hostile-review passages where the argument itself should dominate;
- final lines of Chapter 16 after the last visual pullback.

Whitespace and text-only pages are active design tools.

# 21. PROSE-REDUCTION RULES

Visual integration permits copy reduction only when all of the following are true:

1. the figure fully teaches the repeated relationship;
2. essential terminology remains defined in prose or caption;
3. audiobook readers still receive the complete concept;
4. scientific qualifications are not lost;
5. the visual is not required to understand the book.

Priority candidates for later trimming:

- repeated observer-window descriptions;
- some boundary-category repetition;
- mechanism repetition surrounding memory chain;
- repeated signal/information/integration chains;
- dense explanation of civilization matrix after the spread.

Do not trim the canonical scientific brakes merely because the figure contains them.

# 25. CROSS-MEDIA INTEGRATION

## Print

Use page turns, spreads, margins, and negative space.

## Ebook

Preserve sequence even where spreads collapse vertically.

A figure that depends on left/right page reveal must receive digital sequencing instructions.

## Web

Interactive zoom/layer reveals may expand figures, but core meaning must remain available statically.

## Audiobook

No essential argument may exist only in a visual.

Audio companion can reference figure IDs but should be optional.

## Presentation

Use sequential reveals matching manuscript order: question first, mechanism second, implication last.

# 27. PUB-4F LOCKS

1. Visual density is rhythmic, not uniform.
2. Text gets the first imaginative move in most chapter openings.
3. Mechanism figures generally arrive after the mystery/question is posed.
4. Visuals may reduce repeated prose but may never become required for comprehension.
5. Scientific brakes remain text-visible even when diagrams repeat them.
6. Chapter 14 uses deliberate visual seduction followed by text-only rejection.
7. Chapter 15 remains visually sparse.
8. Chapter 16 increases visual support during testing, then tapers to zero.
9. Final visual occurs before the final text-only page.
10. The final sentence receives no competing image.
11. Civilization repair is not illustrated early in Act III.
12. Class C visuals never appear before their speculative status is established.
13. Page turns reveal mechanisms or questions, not fake proof.
14. The audiobook remains fully intelligible without visuals.
15. Every major visual has a setup/payoff relationship to the manuscript.
16. Layout markup will use stable figure IDs and insertion comments.
