## Civilization verdict

The manuscript does **not** establish civilization as a biological individual.

The controlling conclusion remains:

> **Civilization is an unusually integrated macroscopic organization and cognitive ecology. Whether it is moving toward a new level of individuality remains an open question.**

Reproduction, heredity, higher-level selection, coherent organismal boundary, conflict suppression, and unified action remain weak, absent, ambiguous, or unestablished at the civilization-individual level.

## Hardened hypothesis

> **Macroscopic Life is the hypothesis that living or life-like organization may occur in nested hierarchies at scales larger than familiar individual organisms. A candidate higher-order individual must not be inferred from resemblance, complexity, connectivity, or useful large-scale description alone. It must earn individual-level treatment through converging causal evidence that the proposed higher-level organization maintains historically continuous, bounded or asymmetrically coupled, self-affecting organization in ways that improve explanation and prediction beyond independent components plus their shared environment. Strong biological claims additionally require appropriate evidence concerning reproduction, heredity, and selection at the proposed level.**
