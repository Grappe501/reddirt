# Testable science module

Canonical questions:

- perturb genes or regulatory pathways and measure developmental consequences;
- perturb signaling pathways and measure spatial/temporal responses;
- alter mechanical conditions and observe changes in morphogenesis;
- alter ion-channel activity or electrical state in systems where a bioelectric hypothesis is being tested;
- compare results across organisms, tissues, and experimental models;
- reproduce predicted effects with independent measurements;
- construct quantitative models and test where they succeed or fail;
- distinguish mechanisms that generalize from those that are system-specific.
