> **Figure 9. The Missing Limb — From Damage to Restoration.** Injury removes structure, but it also changes the physical conditions of the tissue that remains: wound boundaries, cell contacts, geometry, chemical and immune signals, mechanical forces, ionic and electrical conditions, and neural or vascular relationships can all change.
