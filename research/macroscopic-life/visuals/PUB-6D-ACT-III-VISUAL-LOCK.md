## Purpose

Act III supplies Book One's strongest biological proof of principle: local components can participate in system-level form, repair, memory, and anticipation without any single component possessing a complete representation of the organism.

The visual package must make distributed control intuitive while aggressively protecting the line between established bioelectric/developmental biology and unsupported claims about mystical energy, consciousness, telepathy, or paranormal fields.

## Figure 7 — The Body Electric

**Placement:** Chapter 7.

**Core image:** A multiscale biological electrical map: cell membrane with ion gradients and membrane potential → electrically coupled cell field/gap junctions → tissue-scale voltage differences → wound current/electrotaxis and developmental patterning context.

**Scientific job:** Show that electrical organization is ordinary, measurable biology extending beyond neurons.

**Required visual logic:**
- label membrane potential as voltage across a membrane;
- distinguish ion gradients, channels, gap junction coupling, action potentials, and slower bioelectric states;
- show instrument measurement rather than supernatural visualization;
- use false color only with an explicit scale/key;
- distinguish nervous-system electrophysiology from non-neural developmental bioelectricity;
- no aura silhouette, chakra imagery, resonant-human glow, or implied telepathy.

**Caption:** *Living cells maintain electrical potentials through ion gradients and membrane transport. In many tissues, electrical states and electrically coupled networks participate in signaling, migration, wound response, and development. These measurable bioelectric phenomena are physical biology; they are not evidence for paranormal energy fields or telepathy.*

## Figure 8 — No Cell Holds the Blueprint

**Placement:** Chapter 8.

**Core image:** Embryonic/tissue development shown as interacting control layers rather than a DNA-to-body assembly line. Layers include gene regulation, morphogen gradients, cell-cell signaling, mechanics, geometry, bioelectric state, feedback, and environmental context, converging on changing tissue form.

**Scientific job:** Replace the simplistic mental model `DNA = literal architectural blueprint` with a distributed developmental-control model.

**Required visual logic:**
- DNA/gene regulation is essential but not depicted as a tiny complete drawing of the adult;
- feedback arrows run in multiple directions;
- mechanical and positional information influence gene expression and cell behavior;
- emerging anatomy feeds back into subsequent development;
- no homunculus, central controller, or hidden master cell;
- terms such as `target morphology` or `morphogenetic code` require qualified labeling when used.

**Caption:** *Development is produced by interacting genetic, chemical, electrical, mechanical, geometric, and environmental processes. No individual cell needs to contain or comprehend a complete picture of the finished organism for coordinated form to emerge.*

## Figure 9 — Rebuilding What Is Missing

**Placement:** Chapter 9.

**Core image:** Comparative regeneration triptych: planarian pattern regulation, salamander limb regeneration, and mammalian liver compensatory growth. Each panel shows injury → local/systemic response → reconstruction or growth → stopping behavior, emphasizing that these are different biological processes.

**Scientific job:** Show how a macroscopic structural deficit can alter local states and trigger organized repair without invoking a ghost blueprint.

**Required visual logic:**
- planarian panel emphasizes polarity/positional pattern regulation;
- salamander panel identifies wound epithelium, blastema, nerves, positional information, and patterned outgrowth;
- liver panel explicitly labels compensatory growth as a contrast, not limb-like regeneration;
- stopping rules/homeostatic termination are visible;
- no implication that tissues consciously `see` the missing structure;
- no anthropomorphic cells looking at or remembering a limb.

**Caption:** *Regenerative systems can translate injury and altered positional relationships into coordinated repair. Different organisms accomplish this through different mechanisms. The remarkable result does not require conscious cells or an immaterial blueprint: local biological processes can collectively implement a higher-level restoration function.*

## Figure 10 — Memory Is a Physical Consequence of the Past

**Placement:** Chapter 10.

**Core image:** A taxonomy of physically instantiated memory across scales: neural synaptic/network memory, immune memory, epigenetic/cellular state persistence, bistable regulatory states, positional/tissue memory, structural traces, and externalized memory. A central timeline shows `past event → persistent physical change → later causal effect`.

**Scientific job:** Give the reader an operational definition of memory broad enough for multiscale biology without turning every durable mark into cognition.

**Required visual logic:**
- distinguish storage/persistence from functional retrieval or causal use;
- show different mechanisms rather than one universal memory substance;
- explicitly mark passive records as a boundary case;
- avoid implying that immune or cellular memory is autobiographical consciousness;
- replacement of individual components can coexist with persistence of system-level organization.

**Caption:** *At its broadest operational level, a system remembers when consequences of past events persist physically and remain causally available to influence later behavior. Biological memory exists through many mechanisms; this does not mean that every form of memory is conscious or that every persistent trace is functional memory.*

## Figure 11 — Living Ahead of Now

**Placement:** Chapter 11.

**Core image:** A time-axis control diagram contrasting reactive regulation with anticipatory regulation. Examples progress from circadian preparation before sunrise/feeding, to allostatic/feedforward adjustment, learned prediction, nervous-system planning, and human institutional forecasting. A prediction-error feedback loop shows expectation being corrected by incoming evidence.

**Scientific job:** Demonstrate that present biological state can be organized partly by information about recurring or learned future conditions without implying access to future facts.

**Required visual logic:**
- clearly separate prediction from prophecy;
- expected future is represented as an internal/model-based state, not information traveling backward through time;
- show entrainment and learning from prior regularities;
- prediction error updates future expectation;
- distinguish physiological anticipation from conscious planning;
- no precognition or paranormal symbolism.

**Caption:** *Living systems can prepare for expected conditions before those conditions arrive. Circadian anticipation, feedforward regulation, learned prediction, and planning use past regularities and current information to alter present state. Prediction is fallible and updateable; it does not require paranormal access to the future.*

# Production Guardrails

- Electrical does not mean mystical.
- Distributed does not mean conscious.
- Information does not require a mind reading symbols.
- Memory does not automatically mean subjective recollection.
- Prediction does not mean prophecy.
- Repair does not prove an immaterial blueprint.
- `Goal`, `target`, `decision`, and `code` language must be operationally anchored or visibly qualified.
- Do not draw a central command center unless the biology actually contains one.
- Do not use anthropomorphic cell faces, eyes, brains, or intention icons in the scientific figures.
- Prefer causal arrows, measured variables, state transitions, and feedback loops over metaphorical decoration.
