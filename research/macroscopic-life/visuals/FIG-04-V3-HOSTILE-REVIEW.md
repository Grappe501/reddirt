# 9. Renewal and turnover

**PASS VISUAL; P2 QUALIFICATION**

This is now one of the strongest conceptual panels.

Generated footer:

Better than v2, but `same individual` touches philosophical personal identity. The biological claim needed is narrower.

Use:

> `Many cell populations turn over at different rates. Components can be replaced while organism-level organization persists.`

# 10. Cancer / conflict-control panel

**P1 — ONE IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC COPY PROBLEM REMAINS**

and

`It also shows that groups of cells can pursue their own short-term interests...`

**PASS DIRECTION; P2 LIST REFINEMENT**
