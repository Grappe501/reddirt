# 3. FIG-01 Composite Review

## What now works

The composite finally moves toward the intended inside/outside relationship: a small observer exists within a larger organism and lacks access to the whole.

## Remaining correction

The deer is an unnecessary biological claim vehicle. The canonical Chapter 1 scenario should remain a deliberately constructed thought experiment inside a **human body**, matching the manuscript architecture.

The phrase `You are inside a larger living system` must not be presented as a statement about the human reader. It applies to the hypothetical embedded observer.

## Locked final structure

**Title:** The Embedded Observer

**Embedded label:** `THOUGHT EXPERIMENT`

Visual levels:

1. hypothetical intelligent microscopic observer;
2. accessible local cellular/tissue environment;
3. organ-scale context hidden from observer;
4. human organism containing all previous levels.

Primary line:

> `An observer can build an accurate science of its local environment without direct access to the larger organizational level that contains it.`

Brake:

> `This thought experiment demonstrates an observational limitation. It is not evidence that humans inhabit a larger organism.`

Status after deterministic rebuild: **CLAIM-READY**.

# 5. FIG-14 Composite Review

## What works

The scale progression visually communicates emergence and higher-order capability.

## Remaining problem

The composite still asks `Does human civilization exhibit organism-like properties?` and then supplies examples that visually encourage `yes`.

The figure's job is not resemblance collection. It is to expose where the organism analogy succeeds and fails.

## Locked final structure

Use a mixed-evidence matrix with rows:

- boundary coherence;
- integration/interdependence;
- energy/material coordination;
- distributed memory;
- prediction/anticipation;
- repair/recovery;
- conflict suppression;
- higher-level goal closure;
- reproduction/heredity;
- system-level causal autonomy;
- advantage over simpler network models.

Evidence states:

`STRONG / PARTIAL / WEAK / UNRESOLVED`

Do not give a total organism score.

Final conclusion:

> `Civilization clearly has capabilities no individual human possesses. Whether it constitutes a higher-order biological individual remains unresolved.`

Brake:

> `Functional similarity is not biological identity.`

Status after deterministic rebuild: **CLAIM-READY**.
