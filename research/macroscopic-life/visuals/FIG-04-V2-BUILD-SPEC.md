# Macroscopic Life — FIG-04 v2 Build Spec

## Figure
**Figure 4 — The Civilization Inside Your Skin**

## Build status
**V2 STRUCTURAL REBUILD SPEC — READY FOR VISUAL GENERATION**

## Mission
Rebuild Figure 4 around the scientifically defensible fact that a human organism is a higher-order biological individual constructed from many specialized living cells organized into tissues, organs, and interacting systems. The microbiome is a supporting ecological partner layer, not the organizing center of the figure.

The figure must prepare the reader for Chapter 5's harder question: what mechanisms turn many living units into a durable higher-level individual?

---

# 1. Locked headline architecture

**Title:**
`THE CIVILIZATION INSIDE YOUR SKIN`

**Subtitle:**
`What appears to be one organism is built from many living components with different roles, signals, dependencies, and degrees of autonomy.`

Small analogy marker adjacent to title:

`CIVILIZATION = ANALOGY FOR SPECIALIZATION + COORDINATION`

Do not imply biological and political organization are identical.

---

# 2. Primary visual — nested biological organization

Use a wide editorial/scientific poster in the established Macroscopic Life visual family.

Primary left-to-right or center-out hierarchy:

`HUMAN ORGANISM → ORGAN SYSTEM → ORGAN → TISSUE → SPECIALIZED HUMAN CELLS → LOCAL CELLULAR SIGNALS`

The nested levels must be visually unmistakable.

Suggested central composition:

- full human anatomical silhouette;
- highlighted cardiovascular/digestive/immune or mixed organ-system layer;
- zoom into one organ;
- zoom into tissue architecture;
- zoom into several specialized human cell types;
- final local-signaling inset.

Do not make the gut the dominant anatomical focus.

---

# 3. Specialized cell layer

Show a representative selection rather than a comprehensive catalog:

- neuron;
- cardiomyocyte or skeletal muscle cell;
- epithelial cell;
- immune cell;
- red blood cell;
- hepatocyte or another metabolic cell.

Primary message:

`Different cells perform different local functions. No single cell performs the functions of the human organism as a whole.`

Avoid anthropomorphic faces, workers, citizens, voting, command-center imagery, or conscious-cell language.

---

# 4. Coordination rail

Use the established project systems grammar:

`SIGNAL → LOCAL RESPONSE → MATERIAL / ENERGY FLOW → FEEDBACK → ORGANISM-LEVEL FUNCTION`

Below or adjacent, show representative coordination channels:

- chemical signaling;
- electrical signaling;
- mechanical signaling;
- direct cell-cell contact;
- circulation/material transport.

Do not imply one central controller coordinates all body functions.

---

# 5. Microbiome — supporting partner layer

Include a restrained gut/skin interface inset labeled:

`MICROBIAL PARTNERS — ECOLOGICAL RELATIONSHIPS AT HOST INTERFACES`

Copy direction:

`Human-associated microbial communities interact with host tissues through metabolism, immune-system interactions, competition, and other context-dependent ecological relationships.`

Explicit distinction:

`Microbial partners are not equivalent to human cells and are not automatically members of the human biological individual in the same sense.`

No microbiome wellness claims, no mood/serotonin claims, no universal healthy-balance language, no unnecessary microbial counts.

---

# 6. Persistence through component turnover

Create a strong inset titled:

`THE PARTS CHANGE. THE ORGANISM PERSISTS.`

Visual rail:

`CELL PRODUCTION → DIFFERENTIATION → FUNCTION → DEATH / REMOVAL → REPLACEMENT`

Above the rail, keep a continuous human-organism silhouette or timeline to show higher-level persistence.

Copy:

`Many cell populations turn over at different rates. Components can be replaced while organism-level organization persists.`

Brake:

`Not every human cell is replaced on one universal schedule; some cell populations are exceptionally long-lived.`

Never use the false `the body replaces itself every seven years` claim.

---

# 7. Conflict-control inset

Title:

`COORDINATION IS MAINTAINED — NOT AUTOMATIC`

Use a restrained split schematic:

LEFT:
`regulated proliferation → tissue organization → organism-level function`

RIGHT:
`regulatory escape → clonal overgrowth → disrupted tissue function`

Label right side:
`CANCER AS A BREAKDOWN OF MULTICELLULAR COOPERATION / GROWTH CONTROL`

Do not depict cancer cells as evil, rebellious, selfish in a conscious sense, or politically insurgent.

Optional small line:

`Immune surveillance, growth regulation, apoptosis, tissue boundaries, and repair are among the processes that help maintain multicellular organization.`

---

# 8. Canonical six takeaways

Use these as the deterministic scientific payload:

1. `A human organism is composed of vast numbers of living cells with specialized roles.`
2. `Cells communicate through chemical, electrical, mechanical, and direct-contact signals.`
3. `Tissues and organs coordinate local activity into organism-level functions no single cell can perform alone.`
4. `Many human-associated microbes participate in ecological relationships with the host, but they are not equivalent to human cells.`
5. `The components of a body can cooperate, compete, die, reproduce, migrate, and be replaced while the organism persists.`
6. `The apparent unity of the person is therefore a higher-order biological organization, not evidence that every collection of organisms forms a new individual.`

Generated image text must not override these statements.

---

# 9. Concept panel

Replace wellness-style `Why It Matters` content with:

### SPECIALIZATION
Different components perform different local functions.

### INTEGRATION
Signals, forces, and material flows coordinate activity across tissues and organs.

### PERSISTENCE
The higher-order organism can persist despite turnover of many constituent cells.

### CONFLICT CONTROL
Multicellular organization depends partly on limiting or repairing processes that undermine the whole.

---

# 10. Final line

Large footer:

`ONE ORGANISM. MANY LIVING COMPONENTS.`

Secondary line:

`Biological individuality can be multiscale.`

---

# 11. Critical brake

This must appear in the figure itself:

`Multicellular integration demonstrates that higher-order biological individuality exists. It does not show that every group, network, ecosystem, microbiome, or civilization forms an organism.`

---

# 12. Visual guardrails

- established deep navy / blue-black field;
- cream/white editorial type;
- restrained electric blue and amber accents;
- documentary/anatomical realism plus systems diagrams;
- no psychedelic glow;
- no aura field;
- no tiny people/city buildings inside cells;
- no literal civilization imagery that implies equivalence;
- no central `master cell`;
- no homunculus/controller;
- no anthropomorphic cells;
- no microbiome marketing aesthetic;
- no medical-treatment claims;
- no universal cell-turnover number;
- no exact cell/microbe counts unless separately sourced and intentionally retained.

---

# 13. Screenshot-safe claim

The composition should remain scientifically defensible if detached from the chapter:

`A human organism is a coordinated higher-order biological system built from many specialized living components. This demonstrates that biological individuality can be multiscale; it does not establish that arbitrary collectives are organisms.`

---

# 14. Production instruction

For visual generation, prioritize:

1. nested anatomy;
2. specialization;
3. coordination;
4. turnover/persistence;
5. conflict control;
6. microbiome as secondary partner layer.

Keep generated prose minimal. Scientific copy, labels, counts, and final typography will be deterministically rebuilt after visual lock.

## Acceptance target

FIG-04 v2 should make a skeptical reader immediately understand:

> `The ordinary human individual is already a successful example of many living units becoming one higher-order biological individual.`

and immediately understand the brake:

> `That fact opens the question of higher-scale individuality; it does not answer it for societies, ecosystems, or planets.`
