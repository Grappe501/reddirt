# 6. TEST 8 — WHOLE-ONLY INFORMATION

The exact canonical test name must remain unchanged.

## Header

**TEST 8 — WHOLE-ONLY INFORMATION**

## Operational definition

> **Does the state or relationship of the proposed whole provide reproducible task-relevant information that cannot be recovered from any single component alone and that improves prediction of system behavior beyond appropriate component-only models?**

## Procedure

1. Define the proposed system boundary, components, task, and outcome.
2. Construct an appropriate component-only baseline.
3. Define candidate relational or system variables.
4. Test whether those variables improve out-of-sample prediction of system behavior.
5. Where possible, perturb the relational organization and measure the resulting change.
6. Compare alternative boundaries, decompositions, and simpler models.

## Firewalls

> **WHOLE-ONLY INFORMATION IS INFORMATION IN ORGANIZATION — NOT INFORMATION FLOATING ABOVE MATTER.**

> **INFORMATION IN RELATIONSHIPS IS STILL PHYSICAL INFORMATION.**

A positive Test 8 result does not by itself establish intelligence, agency, consciousness, or organismhood.

# 7. SYSTEM-LEVEL CAPABILITY ATTRIBUTION TEST

## Header

**WHEN DOES A CAPABILITY BELONG TO THE SYSTEM?**

A system-level capability claim should specify:

- system boundary;
- task or problem class;
- information available;
- possible actions or internal state changes;
- prospectively defined performance criterion;
- mechanism;
- dependence on organization;
- perturbation sensitivity;
- comparison with component-only and simpler models.

## Paired rules

> **DO NOT ATTRIBUTE A CAPABILITY TO THE WHOLE MERELY BECAUSE CAPABLE PARTS ARE PRESENT.**

> **DO NOT DENY A CAPABILITY TO THE WHOLE MERELY BECAUSE NO PART POSSESSES IT ALONE.**

# 9. WHAT COUNTS AS EVIDENCE

## Header

**WHAT COUNTS AS EVIDENCE**

- A defined system boundary.
- A defined task or outcome.
- An appropriate component-only baseline.
- Explicit relational or system variables.
- Prospectively defined metrics.
- Out-of-sample prediction or reproducible performance improvement.
- Perturbation evidence showing organization-dependent causal contribution.
- Alternative decompositions and simpler models tested.
- Results that generalize beyond one convenient example.

### Footer

> **CAPABILITY MUST BE ATTRIBUTED TO THE LEVEL THAT EARNS IT.**
