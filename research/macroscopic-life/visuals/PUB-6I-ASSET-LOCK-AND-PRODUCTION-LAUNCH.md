# 6. Executable Brief — FIG-01 The Embedded Observer

## Scientific job

Make it immediately intuitive that a locally intelligent observer can accurately model its accessible environment while remaining unable to directly observe the larger organizational level containing it.

## Composition

Create a nested cutaway with three readable scales:

1. tiny embedded observer/local environment;
2. cellular/tissue-scale context containing that environment;
3. larger human-body context visible to the book reader.

The embedded observer's accessible sensory region should be visibly limited. The reader can see the larger nesting relationship, but the observer cannot.

## Must communicate

`Accurate local knowledge ≠ complete access to containing scale.`

## Must not communicate

- humans are literally microbes;
- a cosmic organism has been discovered;
- the observer's ignorance proves a larger organism exists.

## Visual treatment

Restrained scientific editorial illustration; high-detail local region transitioning to increasingly contextual larger scales; no cosmic glow; no spiritual iconography; minimal embedded text.

## Generation boundary

Generative illustration is appropriate for the nested environmental base. Final scale indicators, callouts, labels, and epistemic boundary should be deterministic/editable.

## Acceptance sentence

A skeptical reader should say: `Yes, an observer can be locally competent and still lack direct access to its containing organizational level.`

---

# 7. Executable Brief — FIG-07 The Body Electric

## Scientific job

Show that electrical organization is ordinary measurable biology and that non-neural tissues can use electrical state in signaling, migration, wound response, and development.

## Composition

Left-to-right or nested progression:

`ion gradients / membrane potential → channels and gap junctions → coupled tissue electrical states → wound/developmental context`

Include an instrumentation cue establishing measurability.

## Must communicate

`Bioelectricity is physical cellular/tissue physiology.`

## Must not communicate

- aura;
- chakra;
- telepathy;
- spiritual energy;
- a luminous field surrounding a person;
- all electrical phenomena are action potentials.

## Visual treatment

Scientific mechanism diagram. Any false-color voltage map must have a clear legend and must look instrument-derived rather than mystical.

## Generation boundary

Do not rely on generated imagery for channels, quantitative voltage scales, arrows, terminology, or anatomical mechanism. Naturalistic tissue/cell base imagery may be generated only if independently checked.

## Acceptance sentence

A skeptical biologist should recognize familiar electrophysiological concepts without mistaking the figure for an endorsement of energy-body claims.

# 8. Executable Brief — FIG-14 Civilization Under the Organism Test

## Scientific job

Prevent the book's most tempting overclaim by showing that civilization has impressive higher-order capabilities while remaining weak or unresolved on several requirements for organism-level individuality.

## Composition

Rows represent major criteria; columns compare candidate descriptions or evidence classes. Civilization must visibly score strongly on some dimensions and weakly/uncertainly on others.

Minimum domains represented:

- boundary;
- integration;
- matter/energy/information coordination;
- memory;
- prediction;
- repair;
- conflict suppression;
- unified goals/decision closure;
- reproduction/heredity;
- system-level selection/model advantage.

## Must communicate

`Civilization is highly organized and possesses supra-individual capabilities, but organismhood remains unresolved.`

## Must not communicate

- civilization is already a biological superorganism;
- connectivity equals consciousness;
- a numeric-looking score constitutes an empirical measurement if it is heuristic.

## Visual treatment

Deterministic matrix. Prefer categorical states such as `strong evidence`, `mixed`, `weak`, `unresolved`, with explicit explanation. Avoid fake decimal precision.

## Generation boundary

This figure should be primarily deterministic. Generative imagery is unnecessary except possibly a restrained non-data-bearing header/background element.

## Acceptance sentence

A reader should come away less certain that civilization is an organism than when the chapter began, but more interested in how the question could be tested.

# 9. Executable Brief — FIG-16 The Macroscopic Life Test Matrix

## Scientific job

Convert the book's hypothesis into a research program that can fail.

## Canonical test identities

The framework must preserve exactly these eleven tests:

1. Boundary Perturbation
2. Integration Ablation
3. Conflict Suppression
4. Energetic Organization
5. Memory Turnover
6. Prediction Advantage
7. Repair Autonomy
8. Whole-Only Information
9. Higher-Level Intervention
10. Reproduction/Heredity
11. Model Competition

Reader-friendly explanations may accompany the names, but do not casually rename, merge, split, or add tests.

## Composition

Recommended matrix columns:

- Test
- Question
- Stronger evidence
- Evidence against
- Candidate measurement/intervention

The design should visually emphasize that negative outcomes are legitimate and informative.

## Must communicate

`Macroscopic Life is not a resemblance checklist. It is a falsifiable comparative research program.`

## Must not communicate

- passing one test proves organismhood;
- all tests have equal evidentiary weight;
- the framework has already validated Earth or civilization;
- the tests are established consensus criteria in biology if they are project-specific operational tests.

## Visual treatment

Fully deterministic framework diagram/table. This is the least appropriate Batch 1 figure for generative art.

## Acceptance sentence

A hostile reader should be able to point to specific observations that would weaken or defeat the higher-order-individuality hypothesis.
