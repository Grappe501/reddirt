# P1-1 — `SAME DNA` IS TOO ABSOLUTE

Panel 1 says:

> `Same DNA, many possible cell types.`

The architecture explicitly warned against this wording because somatic mutation, mosaicism, immune-system rearrangements, copy-number variation, and other genomic differences make literal identity too strong.

## Required deterministic replacement

Heading remains:

> **SHARED GENOME, DIFFERENT STATES**

Replace the subline with:

> **Many cells inherit substantially the same genome yet develop very different states and functions.**

This is both more accurate and more interesting.

---

# P1-2 — MORPHOGEN WORDING STILL SOUNDS COGNITIVE

Panel 2 says:

> `Cells read their local environment, not a global map.`

And lower copy says:

> `Cells respond to positional information from multiple sources.`

The second statement is fine. The first anthropomorphizes the process unnecessarily.

## Required replacement

> **Cell behavior depends on local conditions rather than access to a global anatomical map.**

Keep the concept of positional information, but operationalize it.

Recommended lower copy:

> **Spatial differences in signaling activity, neighboring cells, extracellular matrix, mechanics, geometry, and electrical state can contribute to position-dependent cellular responses.**

Do not imply that every listed factor constitutes `positional information` in exactly the same technical sense.

---

# P1-3 — REACTION–DIFFUSION VISUAL NEEDS AN EXPLICIT SYSTEM-SPECIFIC BRAKE

Panel 3 correctly labels reaction–diffusion as `interacting signals`, but because it occupies the first and most visually recognizable subpanel under `LOCAL INTERACTIONS CREATE PATTERN`, a detached crop could still imply that reaction–diffusion is the generic mechanism of anatomical pattern formation.

## Required deterministic label

Use:

> **REACTION–DIFFUSION — ONE PATTERN-FORMING MECHANISM**

And in the lower text:

> **In appropriate systems, local reaction and diffusion can generate spatial pattern. Other developmental patterns arise through different or interacting mechanisms.**

This resolves the screenshot-safety issue without changing the visual.

# P1-4 — BIOELECTRIC `TISSUE-LEVEL FIELDS` IS TOO LOOSE

Panel 4 lists:

- membrane potential
- ion flows
- gap junctions
- tissue-level fields `(system-specific)`

The first three are usable with refinement. `Tissue-level fields` is too broad and is exactly the kind of phrase that can be detached and reused to support unsupported morphogenetic-field interpretations.

## Required replacement

Use:

> **BIOELECTRIC PROCESSES**

with:

- membrane potentials;
- ion fluxes;
- electrical coupling through mechanisms such as gap junctions;
- endogenous electric fields in specific developmental contexts where established.

Then add:

> **Electrical state is one regulatory layer among many, not a universal anatomical voltage code.**

This should be visible inside or immediately beneath the bioelectric module.

# P2-1 — `GENOME PROVIDES INSTRUCTIONS` SHOULD BE MODERNIZED

Panel 1 currently says:

> `Genome provides instructions and constraints.`

`Instructions` is common shorthand, but the figure's entire purpose is to escape simplistic blueprint/computer-program language.

Prefer:

> **Genomic sequence contributes molecular capacities and regulatory possibilities within an inherited cellular context.**

For a public-facing shorter version:

> **The genome supplies essential molecular components and regulatory possibilities, but not a miniature anatomical drawing.**

# P2-2 — `CELL-CELL SIGNALS (PARACRINE, JUXTACRINE)` IS FINE BUT OVER-SPECIFIC FOR THE VISUAL

The terms are accurate but add textbook density without materially helping the central argument.

Prefer the shorter:

Detailed signaling modes belong in caption/endnotes if needed.

# P2-3 — `CELLS ACTIVATE OR INHIBIT SIGNALING MOLECULES` IS IMPRECISE

A cell can secrete ligands, express receptors, activate/inhibit signaling pathways, alter extracellular ligand availability, etc. `Activate a signaling molecule` is too vague.

Replace the example list with mechanistically cleaner verbs:

- cells release or respond to signaling molecules;
- cells change adhesion and shape;
- cells migrate, divide, differentiate, or die;
- cells alter extracellular matrix and mechanical conditions;
- neighboring responses change what happens next.

---

# P2-4 — PANEL 5 SHOULD NOT IMPLY A SIMPLE EMBRYO → FETUS → ADULT LADDER IS THE MECHANISM

The imagery is visually powerful, but the three-stage embryo/fetus progression can still look like maturation itself is the explanation.

The deterministic copy should make clear that these are **outcomes at increasing scales/times**, not causal stages replacing the mechanisms in Panels 1–4.

Recommended panel title remains:

> **FEEDBACK BUILDS FORM**

Recommended subtitle:

> **Repeated interactions alter cell states, tissue geometry, and future local conditions as development proceeds.**

Then label the images:

- early spatial organization;
- tissue and organ morphogenesis;
- integrated anatomical form.

Avoid `functional body (mature organism)` as a scientifically unnecessary endpoint claim.

# P2-5 — `STABLE ANATOMICAL FORM` IS TOO STRONG

Panel 5 says:

> `Stable anatomical form emerges...`

Anatomy is maintained and remodeled; development does not terminate in absolute stability.

Prefer:

> **Reproducible anatomical organization can emerge from interacting developmental processes over time.**

Then:

> **No individual cell needs to contain a complete representation of the finished body.**

# THE FEEDBACK LOOP — STRONG, BUT REFINE THE SEMANTICS

The large blue loop is one of V2's best improvements.

Current:

> `FEEDBACK LOOPS AT MULTIPLE SCALES`
>
> `Cells ↔ Tissues ↔ Organs ↔ Whole Organism`

This is useful but could imply that `the whole organism` acts as an independent top-down agent during development.

The figure should not introduce downward-causation philosophy here.

Prefer:

> **RECIPROCAL FEEDBACK ACROSS LEVELS OF ORGANIZATION**

Then:

> `cell states ↔ local interactions ↔ tissue geometry / mechanics ↔ developing anatomical context`

This is less catchy but much more mechanistically precise.

A public-facing compromise:

> **FEEDBACK ACROSS DEVELOPING SCALES**
>
> `Cells ↔ Local environment ↔ Tissues ↔ Developing anatomy`

Recommended.

# `WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW` — EXCELLENT FIREWALL

The V2 firewall is significantly better than V1.

It explicitly rejects:

- conscious designer/blueprint;
- universal morphogenetic field;
- single mechanism explaining all form;
- genes alone determining the final body;
- cells containing a miniature map;
- morphogenesis proving higher-level organismhood.

This is exactly the intellectual posture required.

One wording improvement:

Replace:

> `It does not demonstrate a conscious designer or blueprint`

with:

> **It does not require a conscious cellular planner or a complete explicit anatomical blueprint inside any individual cell.**

Why: science cannot use the figure to adjudicate a metaphysical `designer` claim. The scientifically relevant claim is that the developmental mechanism does not require a cellular planner.

This is an important philosophical correction.
