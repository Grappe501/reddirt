# 7. Developmental / lineage boundary

**P0/P1 — MAJOR SCIENTIFIC ERROR IN GENERATED COPY**

Generated text says:

`Arises from a single fertilized egg. Coordinates growth, repair, and turnover across trillions of cells.`

The first sentence is acceptable as a simplified description of ordinary human development, subject to exceptions such as chimerism.

The second sentence is wrong in category: **developmental lineage does not itself coordinate growth, repair, and turnover.** Developmental programs, signaling, gene regulation, tissue interactions, and physiological mechanisms do that.

Replace with:

> `Most cells in an ordinary human body descend through development from the fertilized egg, but lineage alone is not a complete membership criterion. Chimerism, pregnancy, transplantation, and symbiosis complicate a simple common-origin boundary.`

No `trillions` needed.

**PASS CONCEPT — REMOVE FIXED-SITE OVERGENERALIZATION**

# 9. Functional / extended effects

**P1 — CONCEPT RIGHT, EXAMPLES NEED RESTRUCTURE**

Generated copy lumps:

`tools, prosthetics, shelters, social networks`

into one category.

These are not equivalent relationships.

A prosthetic can be physically attached, sensorimotor-integrated, or medically incorporated in ways a beaver dam or social network is not. A tool can be temporarily incorporated into action/body schema without becoming a biological component. A shelter can be an extended phenotype without being organism tissue.

V3 should split the concept visually:

### BODY-COUPLED ARTIFACT
prosthetic / implanted device / tool

### EXTERNAL ORGANIZED STRUCTURE
beaver dam / spider web / bird nest

Canonical brake:

> `Functional coupling can extend beyond the body without automatically establishing biological membership.`

Do not use `social networks` yet; it unnecessarily telegraphs the later civilization argument.

# 12. Immune case panel

**PASS ARCHITECTURE — P1 COPY CORRECTIONS**

Pregnancy / microbiome / tumors / transplants is an excellent set of examples.

### Pregnancy
Generated:
`The immune system often tolerates the fetus, which is genetically distinct.`

Directionally correct but too simplistic. Maternal-fetal immune biology is an active, highly regulated interface involving fetal/placental tissues; it is not merely the immune system `tolerating the fetus.`

Use:

> `Pregnancy involves genetically distinct fetal and placental tissues at a specialized maternal-fetal immune interface.`

### Microbiome
Generated `Trillions of microbes live in and on the body...` — remove the number. Use:

> `Host-associated microbes can be tolerated or regulated differently depending on location and context.`

### Tumors
Good concept. Use:

> `Tumors arise from host cell lineages yet can alter, evade, or provoke immune responses.`

### Transplants
Use:

> `Transplanted tissues can provoke immune rejection; clinical immunosuppression can alter that response.`

Bottom:

> `Immune membership is context-dependent, dynamic, and imperfect.`

Good conceptual summary, though `membership` is metaphorical shorthand and should be explained in caption/prose.

# 13. Turnover and persistence

**PASS CONCEPT — P0/P1 REMOVE GENERATED NUMBERS**

The panel correctly demonstrates that organism-level continuity does not require retention of identical material components.

But the generated turnover times (`red blood cells ~120 days`, `gut lining cells ~3–5 days`, `skin cells ~2–4 weeks`, `bone cells ~10 years`) should **not be publication copy as generated**. Turnover rates depend on cell subtype, compartment, definition, individual, and measurement method; bone in particular is not represented by one meaningful `bone cells ~10 years` number.

Replace numbers with qualitative labels:

- `some blood-cell populations: months-scale`;
- `intestinal epithelial populations: rapid turnover`;
- `epidermal populations: weeks-scale turnover`;
- `many skeletal cell populations/remodeling processes: substantially slower and heterogeneous`.

Or remove times entirely.

Canonical:

> `Different cell populations turn over at very different rates. Many components can be replaced while organism-level organization persists.`

> `Some cells are exceptionally long-lived; there is no single body-wide replacement schedule.`
