# FIG-01 — The Embedded Observer

## Verdict

**P1 REVISION REQUIRED**

## What works

- strong editorial hierarchy;
- clear idea that perception is filtered;
- effective progression from world to body/brain to experienced scene;
- visually accessible explanation of observational limitation.

## Primary problem

The locked Chapter 1 figure is specifically an **embedded-observer / containing-scale thought experiment**. The render shifts toward a general neuroscience-of-perception argument: world → body → brain → experience.

That is scientifically interesting but overlaps Chapter 2 and weakens Chapter 1's unique job.

## Required revision

Return to the canonical nested-scale construction:

`embedded observer → local cellular/tissue environment → containing human organism`

The outside reader must be able to see the larger level while the embedded observer cannot.

Preserve the useful camera/perception concept only if it remains secondary or is moved to the sensory chapter.

## Claim brake

The figure must explicitly preserve:

`Local observational limits do not constitute evidence that humans themselves inhabit a larger organism.`

# FIG-07 — The Body Electric

## Verdict

**P0/P1 REVISION REQUIRED**

## What works

- excellent visual energy and reader engagement;
- clear presentation of electrical signaling as biology;
- neurons, sensory transduction, cellular membrane voltage, and integrated signaling are productive anchors;
- the figure could become one of the book's strongest visuals after scientific tightening.

## Publication blockers

### 1. Electromagnetic-field panel

The large human-surrounding field visualization is visually too close to an aura/energy-body convention and directly violates the locked anti-mysticism guardrail.

**Remove it.**

Replace it with instrument-grounded measurement imagery: electrode, voltage map, wound-current/electrotaxis diagram, or coupled-cell tissue potential.

### 2. Overbroad whole-body electrical language

Claims such as the body generating/responding to electromagnetic fields at multiple scales require precise mechanism and sourcing. The figure should focus on membrane potentials, ion gradients, action potentials where appropriate, gap junctions, tissue voltage states, wound currents, and electrotaxis.

### 3. Numerical labels

All generated quantitative claims must be independently sourced and deterministically typeset. No AI-generated number becomes publication evidence by appearing plausible.

## Required conclusion

`Bioelectricity is measurable physical biology; it is not evidence for paranormal energy fields.`

# FIG-14 — Civilization Under the Organism Test

## Verdict

**P0/P1 REVISION REQUIRED**

## What works

- powerful planetary systems image;
- immediately communicates connectivity and material/information flow;
- limitations panel is valuable;
- comparison structure is the right general mode.

## Publication blockers

### 1. “This is not a metaphor”

This is incorrect framing. The locked architecture explicitly treats nervous-system/circulatory comparisons as **functional analogies**, not biological identity.

Replace with language such as:

`This is a functional comparison, not a claim of biological identity.`

### 2. “Life can emerge at many scales. Civilization may be one of them.”

This closing line visually pushes the unresolved hypothesis too close to a conclusion.

Replace with:

`Organization can emerge across scales. Whether civilization constitutes a new biological individual remains unresolved.`

### 3. “Organism-like properties” framing

Retain only if every property is operationally defined and paired with unresolved/weak criteria. Connectivity, sensing, communication, and adaptation must not overwhelm weak evidence for reproductive bottleneck, heredity, unified agency, conflict suppression, and system-level Darwinian individuality.

### 4. Reproduction of ideas

Cultural transmission is not equivalent to biological reproduction/heredity. It may be compared as information persistence/transmission but cannot satisfy the reproduction criterion by rhetorical substitution.

## Required conclusion

`Civilization is a strong Macroscopic Organization candidate and an unresolved individuality transition — not a demonstrated superorganism.`

# FIG-16 — The Macroscopic Life Test Matrix

## Verdict

**P0 REBUILD REQUIRED**

## What works

- excellent table/matrix visual language;
- strong legibility and hierarchy;
- clear attempt to distinguish strong, partial, and uncertain evidence;
- this confirms that the final research framework should be deterministic rather than illustrative.

## Publication blocker

The generated figure replaced the project's locked **eleven canonical tests** with a generic eight-property life checklist.

That breaks the canonical research architecture.

The final FIG-16 must preserve exactly:

1. Boundary Perturbation
2. Integration Ablation
3. Conflict Suppression
4. Energetic Organization
5. Memory Turnover
6. Prediction Advantage
7. Repair Autonomy
8. Whole-Only Information
9. Higher-Level Intervention
10. Reproduction/Heredity
11. Model Competition

It must also show what would count **against** the hypothesis.

## Additional issue

Cells/humans/civilization/Earth/universe checkmarks manufacture an impression that the framework has already been quantitatively applied and validated. The canonical figure is supposed to explain the tests, not announce unsupported scores.

## Required rebuild

Use columns:

`TEST | QUESTION | STRONGER EVIDENCE | EVIDENCE AGAINST | CANDIDATE INTERVENTION/MEASUREMENT`

Avoid decorative scoring of Earth or the universe in the core framework.

## Required conclusion

`Macroscopic Life is a falsifiable comparative research program, not a resemblance checklist.`

# Batch 1 Style Lock

The following visual properties are approved for continuation:

- dark navy scientific-editorial field;
- high-contrast cream/white typography;
- restrained blue/orange scientific illumination;
- modular panels;
- prominent figure number/title;
- photographic/naturalistic imagery mixed with deterministic diagrams;
- clear lower-page explanatory zones;
- publication-style rather than sci-fi-interface styling.

The following are **not** approved as recurring conventions:

- human aura/field silhouettes;
- generated quantitative labels;
- AI-generated scientific tables treated as data;
- organism-language without qualification;
- nervous-system analogies presented as identity;
- cosmic imagery as evidence;
- checkmarks that imply empirical validation where only heuristic comparison exists.
