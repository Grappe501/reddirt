## Status

## Purpose

PUB-6H is the adversarial gate between a persuasive figure and a publishable figure.

The reviewer's job is not to admire the artwork. The reviewer attempts to prove that each figure is scientifically misleading, rhetorically manipulative, pseudoscientific, ambiguous, inaccessible, or stronger than the manuscript evidence.

A figure passes only when the strongest reasonable hostile reading cannot turn it into a claim the book does not make.

---

# 1. Prime Rule

For every figure ask:

> **What would a skeptical scientist think this image is claiming before reading the caption?**

If that inferred claim exceeds the manuscript, the image fails even if the caption later retreats.

Visual rhetoric counts as rhetoric.

---

# 2. Review Lenses

Every core figure receives all applicable reviews.

## A. Evidence Leakage

Does established evidence visually bleed into inference or speculation?

Fail examples:
- a continuous arrow from animal sensory limits to an unknown human sensory field;
- a cellular bioelectric map visually morphing into a human aura;
- a multicellular organism transitioning seamlessly into civilization without a qualification boundary;
- Earth drawn as anatomically organism-like before organismhood is demonstrated.

## B. Analogy Inflation

Has an analogy been drawn so literally that the reader is encouraged to treat similarity as identity?

Test:

`A behaves like B in property X` must never become visually equivalent to `A is B`.

## C. Anthropomorphism

Does the figure imply intention, awareness, desire, perception, planning, or knowledge where only regulation/information processing has been established?

Words/icons requiring scrutiny:
- knows;
- wants;
- sees;
- decides;
- remembers;
- predicts;
- goal;
- blueprint;
- brain;
- nervous system.

Operational use is permitted only when mechanism/context prevents literal misreading.

## D. Mysticism Contamination

Does the visual accidentally validate claims the manuscript explicitly does not establish?

High-risk zones:
- bioelectricity;
- frequency/resonance;
- invisible sensory channels;
- auras;
- Earth/civilization organism analogies;
- consciousness above the individual.

No glow, field, halo, resonance pattern, or energy-body convention may be used merely because it looks compelling.

## E. Scale Fallacy

Does the image imply that a mechanism known at one scale automatically persists at another?

Similarity across scale generates a question, not proof.

## F. Agency Smuggling

Does coordination get visually upgraded into unified agency?

A network can coordinate without possessing one decision center, one objective, one reproductive identity, or one conscious perspective.

## G. Evolutionary Inevitability

Does the image depict evolution as a ladder culminating in civilization or Macroscopic Life?

Any transition graphic must preserve branching, contingency, extinction, incomplete integration, and alternative outcomes where relevant.

## H. Causal Ambiguity

Can arrows be mistaken for demonstrated causal relationships when they indicate correlation, influence, analogy, or proposed mechanism?

Every arrow type must have a defensible semantic meaning.

## I. Quantitative Honesty

For matrices, scales, scores, timelines, or comparisons:
- are units real?
- are scores empirical or heuristic?
- are categories operationally defined?
- does visual precision exceed evidentiary precision?
- does ordering imply a ranking not supported by evidence?

## J. Accessibility / Comprehension

Can the figure be understood:
- at actual print size;
- in grayscale or by readers with common color-vision differences;
- without tiny text;
- without depending exclusively on decorative visual effects;
- through useful alt text?

## Figures 1–3 — Perception / Scale / Time

Attack for:
- `humans cannot perceive everything` being converted into `therefore hidden macroscopic organisms exist`;

# 4. Red-Team Roles

Each figure should be reviewed from at least these conceptual positions:

### Skeptical Biologist
`You are confusing metaphor with mechanism.`

### Evolutionary Theorist
`You have not demonstrated a new level of selection or individuality.`

### Physicist
`You are using energy, frequency, information, field, or resonance imprecisely.`

### Complex-Systems Researcher
`Emergent coordination does not require organismhood.`

### Science Communicator
`A normal reader will infer a stronger claim from this image than you intend.`

### Pseudoscience Auditor
`This image could be clipped from the book and used to validate a claim the book rejects.`

### Accessibility Reader
`The argument disappears if I cannot distinguish the visual encoding.`

No single reviewer needs to be an expert in every role. The review record must nevertheless answer each role's central objection.

---

# 5. The Screenshot Test

Every figure must survive removal from context.

Imagine someone posts only the figure on social media with no chapter text.

Ask:
1. What claim does it appear to make?
2. Could it be used as `proof` of auras, telepathy, planetary consciousness, cosmic consciousness, or civilization-as-organism?
3. Would the title/caption/embedded brake prevent that interpretation?
4. Does it still accurately distinguish evidence, inference, analogy, and hypothesis?

A figure that is safe only when surrounded by 2,000 words of qualification is not sufficiently disciplined.

---

# 6. The Beauty Penalty

The more visually compelling a speculative image is, the more aggressively it must be reviewed.

A beautiful image creates intuitive certainty faster than prose can qualify it.

Therefore:

> **Persuasive force increases the burden of epistemic labeling.**

The most dramatic final images receive the highest hostile-review standard.

---

# 7. Review Severity

Each issue receives one status:

- **P0 — Publication blocker:** materially false, scientifically indefensible, or transforms hypothesis into asserted fact.
- **P1 — Major:** likely to produce a materially stronger reader inference than the manuscript supports.
- **P2 — Moderate:** ambiguous, oversimplified, or insufficiently qualified.
- **P3 — Minor:** typography, labeling, readability, consistency, or polish issue with low claim risk.

No core figure can become `FINAL` with an open P0 or P1.

P2 items require explicit disposition.

# 9. Full-Book Sequence Review

Individual figures can be accurate while their sequence creates an unsupported cumulative inference.

After all figures pass individually, review them in order without manuscript prose:

1 → 18.

Ask whether the sequence alone appears to say:

`We cannot perceive everything → bodies are collectives → cells use electricity → networks process information → civilization is a network → therefore civilization/Earth is a conscious organism.`

If the sequence implies that syllogism, the visual program fails.

The required visual argument is:

`Observers are limited → biological individuality can be constructed → distributed control is real → larger networks can possess supra-component capabilities → those facts justify testing higher-order individuality → current candidates remain unresolved.`
