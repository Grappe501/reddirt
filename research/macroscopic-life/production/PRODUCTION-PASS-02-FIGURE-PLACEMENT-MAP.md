## Placement doctrine

Figures are arguments, not decoration. Each figure receives one primary scientific job and should appear only after the prose has supplied enough conceptual vocabulary for the reader to understand it.

Do not front-run a chapter's conclusion with a figure. Do not place a figure so late that it merely repeats a conclusion already absorbed.

Typesetting may move a figure within the same local section for page geometry, but must not move it across the conceptual anchor named below without a production review.

## Figure 02

**Primary job:** sensory/perceptual bandwidth beyond unaided human detection.

**Placement:** Chapter 1 — **The Window**.

**Anchor:** after the nonhuman-senses sequence and before the argument turns from biological senses to instruments/measurement.

**Reader effect:** human perception is a biological sample of reality, not the boundary of measurable reality.

**Required brake:** `A signal's absence from unaided perception is a reason to measure — not evidence that an unsupported hidden phenomenon exists.`

## Figure 03

**Primary job:** temporal scale / mismatch.

**Placement:** Chapter 1 — **The Window**.

**Required brake:** `Temporal mismatch can hide a process; slowness, speed, or duration is not evidence of life, intelligence, or agency.`
