## Technical preflight gates

Each exact publication binary must pass all gates below before final print or EPUB assembly.

### B1 — Identity

- figure number matches register;
- version matches register;
- source/asset hash recorded;
- no alternate/older render silently substituted;
- Figure 14 resolves to V8 only.

### B2 — Pixel geometry

Record:

- native pixel width;
- native pixel height;
- aspect ratio;
- intended placed width/height at 6x9 trim;
- effective PPI at placed size.

Minimum release rule:

- raster photographic/mixed artwork: target 300 effective PPI at placed size;
- line-art/text-heavy raster: target 600 effective PPI where practical;
- vector/deterministic text preferred for small scientific labels and diagrams.

Anything below 250 effective PPI requires explicit P1 review rather than silent acceptance.

### B3 — Typography and generated-text control

No AI-rendered scientific prose, numerical value, table entry, axis label, or citation may become publication authority merely because it is visible in an approved concept render.

Text-heavy figures require deterministic typesetting from locked copy. Numbers must be independently verified or removed when the argument does not require them.

This gate is especially important for Figure 02, whose final hostile review explicitly approved the composition while withholding publication-master text-asset status pending deterministic finish.

### B4 — Crop and trim safety

At final 6x9 placement verify:

- no title/caption/brake clipping;
- no meaningful element inside unsafe trim/gutter zone;
- no unintended aspect-ratio distortion;
- no crop that changes scientific meaning;
- required caveats remain visually adjacent to the element they qualify.

### B5 — Grayscale

Every figure must be inspected in grayscale at target trim size.

Pass requires:

- hierarchy survives without color;
- categories remain distinguishable;
- warning/brake text remains legible;
- no scientific distinction depends on hue alone;
- low-contrast labels remain readable.

Figure 14 V8's own hostile review explicitly leaves grayscale reproduction as a production check.

### B6 — Color and export

For print candidate record:

- source color mode/profile when available;
- conversion policy required by printer/distributor;
- transparency behavior;
- rasterization behavior;
- no uncontrolled neon/screen-only contrast assumptions.

Do not convert to CMYK speculatively before a printer/distributor profile is known. Preserve a controlled master and derive channel-specific exports.

### B7 — Caption and required brakes

Every figure must retain the publication-register brake(s). A caption may clarify but cannot weaken or omit the scientific safeguard.

Required brakes are part of figure identity, not optional decoration.

### B8 — Digital accessibility

For EPUB/digital publication every figure requires deterministic alt text that:

- states the scientific job of the figure;
- describes meaningful structure rather than decorative appearance;
- includes the central caution/brake where needed to prevent overclaim;
- does not attempt to reproduce every word of a dense infographic;
- points readers to surrounding text/table equivalent when the visual contains complex structured information.

No alt text is considered frozen merely because a hostile-review document discusses the figure. Pass 06E must bind final alt text to the exact publication asset.
