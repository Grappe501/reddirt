# Macroscopic Life — Book One Publication Freeze Policy

Once `book-one-rc1-freeze-record.json` reports `FROZEN`, Book One leaves research/manuscript development and enters publication production.

## Frozen without explicit version increment

- five-act / sixteen-chapter architecture;
- reader prose;
- scientific brakes and verdict language;
- Eleven Test names and Test 11 Model Competition requirement;
- Model C treatment;
- 45-slot scholarly architecture;
- verified bibliography authority set;
- approved Figure 2–16 compositions and deterministic scientific copy.

## Permitted after freeze

- typography and page layout;
- line/page breaks that do not alter prose;
- figure placement and sizing that preserve scientific legibility;
- accessibility text derived faithfully from approved figures;
- EPUB/PDF/print/web export engineering;
- metadata, ISBN/copyright/front-matter production once supplied/approved;
- typo correction only through an auditable erratum/version process;
- device, mobile, print, and accessibility proofing.

## Not permitted silently

- adding a new scientific claim;
- strengthening a speculative statement;
- changing a citation's claim burden;
- replacing an approved figure concept because of asset inconvenience;
- renaming/reordering the Eleven Tests;
- weakening Model Competition;
- turning project synthesis into external consensus;
- implying civilization has been established as an organism or higher-order individual.

## Version rule

Any substantive scientific or prose change after RC1 freeze requires an explicit new controlled version and a rerun of the affected scholarly, figure, and integrity gates.
