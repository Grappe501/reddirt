# Definition policy — locked

A term should be formally defined only when one of these is true:

1. ordinary readers are unlikely to know it;
2. the book uses it more precisely than ordinary speech;
3. misunderstanding it would change the argument.

Do not define ordinary scientific words merely because they are scientific.

Preferred pattern:

`term + plain-language meaning inside the sentence`

rather than repeated textbook-style definition paragraphs.

Glossary/endnotes can carry secondary definitions.

---

# Brake policy — locked

Every speculative stretch still needs a brake.

But brakes should be **local and proportional**.

Use three sizes:

### Micro-brake
One clause or sentence.

Example:
> `That expands observational access; it does not establish a hidden organism.`

### Standard brake
Two to three sentences where a tempting inference needs explicit blocking.

### Firewall
A full paragraph only at genuine conceptual danger points:
- intelligent-cell analogy;
- aura/frequency;
- cooperation → individuality;
- civilization reproduction/heredity;
- fuzzy boundary → everything connected.

Do not use firewall-sized disclaimers for every analogy.

---
