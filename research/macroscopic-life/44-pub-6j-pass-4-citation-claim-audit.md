# Evidence hierarchy

Use this order whenever practical:

1. primary experimental/observational literature;
2. major peer-reviewed review or synthesis;
3. authoritative scientific/academic reference;
4. textbook-level source for stable background definitions.

Popular science, commercial pages, unsourced explainers, social media, and AI-generated summaries are not publication evidence.

For contested or evolving mechanisms, prefer recent reviews plus landmark primary work and write the uncertainty into the prose.

---

# Claim classes

## E — Established
Strongly supported and suitable for direct factual wording.

## Q — Qualified
Evidence supports the core claim, but species, mechanism, scope, or interpretation requires qualification.

## D — Debated / multidimensional
Legitimate scientific framework with competing definitions or active debate. Never present one framework as universally settled.

## A — Analogy / thought experiment
No citation can turn analogy into evidence. Must be labeled as conceptual scaffolding.

## S — Speculative
May be asked as a hypothesis/question only. Must not inherit evidentiary status from neighboring established science.

# Chapter 2 — The World Your Senses Built

## Human visible range is only part of electromagnetic spectrum
**Class: E**

Stable physics/vision background.

## Bee ultraviolet sensitivity
**Class: E/Q**

Lock `many bees` or species-specific wording rather than universalizing all bees.

## Bee polarization use
**Class: Q**

Species/context-dependent. Source from sensory ecology/vision literature.

## Pit-viper infrared detection
**Class: E**

Strong evidence supports specialized infrared-sensitive pit organs. Important mechanistic correction: radiant infrared heats the pit membrane and is transduced by thermosensory pathways; it is not ordinary photochemical vision.

Lock wording:

> `Pit vipers possess infrared-sensitive pit organs that detect thermal radiation associated with warm objects.`

Do not describe false-color thermal imagery as literal snake subjective vision.

## Bat echolocation
**Class: E**

Use species-qualified `many bats` where appropriate.

Avoid `see with sound` except as analogy.

## Shark/ray electroreception
**Class: E**

Ampullae of Lorenzini and detection of weak environmental electric fields are strongly supported.

Lock wording:

> `Sharks and rays possess specialized electrosensory organs capable of detecting very small changes in environmental electric fields.`

## Bird magnetic orientation
**Class: E/Q**

Behavioral magnetic-compass/orientation evidence is strong across multiple bird species. The precise receptor/transduction mechanisms remain incompletely resolved, with cryptochrome/radical-pair mechanisms strongly investigated and some mechanistic details still open.

Lock wording:

> `Several bird species use information associated with Earth's magnetic field for orientation or navigation; important details of the sensory mechanisms remain an active research area.`

Do not state one molecular mechanism as universally proven.

## Perception as relational/interface
**Class: E + philosophical framing**

Keep close to sensory physiology: physical signals, receptor sensitivities, neural processing.

Avoid sliding into `reality is constructed` metaphysics.

## Instruments extend observational access
**Class: E**

No controversy. Source selectively; individual instrument definitions do not all need primary citations.

## Human body emits heat / contains measurable bioelectric activity
**Class: E**

Use precise terms. ECG/EEG/EMG record electrical consequences of physiological activity. Thermal radiation is measurable.

## Aura/personality-bearing field
**Class: S / unsupported unless independently demonstrated**

Established physiology does not validate a distinct spiritual/personality-bearing field.

Lock:

> `Those measurements establish those phenomena. They do not automatically establish another proposed phenomenon merely because both are described with words such as energy, field, vibration, or frequency.`

## Frequency
**Class: E definition**

Lock:

> `Frequency describes repetition rate. It is not an explanation.`

**Class: E/Q**

**Class: E/Q**

**Class: E/Q**

Valid in many transition models, especially multicellular life cycles; not universal.

**Class: E/Q**

Exists in some multicellular lineages; not universal requirement.

## Mitochondrial endosymbiotic origin
**Class: E**

Strong genomic/phylogenetic support for bacterial ancestry, with mitochondrial genes tracing substantially to alphaproteobacterial ancestry and extensive evolutionary integration/gene transfer.

Lock:

> `Mitochondria descend from bacteria that entered an ancient endosymbiotic relationship with the lineage ancestral to modern eukaryotes. Over evolutionary time, extensive metabolic and genetic integration transformed the relationship.`

Do not say mitochondria are simply free-living bacteria inside modern cells.

## Eusocial colony as superorganism/evolutionary individual
**Class: D**

Legitimate scientific usage, but classification depends on criteria and level analyzed.

## Levels of selection
**Class: D/E framework**

Lock:

> `Selection at a level is evidence that the level matters evolutionarily. It is not, by itself, proof that the level is one organism.`

**Class: S / currently unsupported as biological individuality claim**

Keep negative calibration:

# Chapter 6 — Where Does the Individual End?

## Skin/epithelial surfaces as physical boundaries
**Class: E**

Qualify geometry: gastrointestinal and other epithelial surfaces make `inside/outside` more complicated than skin outline alone.

## Physiological regulation/homeostasis
**Class: E**

Source standard physiology/systems references.

## Immune system as simple self/non-self classifier
**Class: REJECT SIMPLIFICATION**

Modern immunology includes self/non-self discrimination but immune response is context-sensitive and includes tolerance to foreign antigens, responses to altered self, pattern recognition, damage/inflammatory context, and regulatory mechanisms.

Use careful language rather than replacing one universal theory with another.

Lock:

> `Immune recognition contributes to boundary maintenance, but immune responses do not map perfectly onto a binary organismal-membership label.`

## Pregnancy
**Class: E**

Strong literature supports a specialized maternal-fetal interface involving immune tolerance/regulation around genetically distinct fetal/placental tissues.

Lock:

> `Pregnancy involves genetically distinct fetal and placental tissues at a specialized maternal-fetal immune interface.`

Avoid implying the maternal immune system simply `turns off`.

## Tumors
**Class: E**

Tumors derive from host lineages yet can evade, alter, or provoke immune responses. Excellent counterexample to simple self/non-self = membership mapping.

## Transplantation
**Class: E**

Allogeneic tissues can provoke rejection; immunosuppression and tolerance-related mechanisms can modify outcomes.

Do not equate immune acceptance with developmental membership.

## Developmental lineage
**Class: E/Q**

Most ordinary human somatic cells share developmental descent from the zygote, but somatic mutation, mosaicism, chimerism, pregnancy, transplantation, and symbiosis prevent simplistic `same genome = individual` rules.

## Symbiosis
**Class: E/D for individuality inference**

Symbiotic association is real; whether partners constitute one evolutionary individual is a separate criterion-dependent question.

## Turnover and persistence
**Class: E**

Many components can be replaced while organism-level organization persists. Avoid philosophical personal-identity claims.

## Extended phenotype
**Class: E as evolutionary concept / Q in application**

Beaver dams, nests, and similar structures are standard examples of gene effects extending outside the body.

Critical brake:

> `An extended phenotypic effect does not automatically make the external structure an anatomical part of the organism.`

Do not label every human tool an extended phenotype without a specific evolutionary argument.

## Functional coupling
**Class: E as systems concept; D for membership inference**

Functional dependence or reciprocal causal coupling can be measured. It does not by itself establish common biological individuality.

## Boundary perturbation logic
**Class: methodological proposal**

The book may propose perturbation as an operational test. It must be presented as the book's analytical framework, not as an already universally accepted diagnostic of organismhood.

Lock:

> `A proposed boundary becomes scientifically useful when it predicts measurable asymmetries in regulation, exchange, perturbation response, lineage, or shared fate.`

# Claims deliberately removed from publication burden

The short book should avoid unnecessary precision that creates citation burden without improving the argument.

Remove or avoid unless specifically needed:

- exact total human-cell counts;
- exact microbiome-cell counts;
- exact cell-replacement schedules;
- universal `body replaces itself every X years` claims;

- exact geomagnetic receptor mechanism presented as settled;
- decorative numerical frequency values;
- sweeping percentages of microbial contribution;
- universal statements about what every bee, bat, bird, shark, or snake species senses.

Principle:

> **Precision should earn its place. Decorative numbers create fragile prose.**

# Citation density rule

The publication manuscript should not read like a journal article.

Recommended approach:

- narrative prose remains clean;
- consequential factual paragraphs receive endnote markers;
- one endnote can support a tightly related cluster of claims when the cited source genuinely supports the cluster;
- contested claims receive direct qualification in prose, not merely a caveat hidden in an endnote;
- thought experiments are labeled in prose and do not borrow authority from citations beside them.

---

# Claim-language firewall

`measurable electromagnetic activity → aura validated`
