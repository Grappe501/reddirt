# Macroscopic Life — Macroscopic Evidence Ladder v1.0

## Status

**PROJECT-SPECIFIC FORMALIZATION — PROPOSED, TESTABLE, NOT A CONSENSUS BIOLOGICAL TAXONOMY**

The distinctive Macroscopic Life ladder is:

> **MACROSCOPIC PATTERN**
> ↓ *evidence barrier 1*
> **MACROSCOPIC ORGANIZATION**
> ↓ *evidence barrier 2*
> **INTEGRATION**
> ↓ *evidence barrier 3*
> **HIGHER-ORDER INDIVIDUALITY**

The central claim is not that these are four names for increasing complexity. Each transition requires evidence that the weaker level cannot already explain.

---

## 1. Macroscopic Pattern — P

A pattern exists when observations at a proposed macro scale depart reproducibly from an appropriate null or baseline.

Let observations be `X`, a candidate macroscopic description be `Z=f(X)`, and a null model be `M0`.

A first-stage evidence quantity can be written generically as:

`E_P = Score(M_pattern | X) - Score(M0 | X)`

where `Score` may be out-of-sample log likelihood, predictive accuracy, a Bayes factor, information criterion, or another preregistered model-comparison score.

A positive `E_P` establishes a reproducible macroscopic regularity under the tested description.

It does **not** establish organization, integration, agency, intelligence, life, or individuality.

---

## 2. Macroscopic Organization — O

Organization requires more than pattern. Relations among components must do explanatory, predictive, or causal work.

Let `R` describe relationships among components. Compare:

- `M_rel`: a model retaining observed relationships;
- `M_shuffle`: a model preserving relevant component properties while disrupting or randomizing the relationships under test.

Define:

`E_O = Score(M_rel) - Score(M_shuffle)`

If relationship-preserving models repeatedly outperform appropriate relation-destroying controls, the system has evidence of organization at the tested scale.

This is the first barrier where **the arrangement of parts matters beyond the inventory of parts**.

Still:

> **ORGANIZATION ≠ INDIVIDUALITY**

Markets, storms, ecosystems, transport networks, and institutions may contain real organization without thereby becoming biological individuals.

---

## 3. Integration — I

Integration asks whether important macro-level capabilities or states depend on coordinated relationships strongly enough that targeted disruption produces characteristic loss, fragmentation, or reorganization beyond a component-loss baseline.

Let `Y` be a macro-level performance/state variable. Let `do(A_R)` be an intervention that disrupts a relationship or integration pathway, and `do(A_C)` a matched intervention that removes comparable component capacity without specifically disrupting the relational architecture.

One generic integration contrast is:

`E_I = [Y_baseline - Y_do(A_R)] - [Y_baseline - Y_do(A_C)]`

A positive and reproducible `E_I` means relational architecture contributes more than mere component quantity.

Additional integration evidence can include:

- cross-component dependence that survives confound controls;
- synergistic/whole-only information;
- characteristic recovery after network disruption;
- phase or state coordination;
- reduced independence of components;
- failure propagation constrained by the proposed macro architecture.

Integration is not a single universal scalar. Different systems may integrate through different mechanisms.

Still:

> **INTEGRATION ≠ HIGHER-ORDER INDIVIDUALITY**

---

## 4. Higher-Order Individuality — H

Higher-order individuality is the strongest claim. It is not awarded because `P`, `O`, and `I` are large.

It must compete explicitly against lower-level models.

Let:

- `M_A` = independent agents + shared environment;
- `M_B` = coordinated components/institutions;
- `M_C` = distributed macroscopic organization;
- `M_D` = higher-order individual.

Define the model-competition quantity:

`E_H = Score(M_D) - max[Score(M_A), Score(M_B), Score(M_C)]`

A higher-order-individual interpretation becomes scientifically stronger only when `M_D` makes additional successful predictions, survives discriminating interventions, or compresses/explains the evidence better than the strongest lower-level alternative under prespecified penalties for complexity.

No universal numerical threshold is asserted in v1.0. Thresholds must be system-specific and preregistered.

The Eleven Tests remain the current operational battery used to generate the evidence vector supplied to model comparison.

---

## 5. Evidence barriers are asymmetric

Evidence for a later stage ordinarily presupposes evidence for relevant earlier-stage structure, but evidence for an earlier stage never licenses promotion by itself.

In shorthand:

`Pattern ⟹̸ Organization`

`Organization ⟹̸ Integration`

`Integration ⟹̸ Individuality`

The project therefore forbids the common inference:

`interesting macro pattern → organism`

---

## 6. The Ladder as a nested research program

The practical workflow is:

1. **Detect:** Is there a reproducible macro pattern?
2. **Relationally explain:** Do relationships among components add predictive or causal power?
3. **Perturb:** Does disrupting those relationships selectively damage macro function/state?
4. **Compete:** Does a higher-order-individual model outperform serious lower-level alternatives?

This produces a compact scientific rule:

> **DETECT THE PATTERN. PROVE THE ORGANIZATION. PERTURB THE INTEGRATION. MAKE INDIVIDUALITY COMPETE.**

---

## 7. Ancestry of the formalization

The ladder explicitly inherits from established work rather than claiming those ingredients as discoveries:

- pattern formation and self-organization;
- network and systems science;
- causal intervention and model comparison;
- philosophy of mechanisms and higher-level explanation;
- major evolutionary transitions and evolutionary individuality;
- conflict suppression and multilevel selection;
- information-theoretic approaches to multiscale causation.

The project-specific contribution is the **ordered evidentiary hierarchy and its promotion rule**: every arrow is a burden of proof, and no higher-level identity claim is licensed merely by resemblance, scale, or complexity.

## 8. Publication rule

When presented publicly, distinguish:

- **ancestral science:** established ideas on which the ladder depends;
- **Macroscopic Life synthesis:** the four-stage hierarchy;
- **project formalization:** the generic mathematical evidence contrasts above;
- **future work:** system-specific metrics, thresholds, simulations, and empirical validation.

Do not claim priority until a systematic literature-priority review has been completed.
