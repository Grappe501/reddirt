# Pass 01 — Evidence Ledger Update

## Claims hardened

### ML-001 — Different species inhabit substantially different sensory worlds

**Status after Pass 01:** SUPPORTED / FOUNDATION-HARDENED

**Evidence class:** E1

**Supporting systems documented:**
- bat echolocation;
- snake infrared sensing;
- elasmobranch electroreception;
- avian magnetoreception behavior;
- invertebrate polarization vision;
- star-nosed mole tactile specialization.

**Best formulation for Book One:**

> Different organisms extract different classes and ranges of information from the same physical environment. Their experienced and behaviorally useful worlds therefore differ substantially.

**Required caveat:** This does not mean each species occupies a different physical universe; it means sensory access and perceptual construction differ.

---

### ML-002 — Unaided human senses reveal only a subset of physically measurable phenomena

**Status after Pass 01:** SUPPORTED / FOUNDATION-HARDENED

**Evidence class:** E1

**Supporting examples:**
- infrared radiation used by pit-bearing snakes;
- weak electric fields detected by electroreceptive fishes;
- geomagnetic information used behaviorally by migratory animals;
- polarization structure of light detected by many invertebrates;
- ultrasonic echo information exploited by echolocating mammals.

**Best formulation for Book One:**

> Physically real information exists in the human environment that unaided human sensory systems do not directly access but other organisms routinely use.

**Required caveat:** Human technology can measure many such phenomena. The claim is about unaided biological perception, not an absolute limit on human knowledge.

## New derivative claim

### ML-021 — Non-perception is not sufficient evidence of nonexistence

**Claim:** The failure of unaided human senses to detect a physical phenomenon cannot, by itself, establish that the phenomenon does not exist.

**Evidence class:** E1 as a methodological inference from established sensory science.

**Best supporting evidence:** Multiple well-characterized sensory modalities detect real environmental information unavailable to unaided humans.

**Alternative explanation / limitation:** This principle does not positively support any particular unseen phenomenon. A proposed phenomenon still requires independent evidence.

**Intro-book usage:** Yes — central pivot in Act I.

**Required caveat:** Absence of perception is not proof of presence either.

**Status:** supported.

Pass 01 strengthens the epistemic premise of Macroscopic Life without strengthening the literal macro-organism hypothesis directly.

That distinction is intentional and important.
