# Pass 02 — Evidence Ledger Update

## ML-002 — Unaided human senses reveal only a subset of physically measurable phenomena

**Status:** FOUNDATION-HARDENED

Pass 02 adds quantitative and technological support. Approximate human visual sensitivity occupies a limited wavelength band, and human hearing occupies a limited acoustic frequency range. Instrumentation routinely measures physical signals outside those ranges.

Evidence class: E1.

---

## ML-021 — Non-perception is not sufficient evidence of nonexistence

**Status:** FOUNDATION-HARDENED

Pass 02 strengthens the methodological basis: instrumentation and sensory substitution demonstrate that environmental information can exist outside direct biological access and later become measurable or behaviorally useful through translation.

Required reciprocal caveat remains:

**Non-perception is not evidence of presence either.**

---

## ML-022 — Human sensory ranges are physically limited

**Claim:** Human sensory organs respond to bounded ranges and classes of environmental signals rather than functioning as universal physical detectors.

**Evidence class:** E1

**Status:** supported.

**Intro-book usage:** Chapter 3.

---

## ML-023 — Instruments translate inaccessible signals

**Claim:** Scientific instruments can detect physical variables outside unaided human sensory access and encode the measurements into representations humans can analyze.

**Evidence class:** E1

**Status:** supported.

**Required caveat:** Instruments have their own thresholds, assumptions, noise, calibration limits, and resolution.

---

## ML-024 — Sensory substitution can provide usable environmental information through unconventional channels

**Claim:** Humans can learn to use information translated from one sensory domain into another, including visual information encoded as tactile or auditory signals.

**Evidence class:** E1

**Status:** supported.

**Implication:** The channel carrying information can change while environmental structure remains behaviorally useful.

---

## ML-025 — Perceptual experience is constrained by receptor access and neural processing

**Claim:** Human perception depends on selective sensory transduction and subsequent neural processing, integration, attention, and interpretation.

**Evidence class:** E1 for selective transduction/processing; E2 for broader philosophical interpretations of how perceptual experience should be characterized.

**Status:** supported with formulation guardrail.

---

## ML-026 — Phenomena outside current detection capabilities are logically possible

**Claim:** Humanity cannot establish from first principles that all physically real phenomena are already detectable with existing senses or instruments.

**Evidence class:** E5 / epistemological proposition.

**Status:** open logical possibility.

**Critical caveat:** This provides zero positive evidence for any specific proposed phenomenon.

---

# Pass 02 Book Rule

Book One may confidently state:

> Human beings do not possess a universal sensory interface, and science routinely gains access to physical information by translating it into representations our nervous systems can use.

Book One may not infer:

> Therefore a macroscopic organism, aura, hidden dimension, or higher consciousness exists.

That inferential boundary is now formally recorded.
