# Pass 03 — Evidence Ledger Update

## ML-027 — Cross-sections and projections can be accurate but incomplete

**Claim:** A lower-dimensional cross-section or projection can preserve genuine information about a higher-dimensional object while failing to reveal the object's complete structure.

**Evidence class:** E1 — mathematics/geometry.

**Status:** supported.

**Book use:** Chapter 4.

---

## ML-028 — Embedded observers can infer inaccessible structures indirectly

**Claim:** Scientific observers can infer structures they cannot directly inspect from an external or internal vantage point by measuring accessible consequences, signals, and projections.

**Evidence class:** E1.

**Examples:** tomography, seismology, helioseismology, astronomical/cosmological inference.

**Status:** supported.

---

## ML-029 — Higher-dimensional spaces are mathematically coherent

**Claim:** Mathematics can rigorously define spaces with more than three dimensions.

**Evidence class:** E1.

**Status:** supported.

**Required caveat:** mathematical coherence does not establish physical existence.

---

## ML-030 — Extra spatial dimensions appear in serious theoretical physics

**Claim:** Multiple established theoretical-physics research programs employ additional spatial dimensions beyond ordinary three-dimensional experience.

**Evidence class:** E2 for active/theoretical physics.

**Status:** supported as a statement about theory; physical existence unresolved.

**Required caveat:** no claim that such dimensions contain organisms or explain Macroscopic Life.

---

## ML-031 — Humanity is observationally embedded in the universe

**Claim:** Human cosmological observations are made from locations within the universe; humanity has no literal observational platform outside the universe from which to view it as an external object.

**Evidence class:** E1 as observational condition.

**Status:** supported.

---

## ML-032 — A containing system need not be directly observable as a whole by its internal components

**Claim:** It is logically possible for components embedded within a system to access only local states, cross-sections, projections, or effects rather than the containing system as a complete external object.

**Evidence class:** E5 general systems/geometric inference.

**Status:** supported as a logical proposition; not evidence that humanity actually occupies such a macroscopic organism.

---

# Pass 03 Boundary

Book One may state:

> A local view can be accurate without being complete, and science routinely reconstructs inaccessible structures from indirect evidence.

Book One may not state:

> Higher-dimensional or containing organisms therefore exist.

The research burden created by this pass is explicit:

> If Macroscopic Life exists, identify observable signatures that should differ from conventional explanations.
