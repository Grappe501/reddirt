# 3. FINAL EVIDENCE-CLASS LANGUAGE

## CLASS A — OBSERVED / SCIENTIFIC

Purpose: show established measurements, anatomy, physical mechanisms, documented biological phenomena, or source-grounded empirical examples.

Visual behavior:

- conventional scientific clarity;
- named physical quantities;
- sourceable species/system;
- no speculative anatomy;
- no generic glow;
- no invented data;
- explicit simplification where needed.

## CLASS B — MODEL / CONCEPTUAL

Purpose: show analytical relationships, tests, definitions, comparison frameworks, causal logic, or Book One's conceptual models.

Visual behavior:

- visibly diagrammatic;
- no fake instrument readout;
- no pseudo-quantitative precision;
- qualitative ratings labeled explicitly;
- evidence-entry points and failure routes visible.

## CLASS C — HYPOTHESIS / THOUGHT EXPERIMENT

Purpose: widen the reader's model space without implying observation.

Visual behavior:

- persistent hypothesis/thought-experiment labeling;
- open/incomplete frames;
- uncertainty visible;
- no fake scan/microscopy/satellite evidence;
- no invented higher-order anatomy;
- no consciousness shorthand;
- detached-image safety test mandatory.

Locked rule:

> **Artwork cannot promote a Class C claim into Class A in the reader's mind.**

# 11. CROSS-PROTOTYPE CONTRADICTION AUDIT

## Intelligence terminology

Resolved:

Do not use `intelligence` as an automatic synonym for signaling, information processing, adaptation, or consciousness.

Book-wide distinction remains:

**SIGNAL ≠ INFORMATION ≠ INTEGRATION ≠ INTELLIGENCE ≠ AGENCY ≠ CONSCIOUSNESS**

## Memory terminology

Resolved:

Memory requires a persistent physical trace plus later reactivation/retrieval that alters future response.

## Prediction terminology

Resolved:

Reaction is not prediction. Prediction is not prophecy. Anticipatory processes need not be conscious and must be capable of prospective error.

## Repair terminology

Resolved:

Use `coordinated reconstruction` for civilization unless higher-level repair autonomy is independently established.

## Boundary terminology

Resolved:

A drawn edge is not evidence. Boundary means measurable inside/outside asymmetry or differential coupling/constraint appropriate to the claim.

## Energy/frequency terminology

Resolved:

Energy is physical. Frequency names a rate of oscillation/repetition. Resonance requires a defined system, variable, coupling, and measurable consequence.

## Emergence terminology

Resolved:

`Emergence` may name a system-level result; it cannot replace the mechanism connecting components to capability.
