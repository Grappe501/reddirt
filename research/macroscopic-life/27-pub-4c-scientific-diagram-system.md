# MACROSCOPIC LIFE — PUB-4C SCIENTIFIC DIAGRAM SYSTEM

## Status

**PUB-4C COMPLETE — CLASS A SCIENTIFIC DIAGRAM GRAMMAR LOCKED / EMPIRICAL PRODUCTION SPECS ESTABLISHED**

PUB-4C defines how observed science is visualized in Book One. It is deliberately conservative: the scientific diagram system must carry wonder through accuracy rather than visual exaggeration.

Governing rule:

> **Show what is measured, show how it works, show what has been simplified, and stop before the picture claims more than the evidence.**

---

# 1. CLASS A DESIGN GRAMMAR

Every Class A scientific figure should be built from a small set of visual primitives:

- **OBJECT** — cell, membrane, organism, instrument, network node, tissue, etc.
- **BOUNDARY** — physical membrane, anatomical edge, experimental region, network/interface boundary.
- **STATE** — measurable condition at a moment: voltage, concentration, position, phase, activity.
- **FLOW** — movement of matter, energy, ions, signals, or information.
- **INTERACTION** — binding, coupling, exchange, inhibition, activation, mechanical force.
- **TIME** — sequence, persistence, turnover, anticipation, regeneration.
- **SCALE** — explicit physical or observational level.
- **UNCERTAINTY** — range, debated mechanism, simplified relationship, incomplete knowledge.

No decorative element may masquerade as one of these scientific primitives.

---

# 2. VISUAL CAUSALITY RULES

## Arrows

An arrow means one of only four things and must be visually distinguishable in production:

1. physical movement;
2. signal/information transmission;
3. causal/regulatory influence;
4. time/sequence.

Do not use arrows merely to mean `related to.`

## Glow

Glow is prohibited as a generic sign of life, intelligence, bioelectricity, consciousness, energy, or importance.

It may be used only when representing a real luminous/optical measurement or as a clearly non-scientific design emphasis outside evidence-bearing art.

## Fields

A field must be named and physically defined. `Energy field` is not sufficient.

## Gradients

Gradients must indicate what quantity varies: concentration, voltage, temperature, probability, etc.

## Network lines

A line between nodes must represent a defined relationship: physical connection, communication path, interaction, correlation, or modeled coupling.

---

# 3. SCALE RULES

Scientific diagrams should include scale bars when physical dimensions matter.

When exact scale is not useful, label relative levels explicitly: molecular, cellular, tissue, organismal, population/network.

Never place objects of radically different size together without indicating that the figure is schematic.

Never use larger page area to imply greater evolutionary importance.

---

# 4. SIMPLIFICATION LANGUAGE

Use one of these caption phrases when needed:

- `Simplified schematic.`
- `Not to scale.`
- `Representative pathway; additional interactions omitted.`
- `Conceptual arrangement based on established mechanisms.`
- `Species differ; this example shows...`
- `One documented mechanism among several.`

Scientific simplification is acceptable. Hidden simplification is not.

---

# 5. LABEL HIERARCHY

Every empirical figure should support three reading depths:

### LEVEL 1 — 3-second read

The main relationship is obvious from title and major labels.

### LEVEL 2 — 15-second read

Reader understands mechanism/sequence.

### LEVEL 3 — 60-second read

Reader can inspect technical labels, caveats, scale, and source-linked caption.

This prevents scientific accuracy from producing unreadable diagrams.

---

# 6. CLASS A SOURCE STANDARD

Before final art, each empirical figure requires:

- at least one authoritative review or primary source supporting the central mechanism;
- additional source when a figure combines multiple mechanisms or species;
- species/system named where biological variation matters;
- source publication details in provenance registry;
- image/data rights status if adapting existing visual material.

Textbook-common facts still receive provenance in the production registry even if the final book cites them through consolidated notes.

# 11. EMPIRICAL SPEC 04 — MEMBRANE POTENTIAL

## ML-B1-C07-F01 — THE VOLTAGE ACROSS A CELL MEMBRANE

Evidence class: **A**

Claim:
Cells maintain voltage differences across membranes through selective permeability, ion gradients, channels, transporters, and pumps.

Scale:
nanometer membrane thickness / cellular context; schematic not to scale if needed.

Panels:
1. cell and zoom target;
2. lipid membrane with selected ions;
3. ion gradients and channels/pumps;
4. voltage measurement concept.

Required labels:
- inside;
- outside;
- membrane;
- selected ions;
- ion channel;
- transporter/pump;
- membrane potential.

Critical scientific note:
bulk intracellular and extracellular fluids remain nearly electrically neutral; voltage arises from a very small separation/imbalance of charge near the membrane.

Prohibited visual:
a cell filled with positive charge and outside filled with negative charge.

Prohibited inference:
- membrane potential is a mystical life force;
- voltage alone explains cell behavior.

Source targets:
standard cell physiology/electrophysiology references.

# 22. SCIENTIFIC VISUAL ANTI-MYSTICISM TEST

Every empirical visual involving electricity, fields, waves, frequency, information, emergence, or distributed intelligence must answer:

1. What physical quantity is being represented?
2. How is it measured?
3. What carries or implements it?
4. What mechanism connects one state to the next?
5. What claim does the evidence **not** support?

If those questions cannot be answered, the figure is not ready for Class A treatment.

# 24. EMPIRICAL CAPTION STANDARD

Recommended structure:

**Sentence 1:** what the figure physically shows.

**Sentence 2:** mechanism or established relationship.

**Sentence 3:** simplification/variation if important.

**Sentence 4:** chapter relevance.

**Optional final brake:** likely prohibited inference.

Example for membrane potential:

> Cells maintain voltage differences across their membranes through ion gradients, selective permeability, channels, transporters, and pumps. Only a tiny fraction of total charge needs to be separated near the membrane; the bulk fluids remain nearly electrically neutral. This electrical state participates in ordinary cellular regulation. It is not evidence of a hidden life force.
