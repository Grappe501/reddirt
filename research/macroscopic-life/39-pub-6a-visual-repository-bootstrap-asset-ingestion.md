# 3. CHAPTER-CAPTURE RULES

Each chapter capture must satisfy all of these:

1. **One-image comprehension** — a reader can identify the chapter's central idea without reading the whole chapter.
2. **Emotional accuracy** — image matches the chapter's emotional role in the five-act arc.
3. **Evidence honesty** — image cannot make a stronger claim than the chapter.
4. **Detached safety** — screenshot cannot become false evidence.
5. **Eighth-grade clarity** — central idea readable without specialist training.
6. **Audiobook equivalent** — image has a spoken description capable of carrying the same conceptual turn.
7. **Cross-media usefulness** — usable or adaptable for print, ebook, web, presentation, and promotion.
8. **No redundancy requirement** — where an existing hero already fulfills these conditions, it becomes the official chapter capture rather than creating another unnecessary image.
