# 1. Membrane-potential attack

### Attack

Does the chapter teach the common false picture that the inside of a cell is broadly negative and the outside broadly positive?

### Result

**PASS.**

The manuscript explicitly states that bulk intracellular and extracellular fluids remain close to electrically neutral and that comparatively small charge separation near the membrane can produce a measurable voltage difference.

This is consistent with standard membrane biophysics.

### Lock

Do not later replace this explanation with a simplistic `negative inside / positive outside` diagram without the electroneutrality qualification.

# 5. Gap-junction attack

### Attack

Does direct intercellular coupling become a fictional body-wide electrical network?

### Result

**PASS.**

The manuscript states that gap junctions provide **one route** for direct electrical and chemical coupling and immediately qualifies coupling by tissue, developmental state, channel composition, and physiological context.

This is consistent with connexin/gap-junction biology: gap-junction channels can permit direct intercellular transfer of ions and small molecules, but their properties and distribution are regulated and context-dependent.
