# P0-1 — Major evolutionary transitions

## Corrected doctrine

Evolutionary history includes multiple transitions in which entities that once reproduced or persisted with greater independence became incorporated into more integrated higher-level systems. Authors differ over which events belong on canonical lists, and mechanisms differ among cases.

Book One does not depend on one universal sequence.

### Required replacement for Chapter 5 shorthand

> Evolutionary history contains multiple transitions in which entities with greater prior independence became incorporated into more integrated systems. Different transitions involve different mechanisms and degrees of cooperation, conflict mediation, reproduction, heredity, and higher-level individuality. The important point here is not a universal ladder. It is that the organization of biological individuality has changed before.

Do not present genes-to-chromosomes, endosymbiosis, multicellularity, and eusociality as mechanistically equivalent.

# P0-2 — Selection is not individuality

## Locked rule

> **Selection at a level is evidence of evolutionary relevance at that level, not by itself proof of organismal individuality.**

Publication manuscript must distinguish, where relevant, selection-level language from claims about biological individuals, reproducers/replicators, interactors, beneficiaries, and manifestors of adaptation.

### Chapter 5 insertion

> Natural selection can operate on variation associated with more than one organizational level, but evolutionary relevance and organismal individuality are not synonyms. Evidence of selection at a higher level strengthens a case for higher-level organization; it does not by itself establish a new organism.

# P0-3 — Whole-only information

## Final definition

> **Whole-only information is a relational system property: a representation, estimate, or actionable state whose construction depends on distributed measurements or computations such that no single component independently contains the total state, while the integrated result can causally influence downstream system behavior.**

It does not exist independently of physical components. It does not imply a conscious owner or knower.

### Chapter 13 controlling wording

The rhetorical question `What does civilization know that no human knows?` may remain, immediately followed by:

> Here `know` is functional shorthand, not a claim that civilization possesses a conscious mind. The scientific question is whether distributed components construct an integrated representation or actionable state that no isolated component independently contains, and whether that integrated state changes subsequent system behavior.

# P0-4 — Thermodynamics

## Final doctrine

Living organisms are open systems sustained by nonequilibrium biochemical processes. They continuously transform free-energy sources, maintain electrochemical and chemical gradients, organize fluxes, exchange matter and energy with their environments, and dissipate energy.

## Locked line

> **Nonequilibrium thermodynamics helps explain the energetic cost of biological organization; it does not define life by itself.**

Avoid entropy=`disorder`, life `fighting entropy`, or nonequilibrium organization as sufficient evidence of life.

### Chapter 7 controlling replacement

> Organisms are open systems maintained through continuous energy transformation and nonequilibrium biochemical processes. They build and maintain chemical and electrochemical gradients, replace molecules, repair structures, and dissipate energy while exchanging matter and energy with their environments. Nonequilibrium thermodynamics helps explain the energetic cost of biological organization; it does not define life by itself.

### Chapter 15 correction

Replace loose `far from equilibrium` language with `maintains complex nonequilibrium organization through sustained energy throughput`.

# P0-5 — Bioelectricity

## Final doctrine

Bioelectric state is one component of developmental and physiological regulation, interacting with biochemical, genetic, mechanical, geometric, and environmental factors.

Book One may discuss:
- membrane potentials;
- ion channels, transporters, and pumps;
- endogenous currents and fields where measured;
- electrical coupling between cells;
- electrotaxis/galvanotaxis;
- experimentally supported developmental or morphogenetic roles.

Book One must not imply a universal bioelectric code, unknown long-range communication channel, or extrapolation from tissue-scale electrical phenomena to civilization-scale mechanisms.

### Locked line

> **The stranger the biological result, the more carefully we should insist on an ordinary physical mechanism.**

# P0-6 — Target-state language

## Final definition

> **A target state is shorthand for a state or range toward which a system's regulatory dynamics tend after perturbation; it does not imply conscious representation, intention, or knowledge unless those properties are independently demonstrated.**

### Chapter 9 correction

The phrase `acceptable anatomical state` is permitted only after the functional definition above.

Replace rhetorical `the organism knows what is missing` formulations with mechanistic descriptions of remaining tissue state, positional information, feedback, and restoration dynamics.

# P0-7 — Civilization-scale repair

## Final rule

Rebuilding by humans is not automatically repair by civilization as a higher-order individual.

Use **coordinated reconstruction** as the default term.

Stronger system-level repair evidence requires that larger architecture:
1. detects disruption through distributed sensing;
2. mobilizes resources through established system mechanisms;
3. reconstructs function without one external controller;
4. restores identifiable higher-level variables or organization;
5. is better predicted by the higher-level repair model than by independent local decisions alone.

### Chapter 14 re-rating

**Repair / coordinated reconstruction**
- Property present: MODERATE TO STRONG.
- Evidence for higher-order individuality: WEAK TO MODERATE / AMBIGUOUS.

# P0-8 — Prediction ownership

## Three levels

1. **Component prediction** — an individual agent predicts.
2. **Institution-level prediction** — an organized subsystem predicts.
3. **Integrated civilization-level predictive loop** — distributed sensing/modeling becomes sufficiently integrated with larger-scale action that a higher-level predictive description earns explanatory value.

## Locked rule

> **Capability distributed across civilization is not automatically a capability of civilization as one agent.**

Chapter 11/14 must treat sophisticated forecasting as a strong macroscopic property but ambiguous evidence for civilization-level agency.

# P0-10 — Macroscopic Organization

## Final neutral definition

> **A macroscopic organization is a system with measurable organization at scales above its constituent agents, without implication of organismhood, biological individuality, intelligence, agency, or consciousness.**

Life-associated properties must then be evaluated independently.

This term may not become a rhetorical synonym for `almost alive`.

---

# P0-11 — Three-stage reality ladder

The manuscript must distinguish:

### Stage 1 — Macroscopic pattern
A higher-scale regularity exists.

### Stage 2 — Macroscopic organization
The regularity is maintained through integrated causal architecture.

### Stage 3 — Higher-order individuality
The organization displays sufficient converging evidence—boundary, persistence, component integration, conflict mediation, regulation, reproduction/evolutionary fate, or other system-defining processes—to justify treatment as an individual.

## Locked rule

> **Explanatory usefulness is evidence for higher-order scientific reality but is not sufficient evidence for biological individuality.**

---

# P0-12 — Boundary exclusion criterion

A proposed boundary must generate measurable asymmetry in at least one domain such as interaction density, resource exchange, regulatory coupling, information flow, perturbation propagation, shared fate, persistence, or selection.

If no measurable inside/outside asymmetry can be demonstrated, the boundary is provisionally observer-imposed.

Fuzzy boundaries are allowed. Arbitrary boundaries are not.

# P0-13 — Memory

## Locked criterion

**PAST EVENT → PHYSICAL TRACE → PERSISTENCE → RETRIEVAL/REACTIVATION → ALTERED FUTURE RESPONSE**

Persistent state alone is insufficient.

Do not label every epigenetic state `memory`. Institutional and technological memory are functional analogies implemented differently from biological memory.

# P0-14 — Prediction

Distinguish:
- anticipatory physiology;
- learned prediction;
- engineered forecasting;
- evolutionary adaptation.

Do not describe evolution itself as consciously or literally predicting future conditions.

## Locked rule

> **Prediction must be capable of prospective error.**

# P0-15 — Intelligent-cell thought experiment

## Required Chapter 1 and Chapter 16 clarification

> **The intelligent cell is an epistemic model of an embedded observer. It is not a claim about actual cell cognition, and it is not evidence that the cell-to-human relationship is equivalent to the human-to-civilization relationship. Its only job is to show how an embedded component can have incomplete access to organization at another scale.**

This prevents the central metaphor from quietly preloading the conclusion.

---

# P0-16 — Conflict suppression / cancer analogy

## Locked language

> **The cancer analogy is useful only at the abstract organizational level: lower-level processes can increase their own persistence or reproduction while reducing higher-level viability. It is not an identity claim between social conflict and neoplasia.**

Do not call war, corporations, political conflict, pollution, or crime `cancer` in the scientific argument.

# P0-17 — Consciousness chain

Keep the categories explicitly separate:

**SIGNAL ≠ INFORMATION ≠ INTEGRATION ≠ INTELLIGENCE ≠ AGENCY ≠ CONSCIOUSNESS**

Book One makes no higher-order consciousness claim.

# P0-18 — Final hypothesis correction

The Chapter 16 hypothesis remains viable, but its evidentiary logic is tightened:

> **Macroscopic Life is the hypothesis that living or life-like organization may occur in nested hierarchies at scales larger than familiar individual organisms. A candidate higher-order individual must not be inferred from resemblance, complexity, connectivity, or useful macro-description alone. It must earn individual-level treatment through converging causal evidence that the proposed higher-level organization maintains historically continuous, bounded or asymmetrically coupled, self-affecting organization in ways that improve explanation and prediction beyond independent components plus shared environment. Strong biological claims additionally require appropriate evidence concerning reproduction, heredity, and selection at the proposed level.**

This is now the controlling Book One formulation.
