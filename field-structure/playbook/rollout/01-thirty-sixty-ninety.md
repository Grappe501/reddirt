# 30 · 60 · 90 day rollout

This mirrors the exponential model: **launch leads**, **deepen local units**, **lock neighborhood density**.

## First 30 days — launch lead teams

**Goal:** credible county (or region) **lead triads** operating weekly huddles.

### Weeks 1–2

- Recruit **three coordinators** per lead unit; train on lane pages in this playbook.
- Host first **weekly huddle**; assign each lane a **public task** due before next huddle.
- Establish reporting path to campaign coordinator.

### Weeks 3–4

- Execute **first coordinated statewide/countywide moment** (digital + optional live).
- Spin out **first child triads** where geography demands it.

**Exit criteria:** named coordinators, weekly huddle rhythm, first measurable outputs in each lane.

## 60 days — expand to city and precinct teams

**Goal:** local units cover meaningful population slices without central bottleneck.

- Each county triad has **at least one** city/town child triad with clear turf.
- Precinct teams begin where election geography matters most (per HQ priorities).
- Relational **Power of 5** sprints become normalized, not heroic.

**Exit criteria:** fractal map shows **2+ layers active** in majority of target counties.

## 90 days — neighborhood density + coordinated events

**Goal:** repeated presence in walkable zones and repeatable event formats.

- Block/neighborhood teams operating in priority zones.
- **Event + social + relational** packages run as a single monthly cycle.
- Metrics reviewed honestly; weak formats cut.

**Exit criteria:** stable weekly motion, documented hand-offs, recruitment pipeline for new coordinators.

## If you are behind pace

- **Shrink geography** until throughput returns.
- **Cut** lowest-yield activity before asking for more volunteer hours.
