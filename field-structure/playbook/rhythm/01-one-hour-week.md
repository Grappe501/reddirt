# The one-hour week — how lanes budget time

This model assumes volunteers have busy lives. The campaign asks for **focused throughput**, not heroic overwork.

## Baseline (per person)

Target **~60 minutes per week** in defined tasks for your lane.

## Example split by role

### Events coordinator

- 20 minutes: update run-of-show + assignments.
- 25 minutes: partner/host outreach or attendee follow-ups.
- 15 minutes: debrief note + next-week milestone.

### Social coordinator

- 20 minutes: localize kit + schedule posts.
- 20 minutes: capture proof + uplift locals.
- 20 minutes: inbox/DMs + escalate questions.

### Power of 5 / relational coordinator

- 15 minutes: training tip in huddle.
- 35 minutes: relational touches or follow-ups (split across the week).
- 10 minutes: list hygiene + next-week targets.

## Team rule of three

Only **one** person must attend the **weekly huddle** — rotate intentionally so burnout doesn’t route through a single human.

## If someone misses a week

- Default: **do not shame** — reassign open tasks in the huddle.
- If two consecutive misses: campaign coordinator checks in for capacity, not guilt.

## Escalate time debt

If lanes repeatedly need **>90 minutes** weekly, the geography is probably **too big** — split the triad per fractal rules.
