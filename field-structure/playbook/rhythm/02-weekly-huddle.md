# Weekly huddle agenda (30 minutes)

Rotate note-taker. One person runs time. **Decisions** end in a named owner + a due date.

## 0:00–0:05 — Temperature check

- Wins from last week (1 each, max 1 sentence)

## 0:05–0:10 — Numbers (fast)

Review the smallest set that matches your layer (adjust with HQ):

- Teams launched / in formation
- Volunteers newly recruited or trained
- Contacts or conversations completed (define consistently)
- Events executed or scheduled
- Social proof-of-work units shipped

## 0:10–0:18 — Lane updates (3 minutes each)

Order: **Events → Social → Relational**

Each coordinator answers:

1. What shipped?
2. What is blocked?
3. What is the **one** priority before next huddle?

## 0:18–0:24 — Cross-lane coordination

- Pick **one joint moment** in the next 14 days (event + relational invites + social promotion).
- Confirm **single ask** for the public (avoid seven competing CTAs).

## 0:24–0:28 — Leadership development

- Name **one person** to recruit or coach toward a coordinator lane.
- Assign a **shadow** for an upcoming task.

## 0:28–0:30 — Close

- Confirm **next huddle** time + agenda owner.
- Push notes to your single source of truth channel/doc.

## Optional add-on for state/county layers (+15 minutes, monthly)

- Fractal audit: where are we **missing** a triad?
- Retire busywork that didn’t recruit last month.
