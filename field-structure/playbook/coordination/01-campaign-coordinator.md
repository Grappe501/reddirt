# Campaign coordinator — coaching & accountability

The **campaign coordinator** is not a fourth overloaded lane — they **coach the triad** and align metrics.

## Core functions

1. **Coaching:** help coordinators run clean huddles, recruit replacements, and de-escalate conflict.
2. **Accountability:** ensure commitments have owners; track follow-through without micromanaging tone.
3. **Metrics support:** make numbers honest and lightweight; remove vanity dashboards.

## Standard support touches

### Weekly (15–30 minutes per triad)

- Read huddle notes.
- Remove blockers that require HQ (tools, legal, approvals).
- Pick **one growth intervention** (train a host, split a geography, tune messaging).

### Monthly

- Review fractal map: **coverage gaps** vs **duplicate teams**.
- Celebrate **lane excellence** publicly; coach **lane drift** privately.

## Escalation ladder (template)

1. Lane coordinator attempts local resolution.
2. Triad decision in huddle with documented owner.
3. Campaign coordinator engages HQ for anything risky or unclear.

## What coordinators should never do alone

- Legal claims, process assertions, or confrontations with institutional opponents.
- Volunteer HR-style conversations without a second person / policy where required.

## Success looks like

Coordinators say: **“We know our lane, our geography, and our weekly plan.”**
