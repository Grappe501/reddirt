# Community region leadership — training scaffold

For **identity- and community-native** dashboards (Muslim Community Region, Spanish, Marshallese, county party shells) — same **triad discipline**, partner-shaped titles.

## Shared operating rules

- [ ] **Lane boundaries** hold: Events still runs logistics; Social still vets claims; P5 / VR still protects relational integrity.
- [ ] **Partner review** gates public copy until leadership says “live.”
- [ ] Escalate media or legal heat **up**, not sideways to other communities.

## Leadership training path (30-60-90)

**30 days — orientation**
- [ ] Read **Self-Building Team System**.
- [ ] Walk each dashboard tab once — note where your community needs different labels.
- [ ] Schedule one **upstream** huddle with campaign coordinator.

**60 days — execution**
- [ ] Run **one** full event lifecycle (plan → invite → debrief) with Events playbook support.
- [ ] Publish **one** responsible social story with Social approval.
- [ ] Coach **one** downstream triad or precinct cell.

**90 days — multiplication**
- [ ] Hand off a **documented** rhythm (monthly meeting, youth lane, women’s lane) to a deputy.
- [ ] Submit KPI rollup honestly — gaps are data, not shame.

## What good looks like

Partners see **their** fingerprints on materials — not a generic statewide mail merge with a new banner.
