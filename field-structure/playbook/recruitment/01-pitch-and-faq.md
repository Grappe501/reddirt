# Volunteer recruitment pitch & FAQ

## Core invitation (keep this honest)

> We’re not asking for a heroic amount of time. We’re asking you to **pick a lane** — events, social, or relational — spend about **an hour a week**, and help build teams that **grow exponentially** because the structure stays simple.

## Role “micro-pitches”

### Events

> I’m looking for someone who enjoys bringing people together — coffees, trainings, partner rooms. You’d own a calendar that **creates on-ramps** for new volunteers.

### Social

> I need someone who can keep our local digital presence **human** — local faces, local places, clear asks — without getting dragged into endless arguments online.

### Relational / Power of 5

> This is for people who like **real conversations**. You’ll help volunteers talk to people they already know — and recruit new anchors responsibly.

## FAQ

### Do I have to be political online?

No. Social lane can be collaborative with volunteers who post from personal accounts with consent and coaching.

### What if I can only help sometimes?

Pick **one lane** and protect a **weekly hour**. If life happens, we rotate coverage — but we don’t guilt people.

### Do I need experience?

No. Coordinators get **scripts, templates, and training**. Experience helps; willingness matters more.

### What if my area already has activists?

Great — we’re not competing. We’re offering a **repeatable container** so work scales.

### Can two people share a lane temporarily?

Yes as a bridge, **not** as a permanent fog. Name a single DRI before day 14.

## Call to action

Ask: “**Which lane fits you best for the next 30 days — events, social, or relational?**”

Then schedule **first task** within 48 hours.
