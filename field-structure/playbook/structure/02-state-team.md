# State team — responsibilities & cadence

## Purpose

Set statewide **standards**, **cadence**, and **support** so county teams can clone the three-person model without improvisation.

## The state triad (lane ownership)

### Events coordinator

- Maintain a **90-day state event arc** (tour stops, trainings, volunteer intensives, coordinated days of action).
- Publish a **month-at-a-glance** calendar slice counties can fork locally.
- Run or delegate **statewide trainings** for event leads (venue logistics, safety, accessibility basics, voter education posture).

### Social coordinator

- Own the **content calendar skeleton** (themes, hashtags where appropriate, escalation paths for corrections).
- Ship **weekly social kits**: quote cards, story prompts, “ask a friend” copy blocks.
- Train locals on **voice, compliance posture, and escalation** (“ask HQ, don’t improvise legal claims”).

### Power of 5 / relational coordinator

- Define **relational norms**: contact quality, respectful persistence, data hygiene, privacy boundaries.
- Run **Power of 5 training** (how to map a circle, how to debrief a conversation, how to recruit a new coordinator).
- Ensure each county has a named **relational escalation** path (not venting in public channels).

## Weekly state checklist (about 1 hour each, not all on one person)

- **Events**: confirm next two statewide milestones; update counties on materials due dates.
- **Social**: publish kit; collect top 3 local posts worth amplifying.
- **Relational**: review pipeline of new volunteer leaders; assign follow-ups.

## Monthly state checklist

- Update the **playbook pages** when local feedback reveals confusion.
- Scoreboard: teams launched, volunteers recruited, events executed, posts shipped, relational touches logged (definitions in Metrics).

## Escalation

Anything touching **legal compliance**, **public controversy**, or **sensitive volunteer situations** goes to campaign coordinator / HQ per your ops protocol — not resolved in DMs.
