# Precinct team — responsibilities & cadence

## Purpose

Translate general support into **ballot-box geography** without turning volunteers into data clerks.

## Precinct triad principles

1. **Respect the turf** — precinct work is about being known, not loud.
2. **Schedule realism** — many touches happen in **driveways, porches, gyms, and PTA lines**, not formal canvasses only.
3. **Log light** — capture outcomes that help the team learn; avoid hoarding sensitive notes.

## Events coordinator (precinct lens)

- Plan **micro-events**: porch coffees, “meet at the poll early vote reminder” clusters, partner tabling.
- Coordinate with county on **legally compliant** voter education materials; do not invent process claims.

### Month template

- Week 1: visibility **where people already gather**.
- Week 2: follow-up **relational circle** expansion from attendees.
- Week 3: partner-hosted **low-friction** room.
- Week 4: retrospective + **next month** anchor.

## Social coordinator (precinct lens)

- Hyperlocal content: **roads, schools, businesses people recognize**.
- Encourage **neighbor-to-neighbor** shares — the post is a pretext for a conversation.

## Power of 5 / relational coordinator (precinct lens)

- Build a **neighbor map**: trusted connectors first.
- Use short scripts focused on **listening** and **clear next steps** (volunteer signup, event attendance).
- Track “conversation outcomes” at a coarse level: *supportive / curious / opposed / needs follow-up* — exact schemas live with HQ.

## Weekly precinct checklist

- Confirm **who is covering which blocks** this week.
- Run **15 minutes** of huddle on blockers (materials, escalations, safety questions).
- Close loops on last week’s **promised follow-ups** before adding new names.
