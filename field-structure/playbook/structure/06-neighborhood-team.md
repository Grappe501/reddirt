# Neighborhood / block team — responsibilities & cadence

## Purpose

Achieve **density** — repeated, friendly visibility in a walkable zone.

## Typical shape

- **Block / zone lead** (often also one of the three coordinators).
- **Events**: porch series, yard sign density plans (where allowed), “neighbor hours”.
- **Social**: micro-stories; highlight neighbors doing the work.
- **Relational**: Power of 5 inside **one church roster, one apartment stack, one subdivision loop**, etc.

## Weekly micro-plan (30–60 minutes of team time)

1. **Map** — which doors/porches/community hooks are “warm” this week?
2. **Do** — 3–5 live conversations (voice or in-person) with clear next steps.
3. **Show** — one visible community moment (tabling, coffee, group cleanup, partner room).
4. **Tell** — one localized story online that invites others in.

## Safety & norms

- **Never argue on doorsteps** — de-escalate, thank, leave.
- Pair newer volunteers when possible.
- Follow HQ guidance on **photography, minors, private property,** and **data handling**.

## When you outgrow the block

Spin a second triad when warm contacts exceed **weekly follow-through capacity** — not when drama spikes.

## Hand-off discipline

If someone moves or steps back, **hand off lists in a structured way** (campaign coordinator witnesses if required by your program).
