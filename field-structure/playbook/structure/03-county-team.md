# County team — responsibilities & cadence

## Purpose

Translate statewide standards into **weekly county motion**: local events, local digital rhythm, local relational depth.

## County triad lanes

### Events coordinator

- Build a **county event lane** that matches community patterns (weekday coffees vs weekend canvass-adjacent events, fairs, partner-hosted rooms).
- Maintain a **4-week rolling plan** so volunteers can schedule around life.
- Recruit **event hosts** and **site captains**; never be the only person who knows the run-of-show.

### Social coordinator

- Run **county accounts or sub-pages** where relevant; otherwise coordinate trusted volunteers’ personal posting cadence.
- Localize the social kit: **names of places**, **local allies**, **local civic hooks** — not generic national copy.
- Track **two numbers weekly**: reach (rough) and **inbound asks** (“how do I volunteer?”).

### Power of 5 / relational coordinator

- Maintain a living list of **relational nodes**: clergy networks, union stewards, coaches, small business hubs, alumni circles.
- Set a **weekly relational goal** that is achievable with one hour lanes (see Relational playbook).
- Coach volunteers on **scripts and boundaries**; celebrate logged conversations, not just activity.

## Weekly county checklist

- Pick **one anchor event** in the next 14 days with a named owner.
- Publish **minimum social cadence** agreed for the county (quality over spam).
- Confirm **relational circles** are mapped for each captain — no orphan leads.

## Launch checklist (first 14 days)

- Name **geographies** (town clusters, precinct groupings) and assign parent captains.
- Host a **county onboarding huddle** using the weekly agenda template.
- Create a **single source of truth** for times/locations/links (one doc or one channel policy).

## Common failure mode

County leads try to **personally cover every town** — fix by spinning city/precinct triads early.
