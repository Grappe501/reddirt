# Self-Building Team System

The campaign’s job is to **encourage**, answer hard questions, publish weekly priorities, approve messaging, support major events, and **watch statewide GOTV readiness**. Your job as volunteers is to **recruit**, onboard, train, launch teams, place people downstream, track KPIs, and build capacity — without waiting to be managed.

## Principles

1. **Start with one person.** Every triad begins with a single coordinator who invites the next.
2. **Build to three lanes.** Events, Social, and Power of 5 / VR keep work balanced.
3. **Stay geographic.** Anchor work in real places neighbors recognize.
4. **Keep teams small.** Small actions, statewide impact.
5. **Launch more teams, not bigger teams.** Every team builds another team.
6. **Place people where they fit best.** When a Power of 5 list is full, route relationships downstream.
7. **Train the next team as you build it.** Train as you grow — don’t hand off a cold dashboard.
8. **Report simple numbers every week.** Escalate questions, not routine work.
9. **Escalate questions, not routine work.** Use “Ask the Campaign” for conflicts, legal, or messaging you can’t answer locally.
10. **Build toward GOTV readiness.** The scorecard is a target, not a judgment.

## Language we use

- Build the next team.
- Place people where they fit.
- More teams, not bigger teams.
- Get GOTV ready.

Avoid making volunteers feel surveilled or overwhelmed — the system should feel like a **toolbox**, not a boss.
