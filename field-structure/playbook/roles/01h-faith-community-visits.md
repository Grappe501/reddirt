# Faith community visits — respectful coordination

**Goal:** When feasible, build toward **a church, synagogue, or mosque visit on many weekends** — always **by invitation**, never as a surprise extraction.

## Checklist (track in Events pipeline)

- [ ] **Faith leader identified** (correct title / spelling).
- [ ] **Contact made** through trusted introducer when possible.
- [ ] **Visit requested** with clear purpose and duration.
- [ ] **Scheduled** with dress / protocol notes.
- [ ] **Follow-up completed** (thank-you; any promises logged).

## Principles

- **Listen more than you speak** in someone else’s sacred space.
- **Do not** treat worship as a photo backdrop without explicit permission.
- **Escalate** interfaith sensitivities to HQ; volunteers do not litigate theology in the parking lot.
- **Registration and partisan asks** stay outside worship unless leadership explicitly welcomes them.

## Preparation

- [ ] Confirm **arrival time** vs. public service time (some visits are office hours only).
- [ ] Brief Kelly/surrogate on **local history** of the congregation — avoid blunders.
- [ ] Offer **service project** or **supply drive** partnerships when aligned (no quid pro quo).

## Script — initial request (email / call outline)

“I’m [name], volunteering with [campaign]. We’d be honored to visit briefly to introduce ourselves and listen — **on your terms and timing**. If a conversation with [Kelly / surrogate works better outside service, we’re grateful either way.”

## What good looks like

- Leaders feel **respected**, not mined for lists.
- Follow-up is **prompt** and **honest** — if we promised a resource, it ships.

## Automation note
