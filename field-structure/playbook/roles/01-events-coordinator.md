# Events coordinator — lane guide

## Mission

Create **high-trust gatherings** that move people from curiosity → participation → leadership.

## Weekly responsibilities (minimum viable)

1. **Plan** the next public or partner-hosted touch with a named owner for each function.
2. **Recruit** attendees through relational lists and social promotion working *with* the other two coordinators.
3. **Document** what happened: attendance estimate, signups, follow-ups owed.

## Event quality checklist (fast)

- **Purpose** in one sentence (“recruit precinct captains”, “train Power of 5”, etc.).
- **Agenda** with times and who leads each block.
- **Signup** path tested on a phone (short URL or QR).
- **Accessibility** basics: seating, hearing, scent/smoke considerations when applicable.
- **Safety** plan for public spaces — two-person rule when appropriate.

## Monthly responsibilities

- Train one **new event host** intentionally.
- Retire formats that don’t recruit; double down on what does.

## Metrics you own (with ops support)

- Events executed vs planned.
- Volunteer signups attributed to events.
- Follow-up completion rate within 72 hours.
- County fundraising rows moving: host → date → goal → raised → follow-up (see Fundraising Event Toolkit).
- Faith-visit and County Clerk pipelines honest (no “claimed complete” without thank-you sent).

## Hand-offs

Anything touching permits, venue contracts, or legal questions goes **up** to campaign coordinator / HQ per protocol.

## Deep dive

Open the **Events · hosting playbook** when you are inside seven days of a touch.

**Operating manual (web hub):** `/volunteer/resources/events-lane` — table of contents for house parties, county fundraisers through September, weekend immersions, two-day city visits, faith communities, travel rhythm, and automation intents for the Action Queue.

**Train others:** start a new host on **House party playbook**, then have them shadow **County Clerk** and **Faith visit** checklists before they fly solo.
