# Power of 5 / VR — registration events and polling-place readiness

Execution layer for the **P5 / VR coordinator** when Events runs registration-forward touches.

## Registration event operations (2-hour table model)

**Before**
- [ ] Confirm **forms / links** with HQ; print QR to `/volunteer` and official SOS pages as directed.
- [ ] Train volunteers on **what we do not** do (no legal advice; escalate).
- [ ] Weather and lighting plan for outdoor tables.

**During**
- [ ] **Two-person minimum** at busy tables — one greeter, one navigator.
- [ ] Track **honest counts**: conversations started, cards handed, assists logged.
- [ ] Capture **follow-up owners** for anyone who needs a call later (no SSN or full DOB on clipboards).

**After (48h)**
- [ ] Thank partners and venue hosts.
- [ ] Enter outcomes into rollup; compare to **goal**.
- [ ] Schedule **next** touch before energy fades.

## Community outreach socials (non-table)

- [ ] Pair Social coverage with **one relational ask**: “Who should sit with us next time?”
- [ ] Debrief **three** standout conversations with Events — feed house party pipeline.

## Polling-place readiness (high level)

- [ ] Know **who** owns poll worker recruitment vs. voter info — don’t blur lanes.
- [ ] Build **FAQ** from official sources only; date-sensitive pages checked weekly during deadlines.
- [ ] Monitor **long lines** reports; route to leadership, don’t improvise legal guidance.

## What good looks like

- VR events produce **named follow-ups**, not just paper slips.
- Coordinators can **teach** a new volunteer to run the table in one shadow shift.
