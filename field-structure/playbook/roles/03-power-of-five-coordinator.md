# Power of 5 / relational coordinator — lane guide

## Mission

Turn relationships into **durable organizing capacity** — not one-night guilting.

## Weekly responsibilities

1. Train volunteers on **conversation structure** and **boundaries**.
2. Ensure lists move from “names” → **dated follow-ups**.
3. Recruit **new relational anchors** continuously.

## Relational standards

- Consent-based contact; respect “no” and “not now”.
- Accurate representation of how to get involved — **no improvising** complex rules.

## Coaching pattern (10 minutes in huddle)

- Name **one win** and **one lesson** from the week.
- Pick **one script** to practice live for 3 minutes.

## Metrics you own

- Relational touches completed vs target (define conservative targets locally).
- New volunteer leaders discovered via relationships.
- Follow-up SLA (e.g., 72-hour close rate).
