# Festivals, fairs, and being Kelly’s local guide

## Festivals and fairs

- [ ] **Identify** top 10 annual draws in your county (fair boards, Pride, ethnic heritage, July 4, Christmas parade).
- [ ] **Classify** each: booth possible, walkthrough only, partnership table, or **observe** year-one.
- [ ] **Permits:** start early; Events escalates contracts to HQ.
- [ ] **Rain plan** and **volunteer shifts** (2-hour blocks max without relief).

**What good looks like:** you’re known as **prepared**, not as someone who ghosts mid-fair.

## Building around local events

- [ ] Place **home meet-and-greets** within 48h of the fair so energy converts.
- [ ] Use **P5** to fill rooms with people already in town for the festival.
- [ ] Social posts **celebrate the community first**; campaign mention follows policy.

## Being Kelly’s local guide

**You translate place.** Kelly shouldn’t guess which door is awkward, where elders sit first, or which street has bad lighting.

### Local guide checklist

- [ ] **One-pager** for the day: timeline, addresses, phones, parking, bathrooms.
- [ ] **Name five people** Kelly should know in the room and **why**.
- [ ] **Name three landmines** (family feuds, recent tragedies, toxic local beefs) — private briefing only.
- [ ] **Food:** preferences, allergies, and timing (never skip meals on long days).
- [ ] **Kid dynamics:** if families present, where quiet space is.
- [ ] **Exit cue:** how we end cleanly without insulting hosts.

### Beginner vs advanced

- **Beginner:** accurate driving directions and on-time doors.
- **Advanced:** anticipates **press** questions, redirects **bad faith** provocateurs calmly, debriefs captures with Social same night.
