# Three roles — operational summary

## Events coordinator

**Outcome:** people gather in real life (or tightly facilitated virtual rooms) in ways that recruit, train, and retain volunteers.

**Weekly core:** confirm run-of-show, assignments, follow-up plan.

## Social media coordinator

**Outcome:** disciplined digital presence that recruits and informs — without chasing trends that dilute message control.

**Weekly core:** ship kit localization, capture proof-of-work, amplify locals.

## Power of 5 / relational coordinator

**Outcome:** compounding trust through intentional conversation and structured follow-up.

**Weekly core:** train, debrief, ensure lists don’t rot on paper.

## Lane boundaries (non-negotiable)

- Events does **not** silently become “random ops” for relational follow-up.
- Social does **not** improvise sensitive claims to win an argument thread.
- Relational does **not** hoard information — escalate conflicts of fact to HQ.
