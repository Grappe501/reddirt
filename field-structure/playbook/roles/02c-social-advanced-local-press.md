# Social lane — advanced local media, press graphics, distribution

Use after you have completed **Social · amplifier playbook** basics.

## Advanced Canva patterns

- [ ] **Master file** per county: duplicate slides instead of rebuilding from scratch weekly.
- [ ] **Safe zones** — export both **1:1** and **9:16** from the same design where possible.
- [ ] **Quote cards** — max 20 words on image; full quote in caption with attribution rules from HQ.
- [ ] **Press-safe crops** — avoid busy backgrounds behind Kelly cutouts; leave lower-third space when asked.

## Local media outreach (earned)

- [ ] Maintain **10-row sheet**: outlet, beat reporter (if known), email, last pitch date, outcome.
- [ ] Pitch **community stories** (service, civics, turnout help) — not opponent dumps unless comms directs.
- [ ] **72-hour rule:** if a reporter asks a legal or policy question you don’t know, **say so** and route to HQ.

## Press graphics packet (when comms requests)

- [ ] **Headshot** from approved library only.
- [ ] **Logo lockup** placement per brand guide.
- [ ] **Readable at thumbnail** — test on phone before send.

## What good looks like

- Locals see **their** town in the feed, not generic national churn.
- You can produce a **clean event graphic** in under 30 minutes when date/time are firm.
