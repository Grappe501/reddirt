# Two-day city immersion model

**Standard rhythm:** target **2–3 deep immersions per week** nationally; each immersion = **two consecutive days in one city / metro**.

Pair with **Travel rhythm model** so volunteers and Kelly have sustainable pacing.

## Purpose

Stack **high-trust** touches (clerks, leaders, students, homes, faith) without living in a car. Exit with **named follow-ups**, not just photos.

## Day A — suggested flow

- [ ] **Morning:** County Clerk / elections office touch (professional; confirm appointment). See **County Clerk visit checklist**.
- [ ] **Late morning:** Coffee with **2–4 local leaders** (rotate industries and communities).
- [ ] **Midday:** Lunch with **stakeholders** (small business, labor, civic — per strategy).
- [ ] **Afternoon:** **Student or youth** surface (campus, training, service partner) — coordinate Youth lane.
- [ ] **Afternoon alternate:** Ribbon cutting or **local business** tour when on calendar.
- [ ] **Evening:** **Community event** OR first **home meet-and-greet**.
- [ ] **Late:** **Fundraising reception** or second small gather only if energy and compliance allow.

## Day B — suggested flow

- [ ] **Morning:** **Faith community** visit when scheduled (see Faith playbook) OR second-chance coffee for missed leaders.
- [ ] **Late morning:** **Second home gather** or listening session.
- [ ] **Midday:** **Lunch** with precinct captains-in-training or county party leadership.
- [ ] **Afternoon:** **Festival / fair** walkthrough OR **volunteer training** block.
- [ ] **Early evening:** **4–5 micro meet-and-greets** are acceptable as **split surrogate** model if Kelly is single-tracked elsewhere.
- [ ] **Debrief:** 20 minutes — what shifted, who owns follow-up.

## Staffing pattern

- **Road lead** owns clock and wayfinding.
- **Events** owns host connectors and room readiness.
- **Social** owns capture plan and **same-day** authentic post when approved.
- **P5 / VR** owns signup table logic and relational debrief list.

## What good looks like

- **Clerk visit** moves state: contacted → scheduled → completed → thank-you logged.
- **At least two** distinct neighborhood **home** touches OR one home + one high-quality listening session.
- **Student** block ≠ throwaway photo — named advisor or student captain follows up.
