# Women’s Outreach — family-centered events and listening sessions

Cross-lane execution guide for **Women’s Outreach leads** working with **Events** and **Social**. Aligns with county and community-region dashboards.

## Principles

- Design for **caregiver logistics** — timing, parking, clarity on kids welcome, feeding expectations.
- **Listening** is a standalone win; not every room needs a speech.
- Escalate safety or harassment issues **immediately** — don’t handle alone.

## Family-centered outreach checklist

- [ ] **Venue** — bathrooms, changing space, stroller access noted on invite.
- [ ] **Program** — optional kids’ corner only with **vetted** volunteers and signed-up parents.
- [ ] **Length** — default shorter than all-adult events unless hosts insist.

## Listening session (90 minutes)

- [ ] **Opening** — purpose, ground rules, consent on note-taking.
- [ ] **Rounds** — timed; pass mic or use notecards for shy voices.
- [ ] **Close** — what we heard + **one** honest next step (event, resource, follow-up owner).
- [ ] **Within one week** — thank-you; deliver any promised resources.

## Coordination handoffs

- **Events** owns run-of-show and permits.
- **Social** owns dignified recap — prioritize participant dignity over “content mining.”
- **P5 / VR** owns registration offer at **exit** if culturally appropriate.

## What good looks like

Participants say **they’d bring a friend next time** — the practical test.
