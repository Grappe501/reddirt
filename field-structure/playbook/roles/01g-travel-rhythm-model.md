# Travel rhythm model — sustainable touring

**Operational goal (human and animal care):** protect **Friday and Sunday at home** at some regular cadence when feasible; avoid pretending “every week is the same” — use this as a **planning default** and negotiate reality with HQ.

## Preferred weekly pattern (illustrative)

| Day | Default posture |
|-----|----------------|
| **Friday** | **Home** — light remote, prep packs, animal care, sleep. |
| **Saturday** | **Travel or heavy local** — if away, make it count with stacked touches. |
| **Sunday** | **Home** or **short nearby** only — protect recharge and dependents. |
| **Sunday night / Monday** | **Travel after work** variant for surrogate-heavy weeks; document for volunteers. |
| **Monday–Tuesday** | **Immersion days** in market (two-day city model). |
| **Wednesday** | **Return / debrief**; half-day admin acceptable. |
| **Wed night – Fri** | **Optional second trip** only if treasury, energy, and family coverage align. |

## For volunteers scheduling Kelly (or surrogate)

- [ ] Never book **back-to-back** late nights without **food and drive safety** review.
- [ ] Put **drive blocks** on the public-facing schedule — transparency reduces no-shows.
- [ ] Build **15-minute buffers** between emotionally heavy rooms (grief, conflict, press).

## For local guides

- [ ] Provide **one-pager** per day: addresses, cell numbers, parking, bathroom access.
- [ ] Pre-drive **traffic and weather** note the night before.
- [ ] Keep **snacks and water** in vehicle; low blood sugar wrecks empathy.

## What good looks like

- Schedules volunteers can **explain to their own families**.
- **Predictable** weekly debrief slot with county coordinator.
- fewer “hero days” that extract unsustainable favors from hosts.
