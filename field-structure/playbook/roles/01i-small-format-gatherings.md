# Small-format gatherings — coffee, lunch, appetizers, clergy coffee

Extend the **Events · hosting playbook** with lighter formats that **feed** house parties and immersions.

## Coffee meetup (60–75 min)

- [ ] **Purpose:** relationship maintenance or recruiting **one** new connector.
- [ ] **Size:** 3–8; public cafe or private porch.
- [ ] **Good:** everyone leaves with **one named next step**.

## Lunch meetings (45–90 min)

- [ ] **Purpose:** stakeholders who won’t attend evenings.
- [ ] **Venue:** quiet enough to hear; **Events** confirms AV if Kelly joins virtually.
- [ ] **Good:** **short agenda** on paper; one person runs clock.

## After-dinner appetizers (90 min)

- [ ] **Purpose:** neighbors who want **low speech** social time.
- [ ] **Flow:** food first; **optional** 10-minute framing after 40 minutes of mixing.
- [ ] **Good:** **host** introduces Kelly once, not repeatedly.

## Meet with clergy (often 30–45 min office visit)

- [ ] Use **Faith community visits** playbook for protocol.
- [ ] Bring **leave-behind** only if HQ approves; default is **listening**.

## Schedule county clerk visits

- [ ] See **County Clerk visit checklist** — professional, factual, gratitude for workload.

## What good looks like

- Every format ties to **pipeline stages**: identify → invite → confirm → attend → follow up.
- Social has **one** dignified post per format when appropriate (not every private coffee).

## KPI hooks

- Track **coffee / lunch / clergy** touches in Events KPI grid (demo dashboard mirrors).
