## T-7 days — lock logistics

- [ ] Confirm **date/time/address** and publish internally.
- [ ] Assign: **setup captain**, **program lead**, **signup captain**, **breakdown captain**.
- [ ] Confirm materials: signs, handouts (if any), tablets/clipboards.

## T-3 days — fill the room

- [ ] Relational: **personal invites** to the top 20 likely attendees.
- [ ] Social: **3 posts/stories** with distinct angles (who, what, why).
- [ ] Events: **partner confirmations** (room, AV, parking).

## T-1 day — message discipline

- [ ] Send **24-hour reminder** with parking + what to bring.
- [ ] Confirm **one-minute welcome script** and **one-minute call-to-action**.
- [ ] Safety check: weather, lighting, accessibility.

## Day-of — run-of-show (example 60-minute community coffee)

- [ ] **T=0** — Setup complete; signup station live.
- [ ] **T=10** — Welcome + story (local face if possible).
- [ ] **T=20** — Brief campaign snapshot (non-adversarial, factual posture per HQ).
- [ ] **T=35** — Clear ask (one primary ask, not seven).
- [ ] **T=45** — Q&A with moderation rules (respect, brevity).
- [ ] **T=50** — Signup sprint + photo/video policy reminder.
- [ ] **T=60** — Thank-you + next event date tease.
- [ ] **T+2h** — Send follow-ups to every new signup (template from HQ).

## Within 48 hours — debrief (15 minutes)

- What recruited? What didn’t? What will we **cut** next time?
- Write **3 bullets** for county/state leadership.

## Failure recovery

Low turnout: do a **relational salvage** — 10 personal calls, schedule a smaller micro-event within 7 days.
