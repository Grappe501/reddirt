# Youth Outreach — semester rhythm and campus challenges

Links to `/volunteer/resources/youth-outreach` for long-form stubs; this is the **execution** layer for campus-facing triads.

## Semester planning (college)

- [ ] **Weeks 1–2:** org fair / tabling map; Social publishes **one** authentic “why we’re here.”
- [ ] **Weeks 3–6:** first **VR table** + one **listening** coffee; identify **student captains**.
- [ ] **Midterms window:** lighten programming; maintain **P5** check-ins.
- [ ] **Weeks 10–14:** second VR push; **GOTV** precursor training with HQ dates.

## High school guardrails

- [ ] **Parent / guardian awareness** per district policy.
- [ ] No **pressure** tactics; prioritize **library and club** pathways over hallway chaos.
- [ ] Partner with **trusted adults** (librarians, sponsors).

## Campus challenge playbooks (honest scoring)

- [ ] **Cross-campus recruitment week:** two campuses trade **peer** invites — scoreboard = **meaningful** convos logged, not spam texts.
- [ ] **Service-to-civic bridge:** one service day → one VR or `/volunteer` ask with soft opt-out.
- [ ] **County Clerk visit** (team): contacted → scheduled → completed with **professional** tone — ties Youth to Events.

## KPI targets (adjust per school)

- **Beginner:** 1 campus event / month with named student owner.
- **Intermediate:** VR table **twice** / semester + 20 honest assists tracked.
- **Advanced:** **Second campus** launch pipeline with upstream approval.

## What good looks like

Students can **run the table** without Kelly in the room — that’s multiplication.
