# Social media coordinator — lane guide

## Mission

Translate campaign priorities into **repeatable digital behavior** volunteers can emulate.

## Weekly responsibilities

1. Adapt HQ/State **kits** to local names and faces.
2. Maintain **cadence** agreed by the triad (don’t spam; don’t ghost).
3. Capture **proof** — screenshots, links, volunteer handles worth boosting.

## Content pillars (rotate)

- **Values** — why this race matters to families and communities.
- **Work** — volunteers doing the work (with consent).
- **Learn** — civic process education within guardrails set by HQ.
- **Invite** — clear “DM to volunteer” pathway.

## Guardrails

- If you’re unsure whether a claim is accurate, **don’t post** — ask HQ.
- Never punch down. **De-escalate** public threads; move disputes offline.

## Metrics you own

- Posts/reels/stories shipped vs plan (define “unit of work” locally).
- Inbound volunteer inquiries.
- Error/flag count (aim for zero).
