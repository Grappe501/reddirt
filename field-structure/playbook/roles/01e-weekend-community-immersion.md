# Weekend Community Immersion — repeatable system

**Definition:** Across one weekend, run **4–5 small ~45 minute home meet-and-greets** in distinct neighborhoods or social circles, **anchored by** a larger local moment (fair, game, festival, county party activity, big community event).

## Why this works

- Density creates **momentum** for volunteers and surrogates.
- Short home touches **lower** intimidation vs. one giant hall.
- You collect **multiple relational entry points** for precinct and P5 growth.

## Ideal architecture

1. **Anchor event** — something already on the community calendar (or campaign appearance).
2. **Neighbor gathers** — timed **before or after** the anchor so travel is sane.
3. **Clergy or coffee** — one **high-touch** touch if earned (see Faith playbook).
4. **Debrief block** Sunday evening or Monday (15 minutes live or async).

## T-14 days

- [ ] Lock **anchor** attendance or booth/staging plan.
- [ ] Recruit **4–5 hosts** with geographic spread; no accidental redundancy.
- [ ] Assign **Events + Social** split: logistics vs. promotion.

## T-7 days

- [ ] Personal invites out; **RSVP caps** per home to protect comfort.
- [ ] **Parking plan** per address; notify neighbors if density is high.
- [ ] Brief Kelly/surrogate on **names** likely in each room.

## T-1 day

- [ ] Confirm snacks, water, kid expectations per host.
- [ ] **Comms:** one bundle text to volunteers with times + addresses (privacy-aware).

## Day-of discipline

- [ ] **Start/end on time** — respect child and faith schedules.
- [ ] **One notetaker** captures asks and volunteer pledges (no sensitive voter data in open notes).
- [ ] **Photo policy** repeated at each home.

## KPI anchors

- **Home gathers completed** / planned.
- **New volunteers** identified per weekend.
- **P5 invitations** sent from each coordinator within 48h after.
