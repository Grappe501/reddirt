# House party playbook — execution manual

Use this when you are hosting or captaining a **small in-home or private-space gathering** (typically 10–20 people). Pair with **Events · hosting playbook** inside seven days.

## Types (pick one primary purpose)

| Format | Primary outcome | Typical size |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| Meet-and-greet | Introductions, story, light Q&A | 10–15 |
| Fundraiser | Compliance-approved ask; treasury rules | 8–40 |
| Listening session | Notes to leadership; minimal “speechifying” | 8–15 |
| Volunteer recruitment | Signups to `/volunteer` and lane placement | 10–20 |
| Student / campus-adjacent | Shorter program; student hosts | 8–15 |

## What good looks like

- **Guests know** date, address, parking, and expected length before they arrive.
- **Host feels supported** — not solo on signup, room setup, or follow-up.
- **One primary ask** (RSVP, volunteer shift, or compliant donation path — not three competing asks).
- **72-hour follow-up** completes for every new face.

## Phase A — host recruitment (T-21 to T-14)

- [ ] Name a **host** with suitable space, temperament, and local credibility.
- [ ] Confirm **host availability** and any house rules (shoes, pets, accessibility).
- [ ] Draft **guest target** (e.g. 12 confirmed / 18 invited) and **relational owners** (who invites whom).
- [ ] Schedule a **15-minute host brief** with Events lead: purpose, run-of-show, compliance touchpoints.

## Phase B — guest list and invites (T-14 to T-7)

- [ ] Build list: **60% relational** (named invites), **40%** strategic new faces — adjust to reality.
- [ ] Send **first invitation** (email or text) with save-the-date + RSVP path.
- [ ] Assign **P5 invites**: each coordinator invites their five with personal lines.
- [ ] Track RSVPs in a simple sheet: name, invited by, dietary/access notes, follow-up owner.

## Phase C — reminders (T-7 to T-1)

- [ ] **T-7:** gentle “hope you can make it” nudge from inviter, not mass blast.
- [ ] **T-3:** confirm catering / snacks; buy or delegate.
- [ ] **T-1:** send **24h reminder** with parking, start time, what to bring, host phone.
- [ ] Print or cue **signup QR** + spare paper consent paths if needed.

## Phase D — room setup (T-2 hours)

- [ ] **Flow:** entry → nametags → drinks / food → chairs in semicircle or small clusters.
- [ ] **Signup station** near exit with lit phone / tablet and paper backup.
- [ ] **Quiet corner** for one-on-one conversations after the program.
- [ ] **Photo policy** visible; default to **opt-in** group photo only.

## Phase E — run-of-show (example 75 minutes)

- [ ] **0–10** — Host welcome; land acknowledgement if your community uses one; house norms.
- [ ] **10–25** — Kelly or surrogate: short **why I’m here** + one local story (factual, kind).
- [ ] **25–40** — **Listening block** or Q&A with ground rules (respect, brevity, no pile-ons).
- [ ] **40–50** — **Single ask**: volunteer table shift, next event, or compliant fundraising path.
- [ ] **50–60** — Mixing; coordinators **tag** two follow-up owners Live.
- [ ] **60–75** — Thank-you; **repeat** date for next action; clear exit cue.

## Donation ask (fundraising variant)

- [ ] **Treasurer / HQ** approves language and mechanism before invites go out.
- [ ] **Host never improvises** matching claims or legal disclosures.
- [ ] Use **printed or linked** compliance footer; capture employer/occupation if required.

## Invitation scripts (short)

**Relational text:**  
“I'm hosting a small gathering for neighbors who want to hear [topic / Kelly] in a living-room setting — [date], [time]. Can I count you in? I'll send the address when you RSVP.”

**Second touch:**  
“Quick nudge — we're at [X] RSVPs and I'd love you there if you're free.”

## Follow-up workflow (within 48–72 hours)

- [ ] Thank host publicly (appropriate channel) and privately.
- [ ] **Every attendee** gets a bucket: volunteer, donor-prospect, introduce-to-precinct, or nurture.
- [ ] Log outcomes in dashboard or shared sheet — feeds Events KPIs.

## When things go wrong

- **Low RSVP:** shrink room, shift to coffee-hours format, or merge with another micro-event within 10 days.
- **Noise / neighbor tension:** keep volume down; end on time; debrief upstream.
- **Host fatigue:** rotate captains; never burn one address repeatedly without relief.
