# Relational · contact playbook (step-by-step)

## Before you talk (2 minutes)

- [ ] Know your **one ask** (volunteer, event, relational intros).
- [ ] Bring **two time-bound options** (“Tuesday phone bank intro” vs “Saturday coffee”).

## During the conversation — LISTEN framework (internal mnemonic)

- **L**isten first — confirm what they care about.
- **I**nform accurately — no unsourced process claims.
- **S**tory short — one human example, local if possible.
- **T**ransition — connect values to the ask.
- **E**ngage consent — “Would it be okay if I followed up Thursday?”
- **N**ext step literal — calendared, not vague.

## After the conversation (3 minutes)

- [ ] Log **date + outcome bucket** (supportive / curious / opposed / follow-up).
- [ ] Schedule the follow-up **before** you chat with the next person.
- [ ] If sensitive, route to campaign coordinator **privately**.

## Weekly Power of 5 sprint (60 minutes team time, can be split)

- [ ] Each person completes **5 intentional conversations**.
- [ ] Team produces **5 introduced connectors** for the next week (warm intros, not cold spam).

## When someone is hostile or unsafe

- Thank, leave, **do not engage**. Report patterns to HQ if needed.
