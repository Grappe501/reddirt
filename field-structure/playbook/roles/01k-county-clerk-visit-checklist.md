# County Clerk visit checklist (county seat)

**Purpose:** Professional relationship with the **county clerk / elections office** — accurate info, gratitude for workload, **no** pressure that could embarrass Kelly or clerks.

## Pipeline (same as Youth tab mirror)

- [ ] **Identified** — county seat and correct office for the question (registration vs. business filings).
- [ ] **Contacted** — email or call with specific, humble request.
- [ ] **Requested** — meeting or drop-in window; materials sent ahead if helpful.
- [ ] **Scheduled** — on calendar with travel buffer.
- [ ] **Completed** — on time, short, note-taking for HQ if needed.
- [ ] **Follow-up** — thank-you; any promised info sent **when** you said you would.

## Before you go

- [ ] **Read current public guidance** from SOS / county site — do not wing statutory questions.
- [ ] Bring **one-page leave-behind** only if HQ approved.
- [ ] If student volunteers join, confirm **dress and tone** with Youth lead.

## During

- [ ] Introduce **who you are** and **why you care about accurate elections** — avoid partisan venting.
- [ ] Ask **process** questions, not **gotchas**.
- [ ] Respect **lines** and **busy counters** — wait your turn.

## What good looks like

Clerks remember you as **curious and courteous**, not extractive.
